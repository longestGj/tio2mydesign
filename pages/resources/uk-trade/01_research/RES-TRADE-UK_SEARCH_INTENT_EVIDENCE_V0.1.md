## 2. Traceable search and page observations

The exact query returned a mixed set: an EU legal result from the wrong jurisdiction, commercial commentary, parliamentary discussion and only indirect discovery leads. Expanded UK-official searches located the current TRA case, initiation announcement, registration notice, tariff notice and HMRC operational guidance.

The official-source pattern is consistent: the user is likely trying to establish current procedural status, affected goods/origin, the practical meaning of registration, and which transaction inputs must be checked. It is not sufficient to answer only with a generic supplier proposition or an undated anti-dumping explainer.

## 3. Intent interpretations, alternatives and counterexamples

### Selected interpretation

Current-status informational intent with an import/compliance task: explain that AD0086 is an active UK dumping investigation; distinguish investigation, registration and any possible future remedy; identify the written goods/origin scope; and direct the reader to official transaction verification.

### Alternative intents retained

- `titanium dioxide import registration uk`: answer why specified imports are registered and what additional codes apply.
- `titanium dioxide import duty uk`: separate an anti-dumping procedure from ordinary tariff/VAT checks and avoid inventing a rate.
- supplier/procurement continuation: route to `MARKET-UK-001` rather than duplicate its commercial role.

### Counterexamples rejected

- Treating an EU decision as UK law because it ranks for the exact phrase.
- Calling an allegation or active investigation a final finding.
- Publishing a provisional or definitive TiO2 duty rate not present in the captured UK official sources.
- Offering transshipment, origin manipulation or other avoidance guidance.
- Treating the approved phrase `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide` as an automatic legal result for every UK entry.
