## Approved direction

The article will explain how extrusion and pigment distribution, powder preparation/application, controlled film build, cure and separate finished-film endpoints shape a fair TiO2 comparison in a declared powder-coating system.

The approval does not authorize a broad powder-coating formulation guide, named TiO2 Malaysia Grade recommendation, universal recipe or process settings, direct equivalence, guaranteed outcomes, or transfer from ordinary decorative powder coatings to functional pipe/rebar systems.
