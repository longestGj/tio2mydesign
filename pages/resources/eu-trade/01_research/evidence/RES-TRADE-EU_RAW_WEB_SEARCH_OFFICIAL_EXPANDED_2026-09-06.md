## Discovery notes

- The definitive measure and its precise product, origin, company-group and fixed-duty scope are controlled by Regulation (EU) 2025/4.
- The 2026 notice is a procedural reopening. It is not evidence that a replacement rate is already in force.
- The importing and VAT pages support separation of anti-dumping duty from ordinary customs tariff and import VAT. They do not establish a transaction-specific tariff or VAT rate.
- TARIC / Access2Markets must be checked at transaction time for classification, origin and destination-specific customs treatment. No transaction-specific result was asserted in Gate 1.
