### Common uses of Titanium Dioxide:

  * Pigment: Commonly used in paints, coatings, plastics and inks for its whiteness and UV resistance.
  * Food Additive (E171): Used as a whitening agent in candies, chewing gum and dairy products (though banned in some regions).
  * Ceramics & Glass: Enhances brightness and strength.
  * Photocatalysis: Used in air and water purification due to its ability to break down pollutants.
