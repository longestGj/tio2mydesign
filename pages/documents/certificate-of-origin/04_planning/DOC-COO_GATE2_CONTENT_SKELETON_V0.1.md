## Page task and path

Help a procurement or trade-documentation buyer answer four questions in sequence:

1. What does origin evidence establish?
2. Is the need preferential, non-preferential or another permitted proof/declaration?
3. Which destination, rules and transaction details affect the evidence?
4. What information should the buyer take into the Request Documents form?

## Candidate page sequence

### 1. Hero and direct answer

**Eyebrow:** `Origin Documentation Guide`

**H1:** `Certificate of Origin for Titanium Dioxide: What Buyers Should Verify`

**Candidate copy:**

> A Certificate of Origin certifies the origin of the goods identified in the document. Depending on the applicable procedure, the relevant evidence may be a preferential certificate or proof, a non-preferential Certificate of Origin, or another permitted proof or declaration. Destination, rules of origin and consistent transaction documents still determine how that evidence is used.

**Primary CTA:** `Request Origin Documentation` → `/request-documents/`, with the shared `Origin & Supplier Qualification Documentation` context visibly preselected and editable.

Purpose: answer the search question before asking the buyer to act, without implying document availability or a customs result.

### 2. Which Origin Evidence Is Relevant?

Use a compact three-row comparison:

- `Preferential proof or certificate` — relevant when an importer intends to claim treatment under an applicable trade agreement.
- `Non-preferential Certificate of Origin` — certifies origin under non-preferential rules; it is not a substitute for the proof required by a trade agreement.
- `Other permitted proof or declaration` — covers a declaration, electronic form or other evidence when the applicable procedure permits it.

Purpose: distinguish the three needs before explaining traceability.

### 3. What Determines the Right Evidence?

Ask the buyer to identify destination, intended preferential claim or scheme if known, the exact goods/Grade and the available order or shipment references. Explain that the evidence and transaction documents need consistent identifying details.

Purpose: turn general origin guidance into a practical transaction check without giving destination-specific legal or tariff advice.

### 4. Origin Evidence and Traceability Answer Different Questions

Explain that origin evidence addresses origin under the applicable rules for identified goods and transaction context, while traceability connects product, batch, order or shipment records. Neither concept silently proves the other.

Purpose: answer the page-specific traceability query without claiming an IKHLAS traceability capability.

### 5. Prepare Your Request

**Candidate checklist:** Product Grade; destination; evidence type or scheme if known; order/shipment reference and requested form if available.

**Incomplete-context helper:** A buyer may proceed with the information available and state what the importer, broker or internal review requires. The receiver accepts one Product Grade per submission and more than one document type.

**Primary CTA:** `Request Origin Documentation` → `/request-documents/`.

**Secondary links:** `Explore Titanium Dioxide Products` → `/products/`; `View Document Hub` → `/documents/`.

Purpose: connect the informational decision to the existing form contract and state what happens next.

### 6. Official Source

Link the Royal Malaysian Customs Department `FAQ Rules of Origin`, show its information date and this page's review date, and state that the guidance is general rather than evidence for a particular order or shipment.

Purpose: make the regulatory basis and scope visible without adding a detached reference library.

## Decisions and open boundaries

- The six-module sequence is a review candidate, not a user-approved Skeleton.
- The current site-wide authority allows `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide`, but this page does not need that statement to perform its task and does not extend it to certificate availability, issuance or customs treatment.
- `A Certificate of Origin is available upon request` is not approved for DOC-COO and does not appear in Buyer Copy.
- URL, canonical implementation, indexing and publication remain separate decisions.
