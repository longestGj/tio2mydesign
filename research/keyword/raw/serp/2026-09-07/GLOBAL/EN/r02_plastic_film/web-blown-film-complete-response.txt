# FP-550 Opacity Pigment™  
## For Polyolefin Masterbatches

FP-550 Opacity Pigment™ from FP-Pigments is a highly efficient white pigment for the polyolefin masterbatch industry. The unique engineered structure improves the efficiency of existing titanium dioxide pigments and enhances their performance allowing the formulator to optimise their product performance and reduce raw material costs.

## Easy to Use, Quick to Save

FP-550 Opacity Pigment™ has been developed by FP-Pigments for use in polyolefins as a partial replacement for TiO₂ pigment. The Opacity Pigment™ can easily be incorporated in polyolefin masterbatches using standard compounding operations and let down in end use processing equipment. The FP-550 Opacity Pigment™ finds use in a wide range of end applications including extrusion, blown film and injection moulding.

## FP-550 - Blown Film Colour and Opacity

In 50 micron thick blown PE films, up to 15% of the TiO₂ pigment can be replaced by FP-550 while retaining opacity and film quality. Furthermore, FP-based blown films show high brightness and low yellowness compared to the TiO₂ based reference.

# White Masterbatch

White masterbatch provides opacity to plastic films and parts, allows for a background canvas for printed graphics in packaging applications and enables improved performance in a wide variety of applications.

## Overview

White masterbatch typically contains the pigment titanium dioxide (TiO_{2}), often in combination with another mineral. TiO_{2} provides the highest level of whiteness and opacity to plastic parts. Various grades of TiO_{2} provide different processing and end-use performance benefits. Ingenia carefully selects the grade of TiO_{2 }and the carrier resin to meet the performance targets for dispersion, melt index, undertone, durability and opacity that the application demands.

## Features

Ingenia offers white masterbatch options to cover a wide range of applications including blown film, cast film, injection molding, blow molding, sheet extrusion, and extrusion coating. To best serve our customers, we have designed a wide range of products, some with superior weatherability for outdoor uses, others with high flow for injection molding and even filled whites for low cost applications. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
