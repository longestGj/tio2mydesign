## Approved reader and article purpose

Write for coating formulators, technical procurement teams and application laboratories that already use pigmentary titanium dioxide and are comparing a replacement supplier or grade for a defined coating system.

The article should answer:

> How should a coatings buyer compare a replacement TiO2 grade across optical performance, dispersion and rheology, film appearance, durability and cost-in-use without treating similar TDS values as proof of equivalence?

The working title may be refined, but it must naturally include `titanium dioxide for coatings` and promise a buyer-useful evaluation rather than an internal qualification procedure.

## D23 findings to resolve

### 1. Convert the controlled package into Buyer Clean article prose

- Remove all `<!-- STMT:... -->` anchors from the reader article.
- Remove `S1–S9` production labels from headings.
- Do not expose Workflow, gate, release, publication, review, evidence-status or internal responsibility language.
- Retain only those tables, checklists and advance/hold distinctions that materially help the coatings buyer make a decision.

### 2. Lead with a direct TiO2-for-coatings answer

The opening should quickly state that candidate selection must be tied to the buyer's binder/vehicle, pigment-volume context, dispersion route, application and cure, film/substrate, exposure and accepted endpoints. It should also name the central comparison dimensions: opacity/optical efficiency, colour and undertone where relevant, dispersion/rheology, film appearance, durability and cost-in-use.

### 3. Increase TiO2 and coatings specificity

- Explain why TiO2's optical role, particle distribution/dispersion and surface treatment make a system-specific comparison necessary.
- Distinguish interior/exterior and waterborne/solventborne/powder or other coating contexts only to the depth supported by evidence and needed for selection.
- Do not let standards administration or generic document control become the main story.
- Preserve method comparability, accelerated-weathering limits and application qualification without turning the article into an SOP.

Any materially new technical statement requires traceable, appropriately scoped evidence. Evidence acquisition must remain limited to what the targeted revision genuinely needs.

### 4. Remove competitor dependence from the public article

The current reader source block links four of six entries directly to Chemours pages. Competitor/manufacturer material may help D22 understand industry positioning, but it must not be the public article's principal authority or prove TiO2 Malaysia capability.

For the revised reader article:

- replace competitor-dependent general mechanisms with suitable independent, standards, scholarly or other authoritative sources where available;
- omit unsupported mechanisms when an appropriate source is not available;
- do not cite competitor product pages as evidence of TiO2 Malaysia performance, suitability, supply or service;
- do not copy competitor wording, structure or product-selection claims.

### 5. End with a natural buyer action

Ask the reader to prepare the current grade and reason for change, binder/vehicle and formulation context, pigment-volume or loading basis, dispersion route, application/cure, substrate and film, exposure, target properties, methods and current problem. The next action should be to request relevant technical documents and discuss a controlled sample comparison.

This is article guidance only. D23 will decide the final page CTA label, destination, form and any approved product presentation later.

## Boundaries to preserve

- Do not invent formulation recipes, dosage, pass thresholds, test values, durability years, savings, yield, productivity, compatibility, equivalence or guarantees.
- Do not convert an accelerated test into service life.
- Do not infer a candidate's performance from treatment names or TiO2 percentage alone.
- Do not name or recommend a TiO2 Malaysia grade unless separately justified by the current PRODUCT relationship authority and approved by D23 for this article. Named grades are not required for this revision.
- APP-COAT owns the generic coatings use-case intent; do not take exact grade, country-supplier, process-category or deeper standalone technical-resource keywords as primary targets.
