## 4. Source-precision decisions for Gate 2

Allowed dated statements:

- “As checked on 7 September 2026, TRA lists AD0086 as active.”
- “HMRC has been directed to register the specified imports from 27 March 2026.”
- “The checked official sources did not publish an AD0086 provisional or definitive anti-dumping amount.”
- “Any later liability described by the registration notice is conditional on a definitive remedy being implemented.”

Prohibited transformations:

- registration → duty already charged;
- investigation → dumping or injury finding;
- possible retrospective liability → certain retrospective collection;
- Malaysia origin → automatic non-application, exemption or zero rate;
- a commodity code or additional code → a classification, origin or customs outcome without the full transaction facts;
- GB guidance → an automatic Northern Ireland result.
