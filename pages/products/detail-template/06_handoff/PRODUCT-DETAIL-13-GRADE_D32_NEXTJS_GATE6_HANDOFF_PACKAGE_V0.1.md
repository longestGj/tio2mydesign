## 5. What Gate 6 requires D32 and Gate 9 to confirm

### 5.1 Page identity and route

For every row in §4, Page ID, Grade code, slug, public path and English locale must agree. Unknown, misspelled or non-approved Grade routes must not present another Grade's content. No Grade may fall back to M-350 or to another site's data.

The canonical for each D32 page is the §4 route on the current D32 host, for example `https://tio2products.com/products/m-510/`. Historical `https://tio2malaysia.com/...` canonical values in the old content payload are superseded only at the host level by the approved D32 site-domain baseline; the path and Grade identity do not change.

### 5.2 Visible content and module state

Each page must reproduce its own approved content contract: breadcrumb, Hero, positioning, applications, evaluation guidance, technical content, market support and any approved actions. Module order and conditional visibility follow the Grade contract and the approved Product Detail family. Empty placeholders, copied M-350 copy, internal review labels and invented filler are failures.

`originSupport` and `relatedGrades` remain `not_public` for all thirteen contracts. Their absence is intentional and must not be filled by inference.

### 5.3 Technical data and fact boundaries

Technical properties, standards/test methods, values, units, symbols, footnotes and notes must remain attached to the correct Grade and row. Visible and machine-readable values must express the same meaning. A value, method or threshold must not be promoted into a certification, guarantee or product specification.

The Grade-specific restrictions in §4 are mandatory negative checks. Suppressed claims must be absent from visible copy, metadata and structured data.

### 5.4 SEO, GEO and Schema

Each page must have its own approved title, description, H1, primary keyword ownership, canonical path, breadcrumb and Product meaning from its content contract. Product/Breadcrumb structured data, if emitted, must not be stronger than visible content and must use the same Grade, description, canonical and technical values.

All thirteen pages remain preview-only: `noindex, nofollow`, excluded from sitemap and not represented as publicly released. M-510's older contract does not contain a robots field; this shared preview control still applies.

### 5.5 Actions and external dependencies

The approved contracts define Request Documents, Sample and RFQ semantics, labels and Grade context. D32 must not invent a receiver, fake a successful request, redirect to Contact as an unapproved fallback or silently drop the Grade context.

If a destination is not yet available in the accepted D32 static-site combination, the page may follow the approved M-350 D32 conditional omission behavior. In that state the page can be assessed for its own surface, but integration remains `NOT_READY` and public release remains blocked until the destination and its independent acceptance exist.

### 5.6 Shared experience and responsive result

The pages consume the current D32 shared Header, Footer, navigation, mobile menu and Cookie Settings behavior; they do not create Grade-specific copies of shared chrome. The approved M-350 D32 visual family is the reference for hierarchy and responsive behavior, while each Grade's real content must be tested for its own length and table shape.

At minimum, Gate 9 must observe the completed page at 1440, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 CSS pixels, plus keyboard focus/order and a 200%-zoom-equivalent narrow layout. Text, cards, tables and controls must not clip, overlap or create page-level horizontal overflow. Physical-device and manual assistive-technology checks are reported separately as tested, not tested or explicitly excepted; an M-350 exception does not automatically waive another Grade.

### 5.7 Product Hub and existing-page regression

The Products Hub may expose a Grade link only when that Grade's D32 route is present in the tested candidate. The visible 14-Grade inventory, family/grouping meaning and selector must remain consistent with the approved Product Hub. Home, Products Hub, M-350 and shared chrome must retain their accepted content and behavior after the thirteen pages are added.
