## 5. Expanded-query observations

The expansions were not used to infer the original query's result proportions. They were used only to test specific questions raised by the approved article.

### 5.1 Technical-explanation expansion

Query: `white printing ink titanium dioxide substrate deposited film opacity evaluation`

Observed result types included the ISO 23498 scope, the approved article's MDPI study on opaque-white flexographic print quality, an IS&T opacity paper, patents and unrelated TiO2 deposition research. The result set supports a real technical question about the specimen, substrate, deposited layer and measurement basis. It also shows a counter-intent risk: generic search can drift into inkjet hardware, thin films, electronics, heritage print or hobby printing rather than pigment-supplier evaluation.

### 5.2 Supplier/action expansion

Query: `titanium dioxide printing ink opacity dispersion supplier TDS sample`

Observed result types included supplier Application pages, manufacturer TDS/Grade pages, Request a Sample paths and technical opacity pages. This supports checking whether the future APP-INK page gives a reader a bounded route from technical explanation to neutral Grade review, documents, sample discussion and RFQ. It does not prove that all four actions should have equal visual priority.

## 6. Intent interpretation and counter-explanations

### 6.1 Supported interpretation

The evidence supports a mixed intent:

- **Informational / technical evaluation:** understand why ink chemistry, printing method, substrate, deposited film, dispersion, rheology and measurement method change the meaning of a result.
- **Commercial investigation:** identify candidate Grades, inspect technical documents and move toward a sample or inquiry.
- **Method/safety support:** understand measurement scope or ingredient/regulatory context.

The combination is consistent with a category-level Application landing page that first enables a technically bounded comparison and then exposes project-controlled next actions.

### 6.2 Other reasonable explanations

- Some searchers may be printing operators diagnosing white-ink transfer, pinholes, settling or hardware issues rather than sourcing pigment.
- Some may be looking for finished printing ink, not raw TiO2 pigment.
- Some results target inkjet, electronics, art or consumer/hobby use, which are outside APP-INK's B2B pigment scope.
- Supplier pages often publish recommendations, recipes and thresholds. Their presence does not make those claims safe or necessary for this project.

Because numeric demand, market split and query-persona data are unavailable, the method cannot claim that procurement or any one printing process is the majority intent.
