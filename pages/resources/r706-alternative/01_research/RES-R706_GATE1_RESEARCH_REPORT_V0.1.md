## 5. R-706 first-party findings

### 5.1 Current R-706 product page

Chemours/Ti-Pure describes R-706 as a rutile titanium dioxide grade made by its proprietary chloride process and positioned as a coating pigment for interior and exterior use. Listed applications include gloss and semi-gloss architectural paint, wood coatings, automotive/refinish OEM topcoats and coil coatings. Feature language covers particle-size control, undertone, wet-in/dispersibility, gloss/hiding balance and durability.

These are R-706 facts only. They do not establish another supplier's performance.

### 5.2 Linked February 2020 Product Information PDF

The current R-706 HTML page links to the February 2020 Product Information document. It includes a property table such as TiO₂ content, alumina/silica, specific gravity, bulking value, organic treatment, colour, particle size, oil absorption, pH and resistivity. The document labels values as typical unless otherwise specified and identifies the TiO₂ content entry as a minimum. It also makes end-use evaluation before specification essential.

For a later Gate this means:

- retain the document date;
- distinguish a minimum specification from typical reference values;
- do not copy the table or marketing prose wholesale;
- use the dimensions to form a buyer verification brief, not to infer a passing IKHLAS candidate.

### 5.3 Chemours TS-6706 announcement

Chemours' 2025 release calls TS-6706 its TMP/TME-free version and a drop-in for its R-706, and cites its own testing in water- and solvent-based architectural and industrial paint systems. The named dimensions include viscosity stability, colour, gloss, tint strength, hiding, mechanical strength and bulk flow.

This is useful because it demonstrates how narrow a responsible replacement claim is: named products, owner attribution and a defined test programme. It cannot be transferred to an unrelated supplier or used to label an IKHLAS grade `drop-in`.

## 9. R-706-specific qualification inputs for later Gate 2

These are research inputs, not final copy or module order:

1. exact current R-706 product document/version and which entries are specified versus typical;
2. coating type and formulation system, including waterborne/solvent-borne context where relevant;
3. substrate, finish/gloss target and use environment;
4. optical/appearance criteria such as colour/undertone, tint strength, hiding and gloss, using buyer-defined test methods;
5. wet-in, dispersion, viscosity/rheology and processing/handling criteria used in the buyer's system;
6. durability and mechanical requirements relevant to the actual coating;
7. acceptance criteria, test conditions and control formulation;
8. required technical, safety, quality and origin documents;
9. trial plan before specification or supplier change.

The list is informed by R-706/TS-6706 sources and Agent synthesis. It does not assert that all dimensions apply to every buyer or that any IKHLAS grade passes them.
