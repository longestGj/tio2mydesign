## 6. Content, fact and action invariants

### 6.1 Trade measure meaning

- Preserve the dated statement: **as checked on 7 September 2026**, MDIC listed the definitive anti-dumping measure for the specified China-origin rutile TiO2 product through **24 October 2030**, subject to later official decisions.
- Historical investigation opening (30 April 2024) and provisional measure (21 October 2024) remain historical. Resolution 802/2025, published 24 October 2025, is the definitive-measure event.
- Preserve the written rutile scope and at least 80% TiO2 by dry weight condition. NCM `3206.11.10` is indicative and cannot decide shipment coverage alone.
- Keep anatase and Ex-001 separate. Ex-001 requires every approved cumulative condition: 82–94% TiO2; surface treatment; P2O5 ≥1.2%; SiO2 ≤0.4%; ZrO2 ≤0.1%; pH 6.5–8.1; intended specifically for base papers used in melamine decorative laminates. Do not generalize it to paper, surface-treated rutile, the NCM or an ordinary-tariff concession.
- Preserve all four China-origin producer/exporter conditions and amounts: Anhui Gold Star at USD 1,148.72/t; the five exact LB/Henan entities at USD 1,159.18/t; an exact legal entity remaining in Resolution 802's named list after Resolution 850 at USD 1,223.92/t; `Demais empresas` at USD 1,267.74/t. A brand, group, supplier, affiliate, relationship or amount alone does not assign a band.
- Resolution 850 removes Siegwerk from the named list; removal does not assign another band, an exemption or a shipment outcome. The older MDIC detail-page list must not be treated as exhaustive current assignment without the later resolution.
- The public-interest evaluation opened 27 March 2026 and the checked page lists 4 September 2026 as the final-submissions deadline. The deadline does not suspend, reduce, maintain or terminate the measure. “No later final decision located” remains limited to the named official entry points and review date.
- Anti-dumping, ordinary import duty and import taxes remain separate. Publish no ordinary-duty percentage, Ex-tarifário entitlement, transaction tax result or landed-cost calculation.
- `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide` is approved commercial origin wording. It does not determine Brazilian customs origin, classification, preference, exemption, anti-dumping treatment, tax or a zero-duty result.
