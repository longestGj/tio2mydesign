## 2. Search task and selected audience

The selected service audience is EU procurement, import, compliance and technical-sourcing teams. This is an editorial service decision supported by the query/source mix; it is not a demographic measurement of searchers.

## 4. Current measure fact map

### 4.1 Product and origin scope

The regulation covers TiO₂ in the specified forms and preparations containing at least 80% TiO₂ by weight on a dry-matter basis, all particle sizes, originating in the People's Republic of China. It lists CN ex 2823 00 00 and ex 3206 11 00 and four TARIC subdivisions.

The page may report that written scope. It must not promise a classification or origin result for an individual shipment.

### 4.2 Current fixed duties

| Category in Regulation (EU) 2025/4 | Fixed anti-dumping duty | Additional-code context |
|---|---:|---|
| LB Group | EUR 0.74/kg | 89CB |
| Anhui Gold Star Group | EUR 0.25/kg | 89CC |
| Other cooperating companies in the Annex | EUR 0.64/kg | Company-specific Annex code |
| All other companies | EUR 0.74/kg | 8999 |

The individual group/company rate depends on the specified valid commercial invoice. Without it, the all-other-company duty applies. This condition must stay attached to the table so the numbers are not misleading.

### 4.3 Conditional end-use exemption

Goods imported for use in production of white graphic inks for printing are exempt only under the Union Customs Code end-use procedure referenced by the regulation. Gate 2 must not shorten this to a general graphic-ink exemption.

## 5. 2026 absorption reinvestigation

The 25 August 2026 notice:

- reopens an absorption reinvestigation under Article 12;
- covers the same investigated product originating in the PRC;
- uses an investigation period of 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026;
- says a future affirmative outcome may recalculate the duty, with the resulting level not exceeding twice the amount imposed by 2025/4;
- states a normal six-month conclusion period and a maximum nine months from publication.

These are procedural facts. No future rate, affirmative finding or completion date is asserted.

## 7. Malaysia-origin boundary

`Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide` remains approved for outward use. The official EU measure reviewed here states PRC origin. These are two different propositions. Gate 1 therefore permits both only with semantic separation:

- marketing/product origin may be stated under its approved authority;
- the trade page may state the legal measure's PRC-origin scope;
- neither statement alone produces a shipment-specific customs outcome.

This is not a newly invented company-capability restriction; it prevents an unsupported legal inference while preserving the approved marketing fact.
