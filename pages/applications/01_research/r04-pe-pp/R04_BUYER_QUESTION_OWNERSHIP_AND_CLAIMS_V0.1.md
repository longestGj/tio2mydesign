# R04 Buyer Question, Ownership and Claim Boundary V0.1

## Buyer and decision

Primary reader: plastics compounders, converters, application laboratories, quality teams and technical procurement teams evaluating one pigmentary TiO2 candidate across a portfolio that includes PE and PP finished products.

Core decision: which observations can be compared across PE and PP, and which formulation, conversion, specimen, conditioning, exposure, method and endpoint evidence must be rebuilt for each declared system before attributing a difference to TiO2.

After reading, the team should be able to split one vague `PE/PP suitable` claim into separate, testable finished-system questions and route film or masterbatch work to the correct owner.

## Recommended ownership

- APP-PLAS owns the combined finished-plastic cross-resin decision.
- APPART-PLAS-004 and APPART-PLAS-005 merge into one R04 article; neither becomes a separate article.
- R02 owns film-specific thickness, opacity, dispersion and processing depth.
- APP-MB/R07 owns polyolefin masterbatch carrier, concentrate formulation, loading and production qualification.
- Grade pages own approved Grade facts, not cross-resin suitability.
- Resources own generic explanations of dispersion tests, weathering or TDS interpretation when no PE/PP decision is present.

## Required content boundary

1. Explain that `PE` and `PP` identify material families, not matched test systems.
2. Separate resin/grade and additive context from conversion route and finished specimen.
3. Explain why PE-specific dispersion evidence or a PP processing study does not transfer automatically.
4. Distinguish colour/opacity, dispersion screens, process response, mechanical properties and weathering/durability endpoints.
5. Show how film, moulded part, fibre or other geometry changes what is measured without attempting to cover every end use.
6. Define a matched-within-system comparison and a declared-difference cross-system comparison.
7. End with a concise document and test-context request.

## Prohibited claims

- PE and PP treated as interchangeable or represented by one universal test system;
- named TiO2 Malaysia Grade recommendation, ranking or PE/PP suitability;
- universal dosage, let-down ratio, carrier compatibility, resin choice, stabiliser package, screw design, temperature, residence time, throughput or acceptance threshold;
- guaranteed opacity, colour, dispersion, processing, mechanical property, UV protection, weathering life, recyclability, food contact, compliance, equivalence or savings;
- nanoparticle or photocatalytic study generalized to pigmentary TiO2;
- film evidence generalized to moulded parts, fibres or other geometries;
- masterbatch TDS or carrier label used as finished-product proof;
- historical accelerated exposure converted into outdoor years.

## Reader-quality constraint

The article must explain evidence transfer and causality, not present an internal qualification SOP. A comparison matrix or request list may summarize the explanation but cannot become the article's dominant structure.
