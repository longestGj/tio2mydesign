## 6. Evidence-backed Fact Set

The detailed inventory is in `RES-TRADE-IN_GATE1_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_V0.1.csv`.

- DGTR's current 3 August 2026 remand finding records that the 2025 final finding and levy notification were quashed.
- DGTR §210 says a new duty can only be imposed when notified by the Ministry of Finance.
- DGTR §§231-233 recommend duties for five years from a future notification; they do not themselves create the notification.
- The recommendation covers defined Titanium Dioxide originating in or exported from China PR, with indicative tariff items 28230010, 32061110 and 32061190 and specified exclusions.
- Recommended rates are USD 460-681/MT depending on origin/export/producer path and invoice conditions; the two USD 681 rows must remain distinct.
- No later implementing TiO2 Customs (ADD) notification was located in the official sources checked through 6 September 2026.
- The CBIC GST schedule shows 18% IGST for the relevant Chapter 28 general category and heading 3206; current BCD was not obtained and is not guessed.
