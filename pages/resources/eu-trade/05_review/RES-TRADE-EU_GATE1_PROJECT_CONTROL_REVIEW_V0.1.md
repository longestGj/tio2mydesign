## 2. Independent checks performed

Project control independently checked the registered Page ID, URL, primary keyword and page ownership against the Page Registry and keyword master. It read the Complete Brief, Gate 1 Research Report, Claim / Evidence Register, Current Manifest and completed Search Intent Evidence report.

The two preserved official EUR-Lex PDFs were independently inspected. The review confirmed:

- Regulation (EU) 2025/4 is identified by EUR-Lex as in force and entered into force on 2025-01-10;
- the covered written scope is the specified TiO2 product originating in the People's Republic of China;
- the fixed duties are EUR 0.74/kg for LB Group, EUR 0.25/kg for Anhui Gold Star Group, EUR 0.64/kg for other cooperating Annex companies and EUR 0.74/kg for all other PRC imports;
- individual rates retain the valid-commercial-invoice condition;
- the white-graphic-ink exemption is conditional on the Union end-use procedure, including Article 254;
- Notice C/2026/4533 reopens an absorption reinvestigation, identifies Regulation 2025/4 as the measure currently in force, and does not itself replace the current duty table;
- the notice states the 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30 investigation period, normal six-month / maximum nine-month timetable and possible future ceiling of twice the existing duty.

The project-control review also confirmed that the page does not take over EU supplier positioning from `MARKET-EU-001`, does not offer duty-avoidance guidance, does not invent ordinary tariff or VAT rates, and does not turn the approved `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide` statement into an automatic customs or anti-dumping result.

## 3. Reader and content judgment

The proposed direction is suitable for the registered informational query. It answers what an EU procurement or compliance reader needs first: what is in force now, what may change, and what should be checked before a transaction. The duty table must remain beside its company and invoice context, so isolated numbers cannot be mistaken for a universal rate.

The CTA hierarchy is also appropriate:

1. `Review EU Procurement Options` → `/markets/european-union/`
2. `Check the Official EU Measure` → the official EUR-Lex regulation

The shared fixed RFQ remains available through Global Chrome but is not made the primary action of this regulatory update page.
