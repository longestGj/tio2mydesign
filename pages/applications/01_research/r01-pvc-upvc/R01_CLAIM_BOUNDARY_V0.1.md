# R01 Claim Boundary V0.1

## D22 must not write

- “best TiO2 for PVC,” “recommended PVC Grade,” or any ranking of TiO2 Malaysia Grades;
- universal dosage, phr, replacement percentage, processing temperature or stabilizer recipe;
- direct equivalents to Chemours, Kronos, Tronox, Venator or another supplier;
- guaranteed UV resistance, lifespan, colour retention, gloss retention, impact retention, warpage prevention or cost saving;
- certification, potable-water acceptance, pipe-standard acceptance or regulatory compliance without exact current Grade evidence;
- accelerated-weathering-hour to service-year conversion;
- claims that chloride-process or sulfate-process origin alone determines PVC suitability;
- claims copied from supplier pages as if they were independent proof;
- nanoparticle or photocatalytic TiO2 research presented as evidence for the site's pigmentary Grades.

## D22 may write when properly sourced and bounded

- a system-definition and qualification framework;
- differences among rigid/uPVC, flexible PVC, profiles, pipes, sheets and exterior products;
- questions about surface treatment, stabilizer context, colour, chalking, dispersion, extrusion and weathering endpoints;
- method-bounded summaries of association guidance and published studies;
- a buyer input checklist for requesting current documents or a controlled sample discussion;
- neutral reference to the parent APP-PLAS Grades to Review, without PVC suitability inference.
