## 4. Representative page reading

### 4.1 SpecialChem technical selection guide

The accessible page is structured around why TiO2 is used, light scattering, pigment-volume context, dispersion optimization and selection factors. It also includes links to product discovery, technical datasheets and sample requests.

**Intent interpretation:** a reader may need both technical explanation and a path to candidate materials. SpecialChem's inventory, recommendations and numbers are not evidence of TiO2 Malaysia performance or availability.

### 4.2 Chemours Coatings application/product page

The page explains TiO2 in Coatings, then divides applications into architectural, industrial, automotive, electrodeposition, powder and other uses. It presents proprietary Grade recommendations and provides SDS/contact/sample actions.

**Intent interpretation:** commercial results commonly connect the buyer's coating system and required endpoints to candidate Grade navigation and a supplier action. Chemours facts apply only to Chemours; they cannot be transferred to IKHLAS Grades.

### 4.3 INEOS pigment portfolio

The page presents a dedicated Coatings product family, names application contexts and lists product-specific descriptions before giving a sample contact.

**Intent interpretation:** a generic Coatings query can lead to a portfolio-evaluation task, but portfolio claims are manufacturer-specific. This page is useful only for the observed information/action pattern.

### 4.4 NIST coating studies

The NIST pages describe research linking coating microstructure, appearance, TiO2 dispersion and accelerated-UV conditions. One opened study explicitly reports different degradation patterns and rates across dispersion and wet/dry exposure conditions.

**Intent interpretation:** technical readers need method-, specimen- and exposure-bounded interpretation. These studies support the existing approved article's system-specific evaluation model; they do not choose an IKHLAS Grade.

### 4.5 ASTM D3451-24

The current ASTM product page confirms a dedicated guide for testing coating powders and powder coatings. Full method text is not freely exposed in this observation.

**Intent interpretation:** powder Coatings require their own applicable test-method context; the listing cannot be used to reproduce procedures or invent pass values.

## 5. Supported interpretations and counterevidence

### Supported interpretations

1. The query is not satisfied by a definition alone. Representative pages repeatedly connect the coating system, optical result, dispersion, appearance, durability and cost/performance to Grade selection or supplier action.
2. Buyers need to define binder/vehicle, formulation basis, dispersion route, application/cure, film/substrate, exposure and acceptance endpoints before a comparison is interpretable.
3. A useful APP-COAT page should connect the approved technical article to a neutral TiO2 Malaysia Grade review and controlled Documents/Sample/RFQ actions instead of ending as an isolated article.
4. Architectural, industrial, automotive, powder and other Coatings contexts appear in external pages, but their appearance does not authorize new child URLs or a copied taxonomy.

### Other explanations and counterevidence

1. Scientific results cover nano-TiO2, antimicrobial, photocatalytic and optical-coating topics. These are genuine alternative interpretations of the words but fall outside the registered pigmentary Coatings page intent.
2. Supplier and distributor pages frequently make direct recommendations and superlative performance claims. Their existence shows commercial intent; it does not authorize APP-COAT to rank or recommend IKHLAS Grades.
3. Search results include low-authority pages with recipes, universal loading ranges or durability generalizations. They were not admitted as technical evidence.
4. No current controlled local-Google or Semrush session was run. Local rank, demand size and competitiveness remain unknown.
