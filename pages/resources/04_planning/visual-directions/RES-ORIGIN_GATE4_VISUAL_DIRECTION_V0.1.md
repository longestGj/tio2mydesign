## 1. Design Intent

The direction presents alternative-origin sourcing as an evidence-led procurement review, not a marketing promise. It combines a technical editorial page with a numbered ledger rhythm so buyers can scan identity, origin, technical, application, document and destination questions without mistaking the interface for a certification dashboard.

The page remains white-led, sober and information-dense. Teal identifies actions, sequence and focus; Navy carries headings and navigation; Deep Navy is reserved for high-importance answer or closing surfaces. Thin rules and aligned number rails organize evidence. Decorative media is not required.

## 4. Component Direction

### 4.1 Hero and Qualification Path

- Keep the H1 and supporting copy in the primary reading column.
- Use a three-row qualification-path ledger in the supporting column.
- Use full-width actions on Mobile; do not introduce a decorative image dependency.
- Retain the exact approved Hero copy when Gate 5 is produced.

### 4.2 Direct Answer

- Use Deep Navy with white text and a single vertical teal rule.
- Keep the question and answer in separate Desktop columns and a single Mobile column.
- The dark surface signals an answer boundary, not a compliance badge or verified-outcome claim.

### 4.3 Six Checks

- Use two columns on Desktop where real copy remains legible; collapse to one column on Mobile.
- Preserve `01–06`, heading, body and Buyer check as a repeated ledger grammar.
- Do not substitute icons, certification marks or traffic-light status colors for evidence language.

### 4.4 Comparison and Decision Cards

- Technical comparison uses a numbered sequence and one evidence-boundary callout.
- Destination cards use Navy top rules; decision cards use a short Teal rule.
- Category labels are small and descriptive; they are not badges.
- Route eligibility and conditional rendering remain governed by the Gate 2 contract.

### 4.5 FAQ and Focus

- FAQ states use thin separators, readable question weight and a visible `+` or `−` state.
- Keyboard focus uses a `3px` Functional Teal outline with `3px` offset.
- Every visible interactive target must be at least `44px` high.

### 4.6 Global Chrome and Logos

- Consume Global Chrome V0.5; do not fork Header, Mobile Header, Mobile Menu or Footer.
- `Resources` is current on Desktop and Mobile.
- Use the production primary horizontal logo on white/light Header and Mobile surfaces.
- Use the production reverse monochrome logo only on Deep Navy Footer-class surfaces.

## 5. Image and Claim Restrictions

Do not use stock photography, factory imagery, certificate imagery, customs imagery, flags, maps, gradients, glossy UI, trust badges or decorative performance claims. Do not introduce named grades, M-996/M-2196, product-application mappings, named customs acceptance, tariff treatment or a specific trade outcome.

The approved `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide` phrase may be used where relevant under the site-wide authority, but this page's approved copy does not require it and Gate 4 does not force it into the visual direction.

## 6. Responsive Direction

- Desktop: preserve two-column editorial decisions and evidence comparison where readable.
- Tablet: retain reading order, allow the ledger and card families to reduce columns without truncation.
- Mobile: one-column content, full-width primary actions, number rail retained, direct-answer contrast retained, no horizontal overflow.
- Mobile Menu open: use Global Chrome V0.5 behavior and visibly mark `Resources` current.

## 7. Gate Boundary

This document defines a Gate 4 direction only. The visual board contains representative buyer-facing excerpts and clearly separated design notes. It does not satisfy the Gate 5 requirement for complete 1440px, 768px and 390px full-page visuals, Mobile Menu open and all applicable states.
