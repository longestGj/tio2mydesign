# Titanium Dioxide Supplier for the Netherlands

IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. provides Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide for industrial procurement and technical evaluation in the Netherlands. ## Start with Your Application and Evaluation Requirements

Use the information you already have about the application, process and end use. These details help you compare product information and prepare a useful enquiry; they do not need to be complete before you begin.

The [Vereniging van Verf- en Drukinktfabrikanten (VVVF)](https://www.vvvf.nl/brancheorganisatie) groups the market segments in which its member companies are active into sections for construction, printing inks, automotive refinish, marine, protective coatings, fire-resistant coatings, and roads or road marking. Use those contexts to describe your project; they do not determine which TiO2 grade to use.

### Coatings

For a coating project, state the system and end use. When relevant, say whether the project is construction, automotive refinish, marine, protective, fire-resistant or road marking; whether the system is waterborne or solvent-borne; and whether it is intended for indoor or outdoor use. Add the substrate and the properties you need to evaluate.

### Printing Inks

Include the printing process, substrate, ink system and finished application. Add the appearance, opacity, dispersion and processing requirements you will use to assess a grade.

### Plastics and Masterbatch

Include the resin, processing route and finished product. State whether you are evaluating TiO2 directly in a plastic product or as an input to your own masterbatch production, then add the appearance, dispersion and processing requirements that matter to the project.

## Documents for Product and Supplier Review

For a Grade you are evaluating, request the TDS, SDS, COA or origin and supplier-qualification documentation needed for review. Select one Product Grade and one or more document types on the request form.

A Certificate of Origin is available upon request.

If you do not yet know the Grade, you can start the request and use the Product Hub when you need help identifying candidates; a Grade must be selected before submission.

If you need documents for more than one Grade, select one Grade in the form and name the additional Grades in Additional Requirements. Use separate requests when the document types differ by Grade.

## Request a Quote for Delivery to the Netherlands

You can request a quotation with a known Grade, several candidate Grades, or “Not sure / Need help.” Include:

- the Product Grade or target requirement;
- the application, process and evaluation context;
- the required quantity in metric tonnes (MT), using your current estimate if the final quantity is not fixed;
- the delivery location—the Netherlands is carried into the form and remains editable, and you can add a city or port if known;
- your company details and business contact information.

If you are considering more than one Grade, add the other candidate Grades in Additional Requirements.

After you submit your quotation request, our team will review your requirements and contact you using the information provided.
