### 3.2 Decision the page should enable

After reading, a procurement or technical team should be able to:

1. identify the production route stated for a candidate grade;
2. understand the high-level chemical distinction between the two routes;
3. recognize that route may be technically relevant while rejecting the route label alone as sufficient evidence for a grade-level performance conclusion;
4. identify which grade-specific properties, treatments, methods and application conditions require comparison;
5. decide whether evidence is sufficient to shortlist a grade, whether testing is required, or whether the comparison should remain on hold; and
6. continue to an eligible product, application or process-owner page without transferring this page's comparison keyword to those pages.

The page does not select a winning process, approve a grade, validate a formulation, determine environmental superiority, or make a purchasing decision for the buyer.

## 4. Proposed H1, Hero Promise and Direct Answer

### H1

`Chloride vs Sulfate Titanium Dioxide: A Buyer’s Evaluation Guide`

### Hero promise

`Understand what the production route tells you—and which grade-level evidence still needs to be checked before qualification.`

### Direct answer direction

> Chloride and sulfate identify two different production routes for titanium dioxide pigment. The production route can influence how the base pigment is formed, but the route label alone is insufficient to establish a grade-level performance conclusion. Opacity, hiding power, undertone, weatherability, dispersion and formulation suitability must be evaluated using current grade-specific evidence and application-relevant testing.

### Why this direction

- It answers the comparison immediately without manufacturing an artificial winner.
- It preserves the technically relevant influence of production route while limiting what the label alone can substantiate.
- It uses the primary keyword naturally and preserves the page's informational role.
- It converts all four secondary-keyword themes into evidence questions rather than unsupported promises.
- It routes the buyer from process awareness toward specific-grade qualification.
- It is independently understandable for visible-page and GEO use without hidden facts.

## 8. Product and Process Relationship Boundary

PRODUCT V0.3 remains the sole approved TiO2 Malaysia grade/application/process relationship source.

- Neutral classification available internally: 8 Chloride, 5 Sulfate and 1 Vapor-phase oxidation grade.
- The matrix supports process labels. Those labels may be technically relevant, but they are insufficient on their own to establish grade-level performance conclusions.
- A shared process does not establish equivalence, substitutability or identical application behavior.
- `M-996` versus `M-2196` differentiation remains frozen.
- `M-2377` process and application statements remain limited to their approved evidence rows.
- `CR-901` is Vapor-phase oxidation and must not be forced into the chloride/sulfate binary.
- `NO_PUBLIC_MAPPING` never means “not suitable”.
- No grade list, product card or recommendation is required merely because the implementation template can render one.
