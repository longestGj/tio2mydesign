AD0086 - Rutile Titanium Dioxide from China

  Active  

Dumping Investigation

The goods that are the subject of the investigation are:

Rutile titanium oxides in pigments and preparations based on rutile titanium dioxide, containing a minimum of 80% by weight of titanium dioxide calculated on the dry matter and having all types of particle sizes, classified under Chemical Abstracts Service Registry Numbers 12065-65-5 and 13463-67-7
