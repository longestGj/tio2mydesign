## Approved article direction

**Working title:** Titanium Dioxide for Plastic Film: How to Compare Opacity, Dispersion, and Processing at a Defined Film Thickness

Write one APP-PLAS second-layer article for film fabricators and technical evaluators. Its explanatory centre is the relationship among film thickness, TiO2 concentration/distribution, optical result, dispersion-related defects and process response in a declared film system.

## Why WRITE passes

1. The reader is a specific industrial film buyer/evaluator.
2. The decision is narrower than the approved Plastics pillar and materially different from R01.
3. Search results show coherent technical and commercial film intent despite unavailable metrics and heavy masterbatch noise.
4. Primary technical evidence exists for thickness/opacity, dispersion/agglomeration, film defects and process variables.
5. APP-PLAS clearly owns the finished-film decision; APP-MB owns concentrate formulation and production.
6. The topic can be useful without recipes, Grade recommendations, equivalence or guaranteed outcomes.
7. It leads naturally to a current-document request and controlled matched film trial.

## Merge decision

PVC/calendered film will not become a separate article. Its independent SERP is weak and formulation/patent-heavy. It is merged into R02 only as a controlled non-transfer branch. The article must not imply that PE blown-film evidence proves PVC calendered-film performance.
