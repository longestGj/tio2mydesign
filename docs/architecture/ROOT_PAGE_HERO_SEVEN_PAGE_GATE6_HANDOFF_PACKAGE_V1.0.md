## 6. Required shared behaviour and non-goals

1. **`APPROVED_CONTRACT`** — one reusable `RootPageHero`; consumer differences are limited to the bindings and controlled variants above. No page-owned Hero DOM/CSS, margin patch, independent breakpoint, or fourth variant.
2. **`APPROVED_CONTRACT`** — normal shared typography is Desktop `56px/700`, Tablet `44px/700`, Mobile `36px/700`; normal maximum is three H1 lines. `RES-000` and `ABOUT-001` alone may naturally take four lines at 390px under their exceptions.
3. **`APPROVED_CONTRACT`** — shared layout keeps the approved responsive stack: media after actions on compact layouts, informative media retained unless the page's approved binding explicitly gives a zero-space rule; no fixed-height crop, hidden action, or horizontal overflow.
4. **`APPROVED_CONTRACT`** — primary actions use the accessible functional token `#008078` with white label on light surfaces; all actions are at least 44px and retain exact destinations.
5. **`APPROVED_CONTRACT`** — consume Global Chrome V0.5: desktop/mobile navigation mapping, 84px/64px header geometry, fixed RFQ, production Logo, one accessible current navigation surface at the active viewport, and buyer-visible `CURRENT=0`. Footer remains unchanged.
6. **`APPROVED_CONTRACT`** — preserve each page's immediate body boundary, metadata, canonical, SEO/GEO, Schema, approved facts, routes and form behavior. Do not turn static preview anchors or route-safe names into newly live routes.
7. **`APPROVED_CONTRACT`** — enforce `site_scope=tio2-my` for content/query, routing, cache, menu, metadata/SEO, media, and form context. No cross-scope source, fallback, or buyer-visible internal identity.
8. **`PROTOTYPE_ONLY`** — never merge or treat the Gate 4 prototype source as production code, CMS/API model, security/receiver implementation, or runtime proof. If a cited media/preview dependency cannot be used in D16, preserve the approved visible semantics with an approved asset/derivative path; do not invent facts or silently substitute a different component.
9. Non-goals: no body redesign; no CTA/route/receiver change; no Global Chrome/Logo redesign; no new page, keyword, Schema fact, product/market relationship, deployment, release, DNS, sitemap, or index action.
