## 3. Agent direction proposed for confirmation

### Audience

Brazil industrial TiO2 procurement/import teams and technical or formulation evaluators considering an external supplier. The page should also serve an early-stage reader who has not selected a grade.

This is a useful design audience based on the project B2B position plus supplier/product/contact result types. It is not represented as a population survey or search-demand percentage.

### Buyer problem

The reader needs to determine whether a Malaysia-origin supply option is worth evaluating, find product/application information, understand available document/request paths, prepare enough context for a quotation, and know where to obtain current Brazil trade information.

### Core message

Malaysia-origin industrial titanium dioxide for Brazil procurement evaluation: understand the portfolio, evaluate products by application, request documentation where needed, and submit a clear quotation request.

### Required inclusion and exclusion

Include the approved Malaysia-origin statement, supplier/product clarity, neutral product and application paths, documents path, quote preparation, and a concise dated trade-resource handoff.

Exclude PT-BR page work, detailed rates or legal conclusions, duty-avoidance selling, unapproved local capability claims, Brazil-specific grade ranking/recommendation, public price tables and competitor-derived claims.

### CTA

- Proposed Primary: `Request a Quote` → `/request-a-quote/`.
- Proposed Secondary: `Explore Products` → `/products/`.

Rationale: the supplier query and visible supplier-page actions support a commercial next step; product exploration keeps the page usable when a reader is not ready to specify a grade. Documents and trade information are supporting routes, not competing primary conversions.

This combined direction is `BR-EN-D01` and was approved by the user's reply `同意。` on 2026-09-06.
