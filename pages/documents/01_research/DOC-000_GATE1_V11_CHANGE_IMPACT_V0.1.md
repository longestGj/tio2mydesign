## 3. SEO/GEO/Schema impact

- URL, page type, `NO_PRIMARY_KEYWORD`, secondary keyword set and cannibalization ownership remain unchanged.
- Finder/result/file-record relationships from old drafts cannot be carried forward.
- Future GEO can describe only visible qualification processes, five groups, Grade selection and request handoff.
- No file/record/download Schema is eligible without visible verified records.
- Child pages retain REACH, TDS/SDS/COA and COO search intent; Hub cards do not become child links.

## 4. Conversion impact

- Body primary CTA is unified as `Request Documents`.
- The Hub must require one Grade selection before continuing.
- Intended transfer is the selected Grade only; the receiving field must be editable.
- `/request-documents/` remains a separate Conversion page and is not designed or implemented by this task.
- Receiver readiness, parameter validation, success/error behavior and analytics remain downstream dependencies.
