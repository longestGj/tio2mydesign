## 7. Proposed Page Intent checkpoint

The proposed APP-PAPER page is a mixed informational and commercial technical-evaluation page for paper technical, laboratory, quality and technical-procurement teams evaluating pigmentary TiO2 in a declared Paper system.

Its job is to help the reader define the paper type, furnish, construction, addition route, material conditions, target finished-paper properties and method basis; keep pigment information, system observations and finished-paper results distinct; then review two neutral Paper Grade relationships or continue through appropriate website request paths.

The approved decorative/lightweight replacement article is the bounded technical core, not proof that all Paper contexts are interchangeable. APP-PAPER owns the generic Paper application intent. Grade pages own model facts; Product Process pages own process intent; Documents, Sample and RFQ pages own request behavior; Resources own standalone methods and time-sensitive material. Finished-paper products and new Paper-type landing pages remain outside current scope.
