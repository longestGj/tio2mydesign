## 5. Buyer cold-read outputs

### 5.1 Most dangerous action sentence

Exact buyer sentence: `Thank you. We have received your general inquiry for review.`

Risk and guard: this sentence would falsely claim receipt if triggered by a timer, browser validation or ambiguous network result. The stable contract permits it only after the Gate 8-approved processor returns an authenticated, unambiguous success response for that exact submission. Otherwise, the form renders `Your inquiry was not sent`, keeps all six inputs and offers `Try again`.

Action chain: the buyer submits `Send a General Inquiry` on `/contact/`; the approved processor sends the six-field payload to the Gate 8-established receiver; confirmed success renders the inline success state, while failure does not claim receipt.

It remains clear about task fit and avoids importing or inventing the downstream page's field contract. It should be checked against the current Sample owner during controller review; expansion is not recommended unless that owner provides approved wording.
