### `BE-G6-F02` — live shared-agent identity drift after the Gate 4 freeze

- **Actual:** two declarations, in the V1.1 input index and revision-admission diagnostic, claim the then-current `agents/gate4-complete-visual/agent.md` at 7,953 bytes / `b0a0c682...d9ab`. - **Disposition:** **`OPEN_FOR_ROOT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW / NO_PAGE_SOURCE_REPAIR_PROPOSED`**.
