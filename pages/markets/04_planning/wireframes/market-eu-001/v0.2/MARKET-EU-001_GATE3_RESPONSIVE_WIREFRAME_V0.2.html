Procurement path

Build the Procurement Path Around Your Import

A useful supplier assessment starts with the product and transaction rather than a generic compliance label.

  1. 01

    Define the application

    State whether the titanium dioxide will be evaluated for coatings, plastics, masterbatch, printing inks or paper. The intended use determines which technical questions and product information matter most.

  2. 02

    Identify grades to evaluate

    Share a known grade when you have one. If the grade is still open, begin with the application, formulation, process and evaluation criteria before narrowing the product selection.

  3. 03

    Confirm the importing role

    Identify the EU importing entity and confirm whether an only-representative arrangement is relevant to the substance, supply chain and annual volume.

  4. 04

    Request the required information

    Specify the technical, safety, quality and COA-related, origin, and supplier-qualification information your team needs.

  5. 05

    Check classification and origin

    Verify the current CN or TARIC classification, the substantiated origin of the goods and the measures that apply to the specific transaction.

  6. 06

    Prepare the commercial request

    Provide the destination country, estimated quantity, required timing and packaging requirements so the enquiry can be assessed in context.

Requirements vary by product, importer role and transaction. Confirm the current position with the responsible EU importer and qualified advisers where needed.

EU import responsibilities

Confirm the EU Importing Role and Applicable Responsibilities

For products imported from outside the EU or EEA, REACH and CLP responsibilities generally sit with the EU importer or, for applicable REACH obligations, an EU-established only representative. The non-EU supplier supports the assessment with relevant product and supply-chain information. The exact responsibility depends on the substance, importer role, annual volume and transaction circumstances.

Official reference: European Chemicals Agency, REACH and CLP for non-EU companies. Checked 4 September 2026.

Check the ECHA Guidance
  • Identify the EU importing entity.
  • Confirm whether an EU-established only representative is relevant for applicable REACH obligations.
  • Check the substance, annual volume, intended use and supply-chain circumstances.
  • Confirm the classification, labelling and information duties applicable to the transaction.

EU customs and trade context

Check Current EU Customs and Trade Requirements

EU customs treatment depends on the product's classification, substantiated origin and the measures in force at the time of import. Verify the current TARIC position and applicable official rules for the specific product and transaction.

For EU buyers assessing sourcing diversification, Malaysia-origin supply can be considered as a separate procurement path. Any customs or trade treatment still depends on product classification, substantiated origin and the measures applicable to the transaction.

Current titanium dioxide trade context

Checked 4 September 2026: the EU has definitive anti-dumping measures concerning defined titanium dioxide imports originating in China. On 25 August 2026, the European Commission opened an absorption reinvestigation concerning those measures. The investigation is ongoing, so buyers should not rely on a fixed rate or scope without a current official check.

Measures concerning goods of one origin do not, by themselves, determine the customs treatment of goods of another origin. Product classification, substantiated origin and the measures in force must be assessed together.

  • Notice C/2026/4533

Buyer questions

Questions From EU Procurement Teams

Can TiO2 Malaysia supply titanium dioxide to EU buyers?

Yes. TiO2 Malaysia supports EU industrial buyers evaluating Malaysia-origin rutile titanium dioxide grades through product information, document requests, sample enquiries and quotation requests. TiO2 Malaysia is operated by IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. in Malaysia and is not presented as an EU-established supplier.

Is TiO2 Malaysia an EU-based supplier?

No EU establishment is claimed. TiO2 Malaysia is a Malaysia-based supply platform for industrial buyers, including procurement teams in the European Union.

What applications are TiO2 Malaysia titanium dioxide grades intended for?

The current approved grade paths cover coatings, plastics, masterbatch, printing inks, paper and one Specialty Materials path. Grade selection should be based on formulation, process and technical requirements together with current product information and supporting documentation.

Who handles REACH and CLP responsibilities for an EU import?

Responsibility generally sits with the EU importer or, for applicable REACH obligations, an EU-established only representative. Confirm the exact role, substance, annual volume and transaction circumstances.

What documents can our team request?

Requests can cover technical and product information, safety documentation, quality and COA-related information, and origin and supplier-qualification information. Availability and applicable scope are confirmed after the selected grade and request purpose are submitted.

How should we select a grade?

Start with the intended application, formulation, process and evaluation criteria. Compare the relevant product information and identify the grade in your request when known.

Do EU measures on Chinese titanium dioxide determine the treatment of Malaysian-origin goods?

No. Treatment must be assessed using the specific product classification, substantiated origin and measures in force for the transaction.

What should we include in a quotation request?

Include the application, grade if known, destination country, estimated quantity, required timing, packaging needs and the information your team needs to assess.
