## 1. Independent buyer reading of CL-01, CL-03 and CL-04

### CL-01 — application relevance

The Hero now says:

> Explore eight Malaysia-origin chloride-process titanium dioxide grades for applications including coatings, plastics, masterbatch, printing inks and paper.

Coatings, Plastics, Masterbatch, Printing Inks and Paper buyers can each recognize the page as a possible starting point. The phrase applies to the eight-Grade range and uses `including`; it does not state that every Grade serves every named application.

### CL-03 — scan guidance

The revised sentence `Because application needs can overlap, review all eight entries` is natural buyer guidance. It explains why a buyer should inspect the full set without exposing `relationship`, `mapping`, `exhaustive` or evidence-state language. The following direction to open the individual product page makes Grade-level evidence the basis for judgment and avoids a universal suitability inference.

### CL-04 — evaluation context

The five application contexts are introduced with `For example, this may mean`. They help a buyer identify the information to bring to a Grade evaluation: coating system/exposure, resin/processing/end product, ink system/printing process/substrate, or paper type/production/end use. They do not assign any application to a specific Grade, rank Grades or promise formulation suitability.
