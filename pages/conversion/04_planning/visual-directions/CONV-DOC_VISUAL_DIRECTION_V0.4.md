## 1. Buyer Clean direction

Retain the approved brand palette, typography, responsive single-column form, selected-card treatment and accessible state language. Revise the tone from governance-heavy to buyer-facing:

- Hero is compact Deep Navy with H1 and one action-oriented sentence.
- No warning card or review ribbon competes with the page purpose.
- Helper text explains how to complete the field, not internal evidence status.
- Review is a clean input summary.
- Privacy is a short final sentence with a visible Privacy Policy link.
- Success and failure are concise operational states.
- The shared Footer exposes final legal navigation without page-local styling divergence.

## 2. Conditional validation direction

Other-only required state uses the existing label, helper, error, summary and focus patterns. It does not add a field or create a new document category. Other-plus-named-type returns the field to optional while preserving any entered text.

## 3. Prohibited Buyer Clean elements

No `INTERNAL REVIEW`, `PENDING COPY`, `RELEASE BLOCKER`, approval-required placeholder, internal status ribbon, governance code, file-availability badge, download icon, certificate seal, market selector or Contact fallback may appear.

## 4. State palette

- Focus: Navy control edge, white separation, amber outer ring.
- Error/failure: red structural rule plus text.
- Selected/request received: Teal structural rule plus checkbox/heading.
- Privacy link: underlined high-contrast text with independent focus treatment.
- Shared legal links use the approved Deep Navy Footer hierarchy.
