## 1. Direction statement

The European Union page is a procurement-oriented industrial editorial page. Its visual signature is the six-step review path, followed by clear transitions among application, product review, document responsibility, origin, current trade context and destination-specific continuation.

The page must not look like a tourism page, geographic coverage map, compliance dashboard, warehouse locator or promotional country-card directory.

## 3. Page-specific component direction

### 3.1 Hero procurement path

- Preserve the approved 7/5 Desktop relationship.
- Present the six steps as a vertical editorial rail using 32px numbered nodes, a fine connecting rule and Soft Background surface.
- The rail communicates review sequence only; it is not a logistics route, certification process or guaranteed workflow.
- Tablet and Mobile stack the rail after the Hero actions without changing its sequence.

### 3.2 Direct answer and checklist

- Direct answer uses a White surface, Teal left rule and restrained radius/shadow.
- Checklist cards use consistent Navy headings, Teal editorial numbers and thin borders.
- Cards remain flat and technical; no illustrations or success-state icons are introduced.

### 3.3 Applications and product review

- Application surfaces remain simple text-led cards with Teal inline links.
- No individual Application receives a theme colour or promotional image.
- Product review remains a compact editorial band with one Teal primary action and one Navy-outline secondary action.

### 3.4 Documents and EU import responsibility

- Desktop uses a 7/5 relationship: four document categories form a quiet 2×2 information grid; the importing-responsibility answer receives a White raised surface with Teal top rule.
- The responsibility surface is prominent enough to aid review but must not be presented as a compliance certificate or guarantee.
- Official ECHA source text remains visibly secondary and linked.

### 3.5 Manufacturing and origin

- Use an asymmetric editorial split with Navy rule and source line.
- Do not add factory photography, Malaysia flag imagery, certification seals or provenance icons.

### 3.6 Customs and trade context

- Use a Navy top rule for the evergreen customs guidance.
- Place the dated 4 September 2026 context in a separate White surface with Teal top rule.
- Place official references in a third compact source surface so dated facts, analysis boundary and source actions cannot merge visually.
- Do not visualise duty rates or imply that the ongoing investigation has concluded.

### 3.7 Destinations, questions and conversion

- Six destinations use flat bordered navigation rows, not a map or availability grid.
- Buyer Questions remain expanded in the Gate 4 proof to demonstrate full-copy rhythm; Gate 5 will define the complete interactive-state contract.
- Final conversion and Footer use Deep Navy. The Teal RFQ action remains visually primary; Documents remains outline; Sample remains supporting text.

## 4. Media rule

The approved Gate 4 default is image-free. No generated or stock imagery is required. A later Gate may propose a restrained abstract line motif only if it remains non-geographic, decorative, empty-ALT and free of supply-coverage implications.

Prohibited visual content includes maps, flags, globes, ports, shipping routes, office pins, warehouse markers, stock indicators, coverage regions, factory photographs and certification badges.

## 5. Key state direction

- Default Desktop and responsive Tablet/Mobile surfaces are demonstrated by the current Gate 4 proof.
- Markets current navigation remains Bold + 3px Teal underline on Desktop.
- Mobile Header remains `Logo | RFQ | Menu` at 64px.
- Links and controls retain visible focus rings and minimum 44px action height.
- Buyer Questions are shown expanded for copy validation; collapsed, hover, focus and keyboard states must be completed in Gate 5.
- Mobile Menu-open is a Gate 5 deliverable, not part of this Gate 4 approval object.
