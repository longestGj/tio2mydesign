## 2. Traceable observations

The first ten presented organic blocks for the original query are recorded in `R03_SERP_EVIDENCE_V0.1.csv`. They comprise five dedicated powder-coating pages, two broader coatings/TiO2 pages with powder content, two manufacturer portfolio/application pages, and one manufacturer PDF. This result shape supports a coherent powder-coating application interpretation, but it does not prove local demand, buyer occupation or query volume.

Representative page reading and result-text observations:

- ASTM D3451-24 states that powder-coating test selection and interpretation depend on the application, powder chemistry and purchaser-seller agreement. Its public scope also separates some functional pipe/rebar coating contexts.
- ASTM D6441-05(2024) treats powder-coating hiding power relative to cured film thickness and defined reflectometric measurements. Its public scope does not create a universal acceptance threshold.
- The 2011 *Progress in Organic Coatings* paper on pigment loading reports that appearance changes with TiO2 loading, powder particle size, film thickness and formulation/process conditions in the systems studied.
- The 1999 *Progress in Organic Coatings* paper on block-copolymer dispersants identifies extrusion-stage pigment dispersion as a powder-specific problem and warns that indirect gloss/opacity observations are affected by other formulation and process variables.
- Chemours and INEOS pages show that suppliers treat powder coatings as a recognizable application context. Their grade claims remain proprietary and non-transferable to TiO2 Malaysia.

The commercial expansion returned many generic TiO2 supplier, TDS and sample pages, plus irrelevant consumer/cosmetic results. It therefore helps identify commercial vocabulary but does not establish that `supplier`, `TDS` or `sample` is the dominant original-query intent.

## 3. Intent interpretations and counterevidence

Supported interpretation: the original query can represent a technical-commercial investigation into how a TiO2 candidate should be compared in a powder-coating system. Repeated topic elements include melt extrusion/dispersion, powder manufacture and application, cured film thickness, opacity, gloss/flow/leveling, colour shift during cure and durability.

Other reasonable interpretations remain present:

- a direct supplier or grade search;
- a general explanation of TiO2 in coatings;
- a formulation or processing recipe search;
- accidental ambiguity with titanium metal powder, TiO2 nanopowder or deposited TiO2 coatings.

These alternatives prevent a claim that all searchers share one task. They also require a title and opening that say `powder coatings` clearly and avoid broad `powder` wording.

## 5. Unknowns and limitations

- No controlled Malaysia, US or other local Google result was observed; no location, personalization, ads or SERP-feature evidence is available.
- No Semrush report was available. Volume, KD, CPC and trend remain unknown rather than zero.
- Public ASTM pages provide scope and significance, not the paid procedures.
- Two publisher pages could not be opened directly; their search-result records were retained, so detailed numerical claims must be rechecked by D22 before use.
- Supplier dosage, temperatures, thresholds, grade recommendations and performance claims are discovery leads only.
- Search results do not establish TiO2 Malaysia grade suitability for powder coatings.
