## 4. Stable technical fact and source contract

| ID | Permitted visible predicate | Source boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `PLAS-G2-C01` | Compare candidates in the declared resin, introduction route, process, specimen, exposure, method and accepted endpoint. | User-approved Plastics article statements 001–006 and 037–050. No universal settings, pass rule or Grade inference. |
| `PLAS-G2-C02` | Finished-system opacity/whiteness depends on system and method; CIE Y over black/white is one stated contrast-ratio approach. | TDMA and HunterLab. No finished-part guarantee or universal loading. |
| `PLAS-G2-C03` | ISO 23900-5 FPV and ISO 23900-6 film tests are bounded dispersion assessments; production defects require broader investigation. | ISO 23900-5:2015 and ISO 23900-6:2015. No universal cutoff or single-cause diagnosis. |
| `PLAS-G2-C04` | Accelerated weathering requires named material, property, exposure and comparator; there is no universal hours-to-outdoor-years conversion. | Q-Lab 2022, Atlas 2024 and NIST page dated 2020. NIST's cited study does not establish TiO2-formulation behaviour. |
| `PLAS-G2-C05` | Resin examples generate input questions and remain bounded to the reported materials and methods. | Hricova et al. 2025; Loaeza et al. 2021; Blackwood et al. 1995 plus erratum; Wetzel et al. 2023; ISO 1110:2019. No transferability, compatibility, drying, process or Grade conclusion. Detailed masterbatch formulation stays with APP-MB. |
| `PLAS-G2-C06` | Matching TDS fields do not establish equivalence; price, nominal content and loading become comparable only at the same accepted technical output and a buyer-defined accounting boundary. | User-approved article and ASQ COQ reference. No saving, ROI, price or commercial outcome. |
| `PLAS-G2-C07` | Exactly eight neutral Plastics relations render: M-350, M-510, M-200, M-108, M-210, M-340, M-886 and M-2377. | PRODUCT V0.3. Only neutral relationship and process classification; no ranking, recommendation, resin suitability, performance, equivalence or availability. |

Thirteen source notes remain visible with entity, page type where material, date where published and a buyer-verifiable public landing link. The obsolete BASF product record is excluded because its current URL does not identify the named product. The approved article's 62 private statement markers never render.

## 6. Shared-owner and conditional behaviour

- Global Chrome uses its current owner contract; Applications is current and no buyer-visible `CURRENT` label renders.
- Product and Grade relations use PRODUCT V0.3. An unavailable downstream Grade route is omitted without an empty card or replacement claim.
- APP-MB owns high-concentration masterbatch carrier/formulation and production qualification.
- Documents, Sample and RFQ use their current owner contracts. APP-PLAS adds only the context in §3.
- Failed source or downstream routes never create visible readiness, evidence or governance language.

## 7. Contract-changing future proposals

A new contract version is required for any Plastics child page, APP-PLAS/APP-MB responsibility change, Grade recommendation or resin suitability, formula/loading, universal acceptance threshold, method procedure, equivalence claim, current availability/price/stock/sample/lead-time claim, final URL decision, or new action/prefill/receiver behaviour.
