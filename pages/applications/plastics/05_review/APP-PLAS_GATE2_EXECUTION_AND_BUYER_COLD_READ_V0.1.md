| Grades | Eight equal navigation options appear once each. One, several and unknown-Grade paths all continue without an automatic assignment. |

The most dangerous action sentence is the RFQ post-submit expectation. It is limited to review of requirements/details and contact using the provided information; it does not promise a quote or commercial result.

The weakest sentence identified in the first pass was the route transition. It was resolved in B by explicitly distinguishing how TiO2 enters the final plastic from the separate task of producing or qualifying a high-concentration masterbatch.
