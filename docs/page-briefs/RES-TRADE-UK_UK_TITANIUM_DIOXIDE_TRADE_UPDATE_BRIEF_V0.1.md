## 6. Evidence-backed Fact Set

The detailed inventory is in `RES-TRADE-UK_GATE1_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_V0.1.csv`.

- The TRA initiated AD0086 on 3 March 2026; the official public file showed `Active` and `Last updated 2 September 2026` when captured on 6 September 2026.
- The written case scope concerns rutile titanium oxides/pigments/preparations based on rutile TiO2 and containing at least 80% titanium dioxide by dry weight, originating in China, under the listed commodity codes.
- The investigation period is 1 January–31 December 2025; the injury period in the initiation notice is 1 January 2022–31 December 2025.
- Notice 2026/14 registers specified imports from 27 March 2026 using additional code `8A60`; anatase/brookite exclusions under the same commodity codes use `8A61`.
- Registration continues until a Secretary of State stop notice. A later definitive remedy could create liability for a limited earlier period, but the registration notice does not set a TiO2 duty rate.
- HMRC guidance says anti-dumping duties use non-preferential origin and that Northern Ireland entries may require both UK/EU tariff and at-risk checks.

No definitive outcome, provisional measure, ordinary tariff percentage, VAT rate or transaction-specific customs decision is in the proposed Gate 1 fact set.
