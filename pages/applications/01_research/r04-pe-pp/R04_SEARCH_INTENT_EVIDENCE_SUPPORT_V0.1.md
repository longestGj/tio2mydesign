## 2. Search observations

The first ten presented results for each original query are recorded in `R04_SERP_EVIDENCE_V0.1.csv`.

### PE query

The PE sample mixes polymer weathering/degradation papers, rotomoulded-polyethylene research, generic TiO2 supplier pages, white-masterbatch pages, photocatalytic film research and an unrelated PE association FAQ. One dedicated `PE/PP films` page appears, but that overlaps the already approved R02 Plastic Film article and contains supplier recipes.

### PP query

The PP sample is even more research-heavy: thermal ageing, photocatalytic degradation, nanoparticle surface functionalisation and recycled PP/PET nanocomposites appear beside generic plastics pages and one white-masterbatch product. This does not form a clean pigmentary-TiO2 purchasing or application page set.

### Polyolefin query

The umbrella query returns generic plastics-grade selectors, producer grade pages and polymer-environment research. It shows that suppliers use polyolefin as a portfolio/application label, but it does not demonstrate a dominant standalone `polyolefin` article task.

Comparison expansions are dominated by PP white-masterbatch TDS/product pages, patents and nanocomposite studies. They support a real masterbatch/carrier branch but not an APP-PLAS article about concentrate formulation.

## 3. Supported interpretation and counterevidence

The three original samples do not justify two separate PE and PP articles. They do support a narrower combined interpretation: technical teams working across PE and PP need to decide which TiO2 observations can transfer and which must be rebuilt in each declared resin, conversion route and finished article.

Other interpretations remain material:

- white-masterbatch selection or carrier compatibility, owned by APP-MB/R07;
- plastic-film performance, already owned by R02;
- nano-TiO2 composite functionality or photocatalytic degradation, outside pigmentary-TiO2 scope;
- generic plastics-grade supplier selection, substantially answered by APP-PLAS;
- environmental fate of plastic litter, not a current commercial Application article.

Because these alternatives are strong, a combined article must state its cross-resin evidence-transfer question in the title and opening. A broad `TiO2 for polyolefins` guide would be too ambiguous.

## 5. Unknowns and impact

- No local Google or Semrush metrics were observed; demand magnitude is unknown, not zero.
- `PE` and `PP` abbreviations create query ambiguity.
- Public ISO material does not include complete paid procedures.
- Much of the literature uses nanoparticles, photocatalytic intent, environmental litter, composites or highly specific formulations and cannot support pigmentary Grade guidance.
- Search evidence does not establish TiO2 Malaysia Grade suitability, PE/PP carrier compatibility, universal processing settings or cross-resin equivalence.

These limitations weaken the case for two resin-specific articles but do not prevent one bounded cross-resin comparison article.
