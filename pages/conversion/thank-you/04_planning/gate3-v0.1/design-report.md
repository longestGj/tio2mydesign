## Responsive decisions

- **1440:** a centered result panel keeps the short result task together while the complete shared Header and Footer remain visible in the full-page evidence.
- **768:** the result remains one coherent panel with an independently reduced content width and spacing. The shared narrow Header exposes RFQ and Menu without shrinking Desktop navigation.
- **390:** icon and receipt text stack, heading and body wrap naturally, and every two- or three-action group becomes a full-width single column. All controls remain at least 44px wide and high.
- The exact state copy, action order and targets do not change by viewport. No media is required or introduced.
