## 4. Search intent and procurement stage

### 4.1 Primary interpretation

The registered commercial intent is best served as **technical commercial investigation with an informational entry point**:

- A technically oriented visitor may first need a process definition.
- A buyer or formulator then needs to identify which portfolio Grades carry that classification.
- The reader must continue to an exact Grade/application/document decision; process alone is not enough.
- A buyer who wants route comparison should go to the dedicated comparison resource.

### 4.2 Other plausible intents

The exact query also exposes researchers looking for:

- industrial chemistry or process flows;
- environmental and effluent history;

- route comparison.

These are not all responsibilities of this B2B industrial-pigment Process page. Early scope qualification is necessary. This page should not attempt to rank for or answer every technical variation.

- do not infer a country-specific opportunity from a GLOBAL search surface.

## 7. Process-definition evidence and restrictions

The external evidence supports only a restrained general statement such as:

> The sulfate process is a titanium dioxide production route that uses sulfuric acid to process titanium-bearing feedstock before subsequent steps form and finish TiO2 pigment.

This is a Gate 1 answer direction, not final approved copy. Gate 2 may improve its buyer clarity without adding unsupported technical detail.

It must not be expanded into:

- an assertion that IKHLAS owns or operates this process;
