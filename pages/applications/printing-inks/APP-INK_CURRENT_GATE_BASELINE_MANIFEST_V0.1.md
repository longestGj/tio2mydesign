## 7. Proposed Page Intent checkpoint

The proposed APP-INK page is a mixed informational and commercial technical-evaluation page for ink formulators, converters, laboratories, quality teams and technical procurement teams evaluating a candidate or replacement titanium dioxide pigment in a defined white-ink system.

Its job is to help the reader define the actual vehicle, print process, substrate, deposited film, cure, end use, incumbent reference and rejection rule; interpret opacity, coverage, color, dispersion, rheology and storage evidence in context; then review the four neutral Printing Inks Grade relationships or continue to an appropriate website request path.

APP-INK owns generic raw-pigment evaluation for Printing Inks. Grade pages own model-specific facts; Product Process pages own chloride/sulfate process information; Documents, Sample and RFQ pages own request behavior; Resources own standalone methods and compliance topics. Finished ink products, printer support and new process-specific Application pages are outside this page's current scope.
