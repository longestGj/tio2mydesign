## 2. Buyer task and page architecture

The page gives a coatings buyer a coherent decision sequence: define the actual coating system, separate the technical endpoints, establish a comparable basis, review the neutral Grade relations, and continue with the appropriate owner action. The technical modules are longer than a country landing page because each resolves a different coatings-evaluation question. They do not repeat one generic instruction or turn the page into internal project documentation.

The page does not require every buyer to inspect all eight Grades. It allows one known candidate, several relevant candidates or an unknown Grade to continue without guessing. The fixed module order is suitable for Full Copy approval and no additional FAQ, process-advantage, country, compliance or generic trust module is required.

## 4. Source and claim review

The six visible sources support bounded technical explanations rather than product performance claims. The current copy preserves the relevant study systems and limits:

- ASTM D3451 is used for powder-coating method-selection context, not as a reproduced procedure or universal threshold.
- NIST and Tiarks are used to explain prepared-film microstructure and formulation-dependent observations without a universal Grade rule.
- The waterborne studies remain identified as system-specific evidence.
- Pigment-level treatment/photoactivity screening is kept separate from formulated-film weathering.
- The Wang durability discussion is limited to the reported epoxy and acrylic-urethane matrices, pigment/dispersion variables and dry versus warm/wet UV conditions.

The Tiarks date is stated at the supported month precision. No unsupported light-source comparison, candidate rank reversal, service-life conversion or universal durability hierarchy remains.
