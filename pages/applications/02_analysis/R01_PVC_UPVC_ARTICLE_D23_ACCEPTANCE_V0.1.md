## 5. Content quality judgment

The article is materially stronger than a generic “TiO2 is used in PVC for whiteness and UV resistance” article. Its useful contribution is the decision structure:

- it separates PVC-U and PVC-P evidence;
- it explains the dual protection/photoactivity problem without turning it into a universal grade ranking;
- it prevents profile, siding, sheet and pipe evidence from being transferred automatically;
- it separates compound/programme status from certification and finished-product approval;
- it distinguishes appearance, surface and retained-property endpoints;
- it converts the explanation into a complete buyer input package.

The article is detailed and technically conservative. Sections 6–8 are intentionally procedure-dense. A06/RQR treated that as a non-blocking reading characteristic because the preceding sections establish the technical meaning first. D23 agrees that no revision is required before presenting the exact article to the user.

## 6. Binding limitations

- The article is an evaluation framework, not a completed qualification or rapid Grade-selection page.
- It contains no buyer formulation, process history, batch, incumbent comparison or trial result.
- It authorizes no named-Grade fit, recommendation, equivalence, recipe, dosage, guarantee, certification, water-contact approval, service-life conversion, universal threshold, quantified performance or savings conclusion.
