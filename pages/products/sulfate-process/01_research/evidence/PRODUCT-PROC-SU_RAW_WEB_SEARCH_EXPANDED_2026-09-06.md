In the **Sulfate Process**, ilmenite ore is dissolved in concentrated sulfuric acid, generating a hydrated TiO₂ intermediate that is filtered, purified, and calcined in rotating kilns at 800–1,000 °C over several hours. This extended thermal profile allows polymorph control — producers can steer output toward either anatase or rutile. The process generates substantial acidic iron-sulfate by-products, creating significant waste-treatment obligations. The **Chloride Process** reacts high-grade TiO₂ feedstocks with chlorine to produce TiCl₄, which is then flash-oxidized in a flame reactor at 900–1,000 °C within milliseconds. This yields almost exclusively rutile-form pigment with a tighter particle size distribution and lower oil absorption. Commercialized by DuPont in the early 1950s, the Chloride route today accounts for approximately 60% of global TiO₂ pigment output.\n\nWhat is titanium dioxide pigment, and why is it considered the world's most important white pigment?\n\n### What is titanium dioxide pigment, and why is it considered the world's most important white pigment?\n\nTitanium dioxide pigment (TiO₂, also known as Pigment White 6, CI 77891, or E171) is a white, crystalline powder derived from titanium-bearing ores. It holds its position as the world's most important white pigment because of its exceptionally high refractive index — the highest of any commercially available white pigment — which produces intense light scattering across the entire visible spectrum without meaningful light absorption. This combination delivers outstanding opacity and brightness in coatings, plastics, and paper at relatively low loadings. No alternative white pigment approaches TiO₂'s hiding power-to-volume efficiency, making it the reference standard against which all other white pigments are measured.\n\nHow does particle size affect the light-scattering efficiency of TiO₂ pigment, and what is the optimal particle diameter for maximum opacity in the vi\n\n--------------------------------------------------------------------------------\nTitanium Dioxide Pigment Manufacturer | Complete 10-Series Portfolio (https://wotaichem.com/titanium-dioxide-pigment-manufacturer/)\nciteturn164search8 [wordlim: 200] Crawled: last month; # Titanium Dioxide Pigment Manufacturer ...
