## Source O1 — Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/4

- Adopted: 17 December 2024.
- Published in the Official Journal: 9 January 2025.
- Entry into force: the day following publication, therefore 10 January 2025.
- Measure: definitive anti-dumping duty on imports of the defined titanium dioxide product originating in the People's Republic of China.
- Product definition: titanium dioxide in all forms, as titanium oxides or pigments and preparations based on titanium dioxide, containing a minimum of 80% by weight of titanium dioxide calculated on dry matter, with all types of particle sizes.
- CAS references stated in the regulation: 12065-65-5 and 13463-67-7.
- CN codes stated: ex 2823 00 00 and ex 3206 11 00.
- TARIC codes stated: 2823 00 00 10, 2823 00 00 30, 3206 11 00 10 and 3206 11 00 30.
- Fixed specific anti-dumping duties:
  - LB Group: EUR 0.74 per kg; additional code 89CB.
  - Anhui Gold Star Group: EUR 0.25 per kg; additional code 89CC.
  - other cooperating companies listed in the Annex: EUR 0.64 per kg, with the applicable Annex code.
  - all other companies: EUR 0.74 per kg; additional code 8999.
- Individual company-group rates depend on presentation of a valid commercial invoice containing the required declaration. If that condition is not met, the rate for all other companies applies.
- Goods imported for use in production of white graphic inks for printing are exempted only subject to the end-use procedure in Article 254 of Regulation (EU) No 952/2013.
- The regulation applies ordinary customs-duty provisions unless it specifies otherwise; this does not identify an ordinary customs tariff rate.

## Source O3 — EUR-Lex Notice C/2026/4533

- Published: 25 August 2026.
- Procedure: reopening of an absorption reinvestigation under Article 12 of Regulation (EU) 2016/1036.
- The notice identifies the definitive measures imposed by Regulation (EU) 2025/4 as the measures currently in force.
- Request lodged: 13 July 2026.
- Product and origin scope: the product under investigation originating in the People's Republic of China, aligned with the current measure's product scope.
- Investigation period: 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
- If the reinvestigation results in an affirmative finding, the duty may be recalculated; the resulting level cannot exceed twice the amount imposed by Regulation (EU) 2025/4.
- Normal procedural target: conclusion within six months and no later than nine months from publication of the notice.
- Gate 1 interpretation: the reopening is active and could affect a future duty level, but the notice itself does not replace the current duty table.

- Measures can remain in force for a limited period and may be reviewed.
