## 3. Page Intent Card

### 3.1 Primary audience

- UK-based industrial TiO2 procurement and sourcing teams evaluating a supplier option.
- Technical, formulation, quality, regulatory and import stakeholders supporting that evaluation.
- Buyers whose destination may be Great Britain or Northern Ireland and therefore need the correct regulatory path identified before relying on a general “UK compliance” statement.

### 3.2 Buyer situation

The buyer has explicit United Kingdom supplier intent but still needs to establish destination territory, application, specification or grade requirements, quantity, packaging needs, document-review needs, and the responsible importing route.

### 3.3 Buyer job to be done

Evaluate TiO2 Malaysia as a titanium dioxide supply option for UK procurement, identify the buyer's industrial application and specification needs, establish whether the destination is Great Britain or Northern Ireland, follow the relevant product, document, regulatory and import-review path, and proceed to an RFQ with enough context for human review.

### 3.4 Proposed core message for Gate 2 development

> Evaluate titanium dioxide supply for United Kingdom industrial applications, then identify whether the destination is Great Britain or Northern Ireland so the relevant product, document, regulatory and import review path can be followed.

This is a Page Intent statement and Gate 2 development direction only, not approved public copy. If a later project-level authority closes the site-wide Malaysia-origin claim hold for cross-page use, the preferred qualified variant is: `Evaluate Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide supply for United Kingdom industrial applications, then identify whether the destination is Great Britain or Northern Ireland so the relevant product, document, regulatory and import review path can be followed.` Until that authority exists, the qualified variant must not render. Neither direction claims UK establishment, local stock, local manufacturing, registration coverage, certification, availability, lead time or tariff outcome.

### 3.5 One-sentence page promise

The page helps UK buyers evaluate a titanium dioxide supply option, explore representative products and applications, identify the correct Great Britain or Northern Ireland review path, request supporting documents, and prepare an RFQ without inventing local UK capability.
