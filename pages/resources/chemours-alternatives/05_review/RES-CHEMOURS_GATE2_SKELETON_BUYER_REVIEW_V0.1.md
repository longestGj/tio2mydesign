## 2. Highest-risk action or workload sentence

> Prepare these four inputs before comparing product pages or discussing an IKHLAS grade with your technical or procurement team.

Trace: buyer → completes all four inputs → before visiting/comparing product pages or discussing a grade → becomes eligible to continue the evaluation.

The word `before` turns a useful checklist into a prerequisite. A buyer may know the current Ti-Pure grade and application but still need product pages to clarify performance criteria or document needs. The sentence can therefore create avoidable work and make an incomplete-information buyer pause unnecessarily.

## 3. Weakest Buyer Copy sentence

The same sentence is also the weakest copy. Besides the forced sequence, `discussing an IKHLAS grade with your technical or procurement team` is awkward because the reader may already be a member of one of those teams. Its intended meaning is understandable, but the buyer must reread it to decide whether the checklist is for browsing, internal discussion or both.

## 4. Page-specific value and incomplete-information path

The page-specific value is visible: the Hero rejects a brand-only answer; `Prepare Your Evaluation Brief` converts the query into evaluation inputs; and `Compare Documented Facts, Then Qualify the Grade` separates documented facts from qualification in the buyer's own system.

- Unknown IKHLAS Grade: the buyer can start at Product Hub using application/process context.
- Several candidate Grades: Product Hub may produce a neutral shortlist, and the copy explicitly says this is not a replacement mapping.
- Several document types: CONV-DOC supports multi-select document types.
- Several candidate Grades needing documents: CONV-DOC has one required Grade selector. The Skeleton uses singular Grade language and does not promise a bulk request; this owner limitation does not require added form explanation on this page.

Products → Documents is therefore understandable once F01 removes the false implication that all four inputs must be complete before the buyer can browse Products.

## 5. Finding, preserve and next responsibility

### `RESCH-G2-SKEL-BR-F01` — MINOR
