## Recommended article direction

**Working title:** Titanium Dioxide in PE and PP Plastics: Which Test Results Transfer—and Which Do Not?

Write one explanation-led APP-PLAS child article for teams evaluating one TiO2 candidate across PE and PP finished-product systems.

**Core question:** When a TiO2 candidate is considered across PE and PP, what does existing evidence actually prove, and what must be re-established in each declared resin, conversion route and finished article?

## Approaches considered

1. **One combined PE/PP evidence-transfer article — recommended.** It creates one distinct buyer decision, uses the strongest comparative evidence and controls overlap.
2. **Two separate PE and PP articles — rejected.** Each original-query sample is fragmented and would force repetition of the Plastics parent while amplifying nanomaterial, photocatalysis and masterbatch noise.
3. **Merge both candidates back into APP-PLAS without a new article — defensible fallback.** It avoids cannibalization, but leaves a useful cross-resin transfer question underexplained.

## Required explanatory spine

`material family and exact resin → additives/TiO2 introduction route → conversion history → finished specimen/geometry → conditioning/exposure → method and endpoint → transfer limit`

The article should distinguish matched comparison within each system from a documented cross-system comparison. It must show that a difference between a PE result and a PP result cannot be attributed to the pigment alone when resin, process, geometry or method also changes.

## Why one merged WRITE can pass

1. It serves a specific cross-portfolio plastics evaluation team.
2. It answers a narrower transferability question not developed in APP-PLAS.
3. PE and PP search intent is natural but too fragmented for two articles; the combined framing absorbs that fragmentation without pretending one dominant query.
4. ISO method scope and comparative polymer studies support a substantive non-transfer explanation.
5. APP-PLAS owns finished-plastic evidence; R02 and APP-MB/R07 boundaries are explicit.
6. The article remains useful without Grade recommendations, recipes, equivalence or guarantees.
