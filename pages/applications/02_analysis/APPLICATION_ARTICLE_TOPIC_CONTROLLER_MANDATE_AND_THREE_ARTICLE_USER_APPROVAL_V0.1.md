4. presenting the proposed topic and material trade-offs to the user for approval;
5. after approval, handing one bounded article assignment at a time to D22;
6. reviewing the returned governed article, binding the exact package and obtaining the user's content approval;
7. keeping article approval separate from page transformation, Gate, development and publication authority.

D22 remains responsible only for governed article research and writing under the approved article assignment. D22 does not select the topic, create page architecture, decide URLs, transform content into HTML or approve publication.
