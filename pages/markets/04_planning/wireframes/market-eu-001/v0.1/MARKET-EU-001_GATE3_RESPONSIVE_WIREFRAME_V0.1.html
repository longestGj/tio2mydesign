Procurement checklist

Build the Review Around Your Import

A useful supplier review starts with the product and transaction, not a generic compliance label.

  1. 01

    Define the application

    State whether the titanium dioxide will be evaluated for coatings, plastics, masterbatch, printing inks or paper. The intended use determines which technical questions and product information matter most.

  2. 02

    Identify grades to review

    Share a known grade when you have one. If the grade is still open, begin with the application and evaluation criteria before narrowing the product review.

  3. 03

    Confirm the importing role

    Identify the EU importing entity and confirm whether an only-representative arrangement is relevant to the substance, supply chain and annual volume.

  4. 04

    Request the review information

    Specify the technical, safety, quality and COA-related, origin, and supplier-qualification information your team needs for its review.

  5. 05

    Check classification and origin

    Verify the current CN or TARIC classification, the substantiated origin of the goods and the measures that apply to the specific transaction.

  6. 06

    Prepare the commercial request

    Provide the destination country, estimated quantity, required timing and packaging requirements so the commercial request can be reviewed in context.

Requirements vary by product, importer role and transaction. Confirm the current position with the responsible EU importer and qualified advisers where needed.

Who carries the EU import responsibilities?

For products imported from outside the EU or EEA, REACH and CLP responsibilities generally sit with the EU importer or, for applicable REACH obligations, an EU-established only representative. The non-EU supplier supports the review with the relevant product and supply-chain information.

Official reference: European Chemicals Agency, REACH and CLP for non-EU companies. Checked 4 September 2026.

EU customs and trade check

Check Current EU Import Requirements

EU customs treatment depends on the product's classification, origin and the measures in force at the time of import. Verify the current TARIC position and applicable official rules for the specific product and transaction.

Current titanium dioxide trade context

Checked 4 September 2026: the EU has definitive anti-dumping measures concerning defined titanium dioxide imports originating in China. On 25 August 2026, the European Commission opened an absorption reinvestigation concerning those measures. The investigation is ongoing, so buyers should not rely on a fixed rate or scope without a current official check.

Measures concerning goods of one origin do not, by themselves, determine the customs treatment of goods of another origin. Product classification, substantiated origin and the measures in force must be assessed together.

Official references

  • Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/4
  • Notice C/2026/4533
  • EU Customs Tariff (TARIC)

Buyer questions

Questions From EU Procurement Teams

Is TiO2 Malaysia an EU-based supplier?

No EU establishment is claimed. This page is for EU buyers evaluating a Malaysia-based titanium dioxide supply option.

Who handles REACH and CLP responsibilities for an EU import?

Responsibility generally sits with the EU importer or, for applicable REACH obligations, an EU-established only representative. Confirm the exact role, substance and volume for the transaction.

What documents can our team request?

Requests can cover technical and product information, safety documentation, quality and COA-related information, and origin and supplier-qualification information. Availability and applicable scope are confirmed during review.

How should we select a grade?

Start with the intended application and evaluation criteria, review the relevant product information, and identify the grade in your request when known.

Do EU measures on Chinese titanium dioxide determine the treatment of Malaysian-origin goods?

No. Treatment must be assessed using the specific product classification, substantiated origin and measures in force for the transaction.

What should we include in a quotation request?

Include the application, grade if known, destination country, estimated quantity, required timing, packaging needs and the information your team wants to review.
