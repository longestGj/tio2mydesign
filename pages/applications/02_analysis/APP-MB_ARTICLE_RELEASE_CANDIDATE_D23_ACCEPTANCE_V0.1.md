## 3. Accepted article scope

Working title:

**Titanium Dioxide for Masterbatch: How to Evaluate Dispersion, Processing Stability, and End-Use Performance**

The candidate contains eight ordered buyer decisions:

1. Define the complete two-stage system.
2. Make the comparison basis explicit.
3. Build a complementary evidence map.
4. Keep evidence inside its application context.
5. Match each document to the job it can do.
6. Use four qualification gates.
7. Compare economics only after equal qualification.
8. Prepare a qualification inquiry or preserve the hold.

The article is accepted because it distinguishes concentrate-making evidence from final let-down/end-use verification, keeps method and application contexts bounded, gives the buyer practical declaration and comparison tools, and avoids presenting finished masterbatch supply, product ranking, grade equivalence, quantified economics, compliance conclusions, or performance guarantees.
