1. Does the approved article answer the registered `titanium dioxide for plastics` intent, and where does it need page-level adaptation?

4. Where must APP-PLAS stop and APP-MB, Grade, Document, Resource or Conversion owners begin?
5. Which TiO2 Malaysia Grades may appear without ranking or performance inference?
6. What evidence and fact controls must Gate 2 inherit?
