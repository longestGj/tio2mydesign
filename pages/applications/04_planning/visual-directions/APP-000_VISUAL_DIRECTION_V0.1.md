## 1. Direction Decision

### Recommended: Industrial Editorial Application Atlas

Extend the approved Industrial Editorial system with an application-atlas character:

- six equal application collections form the page’s strongest visual surface;
- grade relationships appear as restrained editorial chips/rows, not recommendation cards;
- Process is visually separated as a second classification layer;
- a six-segment abstract motif communicates structure without implying factory, inventory, certification or performance;
- Buyer Clean copy remains visibly stronger than decoration.

### Approaches considered

| Direction | Strength | Risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application Atlas | Shows all 25 relationships and keeps six categories equal | Requires disciplined content density | Selected |
| Interactive single-result selector | Compact first view | Hides most of the approved baseline and resembles PRODUCT-000 | Rejected |
| Image-led industry gallery | Immediate category cues | Risks unsupported end-use/factory implication and keyword-rich mini landing pages | Rejected |

## 2. Brand Continuity

Inherit without change:

- Primary Navy `#062B5B` for headings/navigation;
- Deep Navy `#031B3A` for terminal RFQ/footer;
- accessible action Teal `#007F77`;
- Bright Teal `#00A99D` only for decorative accents;
- Soft Background `#F5F8FB`, Border Gray `#D9E2EC`, Body `#334155`, Secondary `#526176`;
- Inter hierarchy, 8px rhythm, restrained 10–14px corner radii and thin rules;
- fixed global Header and visible Home text.

APP-000 differs from PRODUCT-000 through six application collection cards rather than a selector/result workbench or 14-grade directory.

## 5. Hero Direction

- Desktop 7/5 split; Mobile one column.
- Right side: abstract six-category relationship map with six labeled count cells and neutral connecting rules.
- No category photo, coating panel, plastic pellets, paper mill, ink press or factory photograph is needed.
- The map uses counts 7/7/6/3/1/1 as information, not prominence; cell size remains equal.
- Primary CTA is solid Teal `Explore Applications`; RFQ is outlined and subordinate.
- Route-safe Mobile contains only the anchor CTA.

## 6. Application Directory — Primary Visual

### Desktop

- Two-column 3×2 editorial card grid.
- Each card has: eyebrow/count, category title, one-sentence neutral purpose line, complete grade chip list, optional route CTA.
- Grade chips are white or pale neutral with Navy text and Border Gray outline.
- No chip uses stars, checkmarks, heat colors, numbered ranking or differing emphasis.
- M-996 and M-2196 use identical chip treatment.

### Mobile

- Six full-width cards in matrix order.
- Grade chips wrap naturally; no horizontal scroller.
- CTA appears below the list only when qualified; absent CTA leaves no reserved footer.
- Specialty Materials looks equal but uses product-path wording, never a sixth Application-page cue.

### States

- Hover: border becomes Navy/Teal and arrow shifts 2px; no card lift beyond 2px.
- Focus: 2px Navy/Teal ring with 2px offset.
- Route-safe: CTA removed, card remains complete.
- Grade link unavailable: chip becomes plain text without disabled affordance.
- No public warning or empty-result card exists for non-mapped relationships.

## 7. Process Direction

- Separate soft-background band with three equal cards.
- Process cards use vertical rule + complete grade text, not chemical or factory imagery.
- Chloride and Sulfate are structurally identical; Vapor-phase oxidation wraps without shrinking.
- Process heading and definition explicitly prevent application-fit inference.
- M-2377 is not shown and leaves no gap.

## 8. Use Guide and Evaluation Note

- Use Guide: three numbered editorial cards, but numbers indicate sequence only, not grade score.
- Evaluation Note: four compact checks with line icons; no certificate seals, lab-result charts or verified badges.
- Copy density stays readable; these modules support judgment rather than decorate the page.

## 9. Support, Buyer Questions and RFQ

- Support Paths use three neutral cards matching PRODUCT-000 spacing but new APP-000 copy.
- Buyer Questions use one expanded answer and four collapsed rows; plus/minus and `aria-expanded` structure.
- RFQ available: Deep Navy terminal band, Teal solid CTA, qualification note visible.
- RFQ unavailable: entire band and all synchronized RFQ surfaces are 0px.

## 10. Imagery Boundary

Permitted:

- abstract category grid;
- crop-free geometric rules, pigment-inspired circles and structured linework;
- approved logo/icon system;
- simple semantic line icons for application/process/evaluation concepts.

Prohibited:

- unverified plant, laboratory, warehouse, shipping, inventory, certificate or Malaysia-origin proof imagery;
- a finished-use image that implies a verified performance or customer application;
- AI-generated product packaging, test data or factory claims;
- decorative national imagery that competes with buyer information.

## 11. Buyer Clean / Internal Review Separation

Buyer Clean may show only public headings, definitions, exact grade names, neutral process labels and qualified CTAs. The separate review board may annotate:

- 25 verified / 55 no-public / 4 conflict;
- `R-M2377-TDS=OPEN_HARD_GATE`;
- M-996/M-2196 independent `comparison_hold`;
- route-safe 0px closures;
- 390px touch and overflow checks.

Those annotations must never appear in public mockups.

## 12. Responsive Visual Direction

### Desktop 1440px

- Hero 7/5, six application cards 2-column, Use Guide 3-column, Process 3-column, Evaluation 4-column, Support 3-column.
- Page rhythm alternates White and Soft Background; no oversized decorative void.

### Tablet

- 1024px retains 2-column Application cards.
- 768px switches to one column; copy remains 16px minimum.

### Mobile 390px

- 16px outer margin, single-column long page.
- Header `Logo + Menu` or `Logo + Menu + compact RFQ` depending route state.
- Six cards and all grade chips remain visible; no carousel.
- Minimum 44px logical interactive target.
- 24–32px logical bottom space after final CTA, never a blank decorative band.

## 13. Motion

- 150–200ms border/arrow transitions.
- Accordion height follows accessible reduced-motion behavior.
- No auto-rotating application carousel, count animation or parallax.
- Reduced motion removes movement but preserves focus/current states.

## 14. Visual Red Lines

- Do not make Coatings or any high-count category visually dominant.
- Do not turn count into popularity, confidence or score.
- Do not highlight M-996 over M-2196 or vice versa.
- Do not expose M-2377 relationship/freeze state.
- Do not show `NO_PUBLIC_MAPPING` as unavailable, unsuitable or disabled.
- Do not use `Recommended`, `Best`, `Ideal`, `Equivalent`, `Replacement` or performance badges.
- Do not use child-page primary keywords as repeated promotional headings/copy.
- Do not create a sixth Specialty Materials child-page visual cue.
