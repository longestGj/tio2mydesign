- H1 contains the exact primary keyword once: `Titanium Dioxide for Paper`.
- SEO title: `Titanium Dioxide for Paper Evaluation | TiO2 Malaysia`.
- Meta description: `Evaluate titanium dioxide for paper in a defined system. Compare method-matched results, review Product Grades, and prepare document, sample or RFQ details.`
- Primary intent: define a paper-system evaluation, compare method-matched evidence, review current related Grades, then continue to the appropriate request owner.
- Geographic scope is Global. No country inventory, customer, language support, local delivery, customs or market-performance claim is permitted.
- Schema candidates are `WebPage` and `BreadcrumbList` only. Schema must match visible copy and eligible routes; no FAQ, Product, Offer, rating or recommendation markup.
- Canonical remains a candidate while the registered URL is provisional.

## 6. Stable exclusions and conditional behaviour

- No decorative, lightweight, laminate, coated, packaging or other Paper child page or hidden taxonomy.
- No Grade recommendation, ranking, Paper-type fit, process, equivalence, substitution, automatic assignment, performance, availability or commercial promise.
- No formula, dosage, retention target, chemical prescription, process setting, universal threshold, mill result or completed compliance conclusion.
- A buyer may continue with one, several or unknown Grade, incomplete technical conditions and a required or estimated MT quantity.
- Missing target routes remove only the affected link or card and do not create a negative suitability statement or hidden Schema relation.

A new contract version is required before adding a Paper-type child page, new Grade or process relationship, new technical predicate/source, new owner action, new public field, new SEO intent, new Schema type, formulation or setting, performance claim, availability statement or commercial commitment.
