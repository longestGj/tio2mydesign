## Result

The inventory separates five questions that must not be collapsed:

1. Does a source file exist?
2. Is it valid evidence for a defined internal product or governance purpose?
3. Which exact claim and scope can it support?
4. Is the claim approved for public rendering?
5. Is a public or controlled route eligible and ready?

The review found binding governance, approved DOC-000 and CONV-DOC boundary records, eighteen local TDS PDF sources covering the approved fourteen-grade product system with several multiple-version cases, and an active user decision that readable grade-matched TDS files are valid product-page evidence. That decision does not create a public document inventory, prove latest revision, authorize downloads or establish SDS/COA availability.

## TDS findings

- Local TDS evidence exists for all fourteen approved Grade identities, subject to the grade-level evidence decisions already completed by the Product page work.
- M-895, M-886, M-996 and M-2377 have multiple candidate source files. DOC-TDS must not merge, average or silently choose between them.
- The filename for M-210 contains `FDA GRADE`; filename text alone is not public authorization for an FDA claim.
- Source presence supports a neutral statement that buyers should identify the grade and revision when requesting a TDS. It does not support a visible 14-grade availability matrix or download action.

## Evidence gaps

The current internal review did not identify an approved first-party inventory for:

- SDS availability, jurisdiction, language or revision;
- COA availability or lot/order coverage;
- IKHLAS/TiO2 Malaysia REACH registration, legal-actor role or grade coverage;
- product- or shipment-specific COO/Malaysia origin;
- batch/order traceability capability.

These are evidence gaps, not proof that the documents or capabilities do not exist. Affected public claims remain `DO_NOT_RENDER` until the exact evidence and approval exist.
