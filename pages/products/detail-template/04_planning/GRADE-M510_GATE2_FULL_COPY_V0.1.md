### 3.2 Product Positioning

## Where M-510 Fits in Product Evaluation

M-510 is a specially surface-treated rutile titanium dioxide pigment produced by the chloride process. Its multi-application profile supports a coatings-first evaluation path, with additional directions in engineering plastics, masterbatch, profiles and printing inks.

Use M-510's listed features and typical technical data to determine whether it should enter formulation testing for your application. Final grade selection should account for the complete formulation, dispersion and processing conditions, target performance and destination-document requirements.

- Chloride-process rutile pigment
- Zirconia, alumina and organic surface treatment
- Bluish tone and high brightness
- High durability and multifunctional profile
- Coatings-first evaluation with secondary plastics, masterbatch and printing-ink directions

### 3.3 Application Directions

## Application Directions to Evaluate

### Architectural Coatings

Evaluate M-510 in interior and exterior architectural coating formulations where its bluish tone, brightness and rutile pigment profile are relevant to the formulation target.

### Automotive Coatings

Include M-510 in evaluation for automotive OEM and refinishing paint systems. Confirm appearance, compatibility, processing and final performance in the complete coating formulation.

### Other Coating Systems

M-510 may also be evaluated in coil coatings, anticorrosion paints, decorating coatings and powder coatings. Each system requires its own formulation and application testing.

### Engineering Plastics and Masterbatch

For secondary application evaluation, review M-510 in engineering plastics, masterbatch and profile systems under the buyer's actual processing conditions.

### Printing Inks

M-510 may be evaluated in letterpress, offset and intaglio printing inks, with final suitability determined in the buyer's ink formulation and printing process.

### 3.4 Formulation Evaluation

## What to Evaluate in Your Formulation

### Optical and Appearance Evaluation

M-510 is identified by a bluish tone and high brightness. During formulation testing, review color direction, brightness, dispersion and the finished appearance required for your coating, plastic or ink system.

### Durability and Versatility Evaluation

M-510 is identified as a high-durability, multifunctional grade. Evaluate durability, compatibility, processing behavior and final performance under the formulation, substrate, application method and service conditions relevant to your project.

Qualification note:

These evaluation directions describe what to check during buyer testing. They do not guarantee a fixed result in every formulation or application.
