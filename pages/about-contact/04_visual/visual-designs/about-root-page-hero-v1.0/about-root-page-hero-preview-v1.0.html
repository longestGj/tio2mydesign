Who We Are

We are a Malaysia-based rutile titanium dioxide manufacturer and supplier. We supply rutile TiO2 grades for paints and coatings, plastics, masterbatch, printing inks and paper, with export coordination through Port Klang and application-based documentation for international buyers.

We focus on rutile TiO2 grades for paints, coatings, plastics, masterbatch, printing inks and paper applications, with quality control, application-based documentation and export coordination for international buyers.

Our finished products are exported through Port Klang, supported by TDS, SDS, COA, COO and other documents according to customer and destination-market requirements.
