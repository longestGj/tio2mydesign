## 5. SEO and indexing impact

- `NO_PRIMARY_KEYWORD` remains unchanged.
- The copy is intentionally utility-focused and avoids informational ownership.
- `noindex, follow` is a candidate recommendation, not an implementation instruction.
- Any robots, canonical, sitemap or index-status change requires page-registry and project-control approval.
- Title, Meta, H1, visible copy and future Schema use the same controlled-request meaning.

## 6. GEO and Schema impact

The copy creates only the relations needed to explain a controlled request. It does not create a Product-to-document inventory, market-to-version, Grade-to-approval or request-to-delivery relation. Candidate Schema is limited to `WebPage` and `BreadcrumbList`; action and document entities remain omitted until evidence exists.
