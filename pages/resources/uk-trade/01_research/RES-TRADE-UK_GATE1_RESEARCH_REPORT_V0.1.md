## 4. Product, origin and registration facts

AD0086 covers the official written goods definition: rutile titanium oxides, pigments and preparations based on rutile TiO2, containing at least 80% titanium dioxide by weight on dry matter, all particle sizes, originating in China. The case lists commodity codes 28230000 and 32061100 and detailed codes 2823000010, 2823000030, 2823000080, 2823000083, 3206110010, 3206110030, 3206110080 and 3206110085.

Notice 2026/14 uses additional code `8A60` for registered goods and `8A61` for excluded anatase/brookite under the same commodity codes. Registration can preserve the possibility of later liability if a definitive remedy is implemented; it is not itself a duty rate.

## 5. Rate and outcome conclusion

The captured current UK TiO2 sources do not impose a provisional or definitive anti-dumping amount. The page therefore must not publish a rate, say that dumping has been finally found, or convert the indicative timeline into an outcome.

`titanium dioxide import duty uk` should be answered constructively: direct readers to the current UK Trade Tariff and distinguish ordinary tariff/VAT from the separate AD0086 remedy procedure. Gate 1 does not hardcode an ordinary duty or VAT percentage.

## 6. Great Britain and Northern Ireland

HMRC guidance shows that a Northern Ireland entry from outside the EU/UK may require consideration of both UK and EU tariff/remedy treatment and at-risk rules. This makes destination within the UK an essential verification input. A single undifferentiated `UK duty` answer would be unsafe and less useful to the reader.

The page should explain the branch plainly, then send the reader to official tools. It must not decide a transaction or present legal advice.
