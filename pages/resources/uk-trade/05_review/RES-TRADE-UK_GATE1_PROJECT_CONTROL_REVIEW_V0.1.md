## 2. Independent official-source review

Project control independently checked the current TRA active-investigation list, the AD0086 initiation announcement and notice, Trade Remedies Notice 2026/14, the AD0086 indicative timeline, the questionnaire-extension notice, HMRC trade-remedy/origin guidance and the current Northern Ireland duty-path guidance.

The independent review confirms:

- TRA opened AD0086 on 2026-03-03 as an investigation into alleged dumping of the defined rutile titanium dioxide goods originating in China;
- the TRA public file still displayed AD0086 under active investigations, last updated 2026-09-02;
- HMRC began registering the specified goods from 2026-03-27 under Notice 2026/14;
- registration uses additional code `8A60`; the notice uses `8A61` for excluded anatase/brookite under the listed headings;
- registration preserves a possible future liability mechanism, but the registration notice does not impose a current TiO2 anti-dumping amount;
- no current UK TiO2 provisional or definitive rate-imposing notice was found in the checked official notice set;
- the published AD0086 timetable is expressly indicative and non-legally binding;
- non-preferential origin is relevant to UK trade-remedy application, and Northern Ireland entries require a distinct UK/EU tariff and at-risk path rather than an undifferentiated UK answer.
