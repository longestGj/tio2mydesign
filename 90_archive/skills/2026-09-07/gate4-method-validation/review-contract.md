# Independent Gate 4 sample review

Read the raw `input.md`, referenced brand rules and the assigned candidate source/assets. Review only the candidate specified in your task. Do not read controller-plan.md, mutation records, other reviews or the designer report/self-check before saving your initial findings. The task is a synthetic method evaluation, not a real page or Gate approval.

Use the project Gate 4 design criteria and `skills/layout-interaction-verification/SKILL.md` for applicable content, geometry and interaction checks. The candidate brand-applied-visual-design Skill can be read as a method specification; its existence is not evidence of successful use. Use actual input and images rather than a prewritten PASS result. Inspect all three widths and required states. All review artifacts must be saved inside your assigned review directory; candidate and existing reports are read-only.

Independently open the supplied images at readable scale, inspect source and run local interactions as needed. You may render additional evidence under your own directory. Check actual rule application, content and relationships, control states, visual meaning, media branches, responsive geometry and source/export correspondence. Record available and unavailable evidence. State images do not prove live behavior, and local behavior cannot prove production delivery.

Save a concise report with:
- candidate identity, exact review scope and overall disposition;
- actual strengths to preserve and the most important supporting evidence;
- all material findings with stable IDs, location/viewport/state, expected/actual, evidence, impact and acceptance criteria;
- separate suggestions and unverified scope;
- explicit next responsible party.

A disposition is only `REVISE`, `NO_REQUIRED_FINDING_IN_TESTED_SCOPE` or `REVIEW_INPUT_INCOMPLETE`; none is project-control or user Gate approval. Discover through the complete assigned scope before summarizing; do not stop after one issue or force a finding quota.

Use bundled offline browser tooling; no external requests, installations, real submissions, developer-project access or editing shared sources. Return saved absolute report path and findings/limits. On later re-review, preserve prior report and Finding IDs, inspect actual revisions and adjacent regression.
