## Construction decision

The vectors were manually reconstructed with SVG paths, circles and internal gradients. The approved raster direction was not embedded, base64 encoded or referenced. The candidate does not use live text and does not require an external typeface. The simplified centre geography and particle system retain brand recognition but must not be interpreted as corporate, manufacturing or origin evidence.

## Identity continuity

The candidate retains the current approved recognition system:

- TiO₂ Malaysia horizontal lockup;
- Symbol + Wordmark composition;
- Navy, Blue and Malaysia Teal palette;
- globe/material-particle/orbit visual language;
- `MALAYSIA` descriptor below the main wordmark.

Raster-only 3D gloss, micro-particles and photographic texture were simplified to achieve stable production SVG behaviour and small-size legibility. This is a production adaptation of the existing direction, not a new brand direction.
