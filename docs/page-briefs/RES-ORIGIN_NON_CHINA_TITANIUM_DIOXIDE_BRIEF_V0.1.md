### Required buyer outcome

A procurement team should leave with a neutral due-diligence framework for comparing alternative-origin titanium dioxide supply: origin evidence, supplier identity, grade-specific technical documentation, application and process requirements, lot/document controls, and destination-market context.

### Explicit non-responsibilities

- No claim that origin alone proves technical equivalence, application suitability, availability or trade treatment.
- No promise that Malaysia origin avoids, bypasses or is automatically outside a trade measure.
- No named-grade recommendation or row-level PRODUCT V0.3 mapping.
- No M-996/M-2196 difference, ranking, equivalence, replacement or selection rationale.
- No manufacturing, capacity, inventory, price, MOQ, lead-time, logistics, certificate or document-availability claim without approved first-party evidence.
- No current tariff, duty, investigation or regulatory conclusion.
