## What to Evaluate in Your Formulation

### Optical and Film Review

- Gloss target in the complete ink or coating formulation

### Processing and Qualification Review

- Abrasivity direction in the intended dispersion, printing or application equipment
- Compatibility with the full formulation and additive package
