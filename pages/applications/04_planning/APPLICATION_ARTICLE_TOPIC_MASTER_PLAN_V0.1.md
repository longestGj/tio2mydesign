## 2. Content-system model

The Application article programme has three layers:

1. **Primary Application articles** — the five approved pillars: Coatings, Plastics, Masterbatch, Printing Inks and Paper. They are not scheduled for rewriting.
2. **Second-layer Application topics** — narrower material, formulation, production or end-use contexts under one existing Application owner. These are the active research backlog.
3. **New-taxonomy discovery topics** — Specialty Materials and Rubber directions that may be researched only after the existing Application backlog and cannot become pages or public claims without separate Gate 0, keyword, evidence and architecture decisions.

Generic explanations such as opacity, dispersion, undertone, weathering, TDS interpretation, replacement qualification and cost-in-use are not automatically separate Application articles. If their search intent is application-independent, they must be routed to Resources rather than duplicated across Application articles.

## 5. Required research package for every unit

Only one research unit may be active at a time. Each unit must return the following before any writing decision:

1. **Keyword evidence** — natural seed terms, variations, questions, available volume/KD/CPC, database and date; unavailable data remains `Unavailable`.
2. **SERP intent evidence** — first ten accessible organic results, page types, dominant intent and whether the SERP differs from the parent Application.
3. **Buyer decision definition** — reader, operating context, decision to be made, evidence they need and the action after reading.
4. **Content-difference test** — what this topic can explain that the approved parent article does not already answer.
5. **Ownership and cannibalization test** — one owner among Application, Grade, Process, Market and Resource; explicit internal-link relationship.
6. **Evidence-feasibility test** — authoritative technical sources, current product/application relationship authority and unresolved fact gaps.
7. **Claim boundary** — prohibited recommendations, recipes, thresholds, equivalence, lifetime, savings, supply and compliance claims.
8. **Final disposition** — exactly one of `WRITE`, `MERGE_PARENT`, `ROUTE_TO_RESOURCE`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT`.

All online/tool research must follow the keyword charter's raw-evidence-first rule: complete local capture, manifest, hash verification, local parsing and then analysis. A transient search result or tool view cannot be the sole basis for a decision.

## 6. Article admission standard

A topic may receive `WRITE` only when all of the following are satisfied:

- it serves a specific industrial buyer rather than a broad educational audience;
- it answers a decision not already answered by the parent article;
- the search intent is natural and materially coherent;
- enough authoritative evidence exists for a substantive article;
- it has one clear content owner and a controlled relationship to neighboring topics;
- it can be written without unsupported product suitability, grade recommendation, equivalence, formulation recipe or guaranteed outcome;
- it can lead to a credible buyer next step such as document review or a controlled sample evaluation.

Search volume is not a mandatory pass condition when unavailable, but no volume may be invented. Low or unavailable volume must be weighed against commercial specificity, portfolio fit and content usefulness.

## 7. Post-research workflow

For a unit that receives `WRITE`:

1. Controller presents the research conclusion and proposed article direction to the user.
2. User approves, modifies or rejects the direction.
3. Controller freezes one article brief: title direction, reader, core question, required coverage, evidence boundary and prohibited claims.
4. D22 completes one governed article only.
5. Controller reviews the exact returned package and presents the result to the user.
6. User approves the article content.
7. Only after closure does the Controller begin the next research unit.

`MERGE_PARENT` means the findings may enrich an existing approved article later through separate change control. `ROUTE_TO_RESOURCE` means the topic is useful but not Application-owned. `HOLD` preserves a plausible topic with a named missing condition. `REJECT` closes a topic that is irrelevant, unsafe, duplicative or unsupported.
