## 4. Page responsibility and non-responsibility

PRODUCT-000 serves broad product-selection intent for titanium dioxide pigment. It helps a procurement buyer enter the correct downstream evaluation path by application, production-process classification or known Grade identity.

It must:

- connect Home, fourteen registered Grade routes, two Process routes, Applications, Documents, Markets and RFQ through route-aware contracts;
- expose the approved six Application result sets and three Process classifications neutrally;
- provide all fourteen Grade identities, exact directory summaries and stable registered URLs;
- explain an evidence-bounded evaluation method;
- support qualified RFQ without behaving like ecommerce.

It must not:

- take the primary keyword of a Grade, Process, Application, Market, Document or Resource page;
- claim suitability, recommendation rank, superiority, equivalence, replacement, origin, certification, stock, lead time or delivery guarantee;
- convert `NO_PUBLIC_MAPPING` into “not applicable” or any negative technical conclusion;
- create Rubber taxonomy, URL, page, filter or keyword;
- start or define the content of Grade or Process child pages;
- declare an unimplemented route live.
