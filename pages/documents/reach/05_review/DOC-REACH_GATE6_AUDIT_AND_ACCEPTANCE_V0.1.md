2. give a general Direct Answer;
3. distinguish public substance information from supplier/supply-arrangement evidence;
4. identify manufacturer, importer and Only Representative roles;
5. separate EU/EEA, Great Britain and Northern Ireland frameworks;
6. provide a seven-point verification checklist;
7. show four dated official framework sources;
8. explain the four-step controlled document request;
9. answer five buyer questions;
10. route to the EU Market and Document Hub;

The page answers what buyers should verify without saying that IKHLAS, a Grade or a particular transaction is registered. Internal words, evidence-gap status, request enums, query-only copy and the reserved TiO2-specific question are absent from the buyer-visible page.

## 4. Regulatory and evidence review

Fresh official-source review confirms:

- the European Commission describes REACH registration obligations around companies/manufacturers/importers and a central ECHA database;
- Your Europe identifies the registrant as an EEA-established legal person acting as manufacturer, importer or appointed Only Representative;
- HSE states that UK REACH and EU REACH operate independently, UK REACH covers Great Britain and EU REACH continues in Northern Ireland;
- ECHA exposes a public Titanium dioxide substance entry, EC/List no. 236-675-5 and CAS no. 13463-67-7.

These facts support the page's existing general answer and jurisdiction boundary. They do not establish the company-specific statement in EG-006.

### 4.1 Current approved buyer-safe answer

> An EU REACH document review should identify the substance, the relevant legal entity and supply-chain role, the intended EU/EEA market or use, and the source, scope and date of the information reviewed. Public substance-level information does not by itself establish that a specific legal entity or supply arrangement is covered by an applicable registration.

This answer remains approved and does not need revision.

### 4.2 Concrete unresolved company proposition

> Titanium dioxide supplied by IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. is covered by an applicable EU REACH registration for the named legal entity and supply arrangement.

Project-control recommendation: retain the approved general answer unless the user confirms the proposition and its legal-actor/scope basis. This is an optional content expansion decision, not a defect in the current approved page and not a permanent automatic prohibition.
