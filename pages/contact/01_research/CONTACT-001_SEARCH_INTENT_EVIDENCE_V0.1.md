## 4. Supported intent interpretations

### Strongly supported

- CONTACT-001 is a utility/navigation page, not a supplier, product, application or document-information landing page.
- A reader needs to identify the correct path: quotation, sample and documents belong to their dedicated workflows; Contact owns other general company communication.
- The page must make the operating company clear and show only contact values approved for this site and use.

### Supported with limits

- A route-first layout is likely useful because the current first-party site separates quote, documents and sample tasks. This is a source observation and project-architecture fit, not proof of universal user preference.
- A small general-inquiry form could complete the residual task without turning Contact into RFQ/Sample/Documents. Its fields, processor and success/failure semantics remain a page decision and implementation contract, not a search finding.

### Other explanations and counterevidence

- Some branded visitors may expect a direct email or physical location rather than a form.
- Some users may reach Contact while actually seeking a quotation, document or sample; the page must route them without duplicating those workflows.
- The exact searches are too noisy and unquantified to justify a primary keyword, a demand claim or keyword-priority upgrade.
