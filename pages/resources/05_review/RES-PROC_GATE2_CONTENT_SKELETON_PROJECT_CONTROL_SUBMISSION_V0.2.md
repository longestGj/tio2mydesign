## 2. Concrete Content Proposed

### H1

`Chloride vs Sulfate Titanium Dioxide: A Buyer’s Evaluation Guide`

### Hero deck

`Understand what the production route can indicate—and which grade-level evidence still needs to be checked before qualification.`

### Direct answer

> Chloride and sulfate identify two different production routes for titanium dioxide pigment. The production route can influence how the base pigment is formed, but the route label alone is insufficient to establish a grade-level performance conclusion. Opacity, hiding power, undertone, weatherability, dispersion and formulation suitability should be evaluated using current grade-specific evidence and, where relevant, application-specific testing under the buyer’s intended conditions.

### Main article sequence

1. How the routes differ.
2. What route can influence and what the label cannot prove alone.
3. Which grade-level evidence buyers should compare.
4. Evidence-backed application overlap without equivalence.
5. Five-step route-to-grade qualification workflow.
6. Four buyer questions.
7. Sources and review basis.
8. Products-first next step with conditional Process links.
