## 5. Buyer strategy

| Field | Direction |
|---|---|
| Primary task | Define the intended white-ink and printed-result comparison well enough to decide whether a candidate should enter a controlled trial |
| Secondary tasks | Separate opacity, coverage/colour, dispersion, rheology, storage, print preparation and economics; identify neutral Grades; prepare useful document, sample or RFQ context |

| Success signal | The reader can state the incumbent, ink chemistry/vehicle, printing process, substrate, deposited-film basis, drying/cure route, end use, optical and process targets, methods and rejection conditions before requesting a candidate action |
| Failure mode to avoid | A long technical article with no project portfolio/action bridge, or a supplier page that replaces controlled evaluation with recipes, performance thresholds or unsupported Grade recommendations |
