## 5. Reader and buyer problem synthesis

### 5.1 Primary readers

- Paper-producer and converter technical teams defining a replacement or qualification programme.
- Application laboratories and quality teams comparing method-matched finished-paper results.
- Technical procurement teams who need to connect a Paper-system question with candidate Grades, documents, samples and a commercial request.
- Decorative- and lightweight-paper teams, which match the approved article's explicit technical scope.

This audience is a page-direction judgment supported by the approved article, Registry buyer stage and observed page types. It is not a quantified claim about the majority of searchers.

### 5.2 Core decision

The reader needs to decide whether a TiO2 candidate merits controlled further qualification in a declared Paper system. The decision cannot be reduced to a matching TDS value, generic `paper grade` label, intrinsic pigment property, isolated lab result or competitor recommendation.

### 5.3 Inputs the reader should be able to state

- current Grade and reason for considering a candidate;
- Paper type, furnish, construction and relevant basis context;
- wet-end, coating or other buyer-defined addition route;
- material chemistry and process conditions;
- target finished-paper properties and method for each;
- baseline/candidate document, sample and lot identities;
- comparable results, deviations and rejection conditions;
- unresolved technical, process and commercial inputs.
