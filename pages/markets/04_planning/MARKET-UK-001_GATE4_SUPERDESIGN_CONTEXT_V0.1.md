## Design objective

Create a premium international industrial B2B procurement page for United Kingdom buyers. Extend the approved European Union Market `Procurement Editorial` visual language without copying EU-specific content or making the UK page look like a compliance dashboard.

The page must help the buyer:

1. understand the Malaysia-origin supply proposition;
2. move from Applications to representative Grades;
3. distinguish Great Britain from Northern Ireland;
4. prepare product, destination, document and commercial context;
5. check current official UK import sources; and
6. prepare a quotation request.

## Visual direction

- Use Inter, strong Navy typography, accessible Teal actions, White/Soft Background editorial alternation, thin rules and restrained 6–12px radii.
- Hero: supplier-led 7/5 layout with a compact six-node `UK procurement path` rail; the rail is an orientation sequence, not a compliance-completion tracker.
- Direct Answer: quiet White or Soft Background surface with a Teal left rule.
- Applications: five equal text-led cards; no imagery and no application-specific colour coding.
- Representative Grades: two equal category panels with three flat Grade rows each; no badges, stars, rankings or recommendation emphasis.
- GB/NI: two equal but clearly distinct destination panels using Navy/Teal top rules and text labels, never flags or map graphics.
- Procurement checklist: six numbered editorial cards, flat and technical.
- Documents: separate qualification block using restrained document-oriented grouping; no fake PDF thumbnails, seals or certification badges.
- Malaysia Origin: Deep Navy trust band without factory, flag or tourism imagery.
- Trade: source-led editorial block with understated external-link treatment; no tariff visualization.
- FAQ: clean disclosure rows, not boxed cards.
- Final RFQ and Footer: Deep Navy closure with accessible Teal primary action.
