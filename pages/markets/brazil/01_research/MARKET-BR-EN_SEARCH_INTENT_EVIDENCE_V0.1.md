## 2. Traceable observations

### 2.1 Isolated original query

Query: `titanium dioxide supplier brazil`

Market/language supplied to the tool: Brazil subject, English query; generic web search.

Raw: `BR-RAW-001`.

The visible result sample included several distinct content types:

1. Brazil distributor or supplier pages: Intercroma, Dixem, KRONOS Brazil, Sulfibra, Inducolor and Titans Quimica. Their snippets or pages offer product discovery, contact, quotation, catalog, datasheet/literature or supplier-representation paths.
2. Supplier/buyer data platforms: Trademo and Volza. They frame the task as discovering suppliers, buyers, importers, shipment activity and company profiles. Their self-reported shipment counts were not adopted as public market facts.
3. Producer or facility material: Tronox Bahia material. It discusses local production and application uses, but it is evidence about that producer, not about our company.
4. Government or technical background: an MME report and generic technical references.
5. Off-intent or small-quantity retail/cosmetic results: Atika and Biotec, plus generic TiO2 references. These are counterexamples to a purely industrial-procurement interpretation.

The sample therefore supports a meaningful supplier-discovery and industrial-product-evaluation interpretation, but it is mixed. It does not justify saying that all, most or a measured percentage of users are procurement professionals. It also does not establish buying volume, purchasing stage, local stock expectations or a preference for a Malaysian supplier.

### 2.2 Representative page reading

- Intercroma English: a local chemical-distribution page with catalog/product and contact orientation. This supports the expectation that a Brazil supplier page should make product discovery and a contact path visible. Its awards, services and capabilities belong only to Intercroma.
- KRONOS Brazil: a country-specific contact page that connects a Brazil contact with products, datasheets/literature and services. This supports the usefulness of clear country context plus product/document paths; it does not prove that our project has a Brazil office or local sales representative.
- Trademo supplier page: a commercial data-platform page for supplier discovery and trade activity. It supports an importer/supplier research task, but its listings and counts require separate verification and are not suitable as our claims.
- Titans Quimica/Sulfibra/Inducolor visible results: their snippets or pages connect TiO2 with industrial applications, quotation/contact, catalog or named product representation. These observations show plausible evaluation tasks but cannot be transferred as our product facts or local-service capabilities.

The discovery call and direct-open precheck are preserved in `BR-RAW-002`; direct opens are not counted again as additional original-query results.

## 3. Evidence-based intent interpretations

### Interpretation A — supplier discovery and commercial evaluation

Support: the primary sample includes country distributors/suppliers, country contact pages, supplier directories and quotation/contact paths. This is a credible interpretation, not a measured majority. It supports a page that quickly establishes the supplier/product context and gives a clear product and commercial path.

### Interpretation B — product/application evaluation

Support: supplier pages connect TiO2 to application areas and product/catalog or datasheet paths; the application expansion reinforces this task. It supports offering product and application navigation. It does not support Brazil-specific product suitability, rankings or recommendations.

### Interpretation C — importer/trade-context research

Support: the importer expansion returns trade-data platforms and official sources; current MDIC material confirms an active Brazil policy context around a China-origin measure. This supports acknowledging trade context. It does not make detailed policy analysis the Market page's primary role.

### Other reasonable interpretations and counterexamples

- Small-quantity cosmetic or retail purchase appears in the original result sample.
- Generic scientific/technical learning appears.
- A searcher may be researching a local producer rather than an importer.
- Directories may reflect lead-generation SEO rather than the searcher's actual stage.

The evidence therefore supports a mixed supplier/product/trade investigation, not a single proven profession or funnel stage.
