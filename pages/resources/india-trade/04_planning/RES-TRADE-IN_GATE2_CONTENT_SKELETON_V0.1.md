## 2. Reader job

An India buyer, importer, broker or finance team needs a dated answer to four questions: what happened to the 2025 anti-dumping levy; what DGTR recommended on 3 August 2026; whether that recommendation has been verified in force through a Ministry of Finance or CBIC notification; and which shipment facts must be checked before procurement or import planning.

## 4. Scan hierarchy

- The first screen carries the dated answer, the distinction between a DGTR recommendation and an implemented measure, the primary procurement CTA and the official-case secondary link.
- Module 2 uses a six-event timeline from initiation through the 2026 remand final findings.
- Module 3 separates written product/origin scope, indicative tariff items and exclusions.
- Module 4 labels every amount as a DGTR recommendation and uses origin, export and producer columns so the two USD 681 paths cannot collapse into one.
- Module 5 states the future-notification and valid-commercial-invoice conditions without applying them to any buyer.
- Module 6 separates anti-dumping, BCD and IGST and publishes no landed-cost result.
- Module 7 presents a shipment-input checklist without deciding classification, origin, duty or clearance.
- Module 8 states receiver expectations beside each action and finishes with official sources and a freshness line.

## 5. Boundary rules

- The page reports the dated official record and a bounded implementation-notification search; it does not determine legal, customs, classification, origin, tax, duty or clearance outcomes.
- The 2026 table is not displayed as a current payable duty table. A future Ministry of Finance or CBIC notification must be checked on its own terms.
- Exact named producers, country of origin, country of export and any required invoice declaration remain cumulative conditions; the page does not assign a shipment to a row.
- Malaysia-origin supply is not presented as automatic exclusion, preferential treatment, zero duty or any other customs result.
- The page does not duplicate the India Market owner’s commercial procurement argument, the Product owner’s grade exploration or the RFQ owner’s full form.
- It gives no avoidance, relabelling, routing, transshipment or circumvention advice.
