# R02 Buyer Question, Ownership and Claim Boundary V0.1

## Buyer and decision

Primary reader: film fabricators, converters, extrusion technical teams, application laboratories, quality teams and technical procurement teams comparing a pigmentary TiO2 input or TiO2-bearing candidate for a defined white or opaque plastic-film construction.

Core decision: what must be held constant, measured and understood before one candidate can be compared with the incumbent at a declared resin/carrier, film construction, process, thickness and end-use requirement.

After reading, the buyer should be able to prepare a bounded request for current documents and a matched film trial—not select a Grade from generic marketing language.

## Content difference from existing articles

The approved Plastics pillar explains application-level candidate evaluation broadly. R02 adds a film-specific technical relationship: opacity is not a pigment property in isolation; it emerges from pigment optical behaviour, concentration and dispersion over a defined optical path length, while the same dispersion and surface/process interactions may appear as specks, pressure development, lacing or other film-quality effects.

R01 owns rigid/unplasticized PVC profiles, siding, sheet and pipe. It explicitly excludes film depth. R02 may mention PVC/calendered film only to define material/process transfer limits.

The Masterbatch article and future R19 own concentrate manufacture, carrier choice, high loading and masterbatch qualification. R02 owns finished-film evidence at the declared let-down and construction. It may require masterbatch identity and history as inputs but may not teach how to formulate or manufacture the concentrate.

R04 will own general PE-versus-PP material questions. R02 may use defined PE/LLDPE or other film systems as bounded evidence, but must not become an umbrella resin-selection article.

## Scope disposition

- APPART-PLAS-002: `WRITE` as one Plastic Film article.
- APPART-PLAS-003: `MERGE_PARENT` into R02 as a controlled PVC/calendered-film boundary; no standalone article.
- Blown and cast film may be direct contexts.
- PVC/calendered film may demonstrate why polymer, plasticizer/stabilizer and process evidence do not transfer automatically.
- Printing, sealing, lamination, food contact, migration, agriculture, hygiene and packaging compliance may be named as end-use requirements to define, but not claimed or developed without evidence.

## Prohibited claims

- named TiO2 Malaysia Grade recommendation, ranking or film suitability;
- universal TiO2 or masterbatch dosage, let-down ratio, carrier, screw design, temperature, throughput or filtration threshold;
- direct equivalence or guaranteed replacement;
- guaranteed opacity, whiteness, absence of specks/lacing, pressure behaviour, film strength, printability, sealability, migration, service life, savings or downgauging;
- food-contact, packaging, water-contact, regulatory or certification claim without exact current authority;
- extrapolation from a supplier graph, one LLDPE study, one PVC formulation or one thickness to another resin, construction, process or end use;
- treatment, rutile/anatase, chloride/sulfate or particle-size labels treated as sufficient proof of candidate performance.

## Reader-quality constraint

The article must lead with technical explanation and use any trial checklist only as a subordinate reader tool. It must not become a qualification SOP, evidence-classification system, internal gate/owner/route framework or masterbatch request-package specification.
