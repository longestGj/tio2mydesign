## 2. Audience and search task judgment

The best-supported reader is a Brazil procurement, import, customs, compliance, finance or technical-sourcing professional evaluating the effect of a TiO2 trade-remedy measure. The job is to identify what is currently in force, which product and origin are written into the measure, how the specific-duty bands work, what later proceedings may change, and which inputs require official transaction verification.

This is an editorial service judgment supported by the query/source pattern, not a measured demographic claim. Commercial supplier selection belongs to the Brazil Market pages. Grade recommendations, legal advice and avoidance tactics are out of scope.

## 4. Current measure, amounts and duration

The current MDIC record identifies a definitive anti-dumping duty on the specified rutile titanium dioxide product originating in China, commonly classified under NCM 3206.11.10. The four published specific-duty amounts are USD 1,148.72/t; USD 1,159.18/t; USD 1,223.92/t; and USD 1,267.74/t for other companies. The measure is listed through 24 October 2030.

The future page may explain the rate structure, but must not calculate a shipment total. It must not copy a stale named-company list. Resolution 850 is later than the detail page and removes Siegwerk from the Resolution 802 producer/exporter list; Gate 2 should link the current official resolution chain instead of reconstructing an exhaustive list that may change.

## 5. Product, NCM and exclusion boundary

The official investigation/measure concerns rutile TiO2 pigments and preparations containing at least 80% TiO2 by dry weight, in the described processed forms, originating in China. NCM 3206.11.10 is the commonly used classification reference, not a substitute for the written product description.

Two exclusions need clear treatment:

- anatase TiO2 under NCM 2823.00.10 is outside the described rutile measure;
- official MDIC Technical Note No. 786/2026 says the specified Ex-001 under NCM 3206.11.10 is excluded from the anti-dumping measure.

The Ex-001 description is narrow and technical. The page should link or summarise it accurately without implying that all paper-use, all rutile, or all products under that NCM are excluded.

## 6. Public-interest evaluation status

The public-interest evaluation began 27 March 2026. Circular 82 extended its evidence and final-submission phases, with final submissions ending 4 September 2026. As of the 6 September official-source check, the MDIC public-interest page and 2026 DECOM publication index did not show a final decision for TiO2.

Therefore the page must say that the evaluation is active/pending a located final outcome as of the review date. It must not state that the measure was suspended, reduced, maintained or terminated because of the evaluation unless a later official decision is captured.

## 7. Ordinary tariff versus anti-dumping

The phrase `titanium dioxide import duty brazil` can refer to ordinary customs duty or to the additional anti-dumping measure. The page must distinguish them. Gate 1 does not hardcode a general import-tariff or tax percentage and does not combine ordinary duty, anti-dumping, freight, tax or customs value into landed cost.

The official Ex-001 note also concerns a separate ordinary import-tariff reduction. This should only be used to explain the difference between tariff treatment and anti-dumping scope, not to advertise a general zero-duty result.
