## 4. Supported intent interpretations

### Strongly supported

- The query is exact-grade and alternative-oriented rather than a generic Chemours portfolio query.
- A reader may need one of three answers: Chemours' own current R-706 alternative, R-706 factual baseline information, or an external supplier candidate.
- Page content that merely repeats a general “how to evaluate alternatives” checklist would overlap the approved `RES-CHEMOURS` brand-level guide.

### Supported with limits

- An independent page can have distinct value if it converts the exact R-706 baseline into an R-706-specific qualification brief: formulation context, target properties, test criteria and source documents. This is an interpretation of the observed information need, not proof that most searchers want this exact format.
- Commercial/technical-evaluation intent is plausible because direct comparison and supplier pages are present. No verified volume or audience share supports `majority`, `dominant` or quantified demand claims.

### Alternative explanations and counterevidence

- Chemours' own TS-6706 announcement may fully satisfy some searchers, reducing the need for an external-supplier guide.
- Some users may only want the R-706 TDS, a distributor or current availability rather than a qualification method.
- Without an approved IKHLAS comparison package, an independent page could disappoint a reader expecting a named equivalent and become thin if it only repeats `RES-CHEMOURS`.
