## Audit result

DOC-COO has a valid independent informational role. Complete HTML captures from the Royal Malaysian Customs Department and MITI were saved and hashed. The current Customs FAQ supports general definitions of proof/certificate of origin, preferential versus non-preferential forms, supporting-document consistency, verification conditions and the requirement that a Malaysia-origin declaration meet applicable origin criteria. MITI supplies supporting rules-of-origin explanation, but its visible content update is 2015-05-14 and its examples are ATIGA-focused; it is secondary and must always carry its date/scope.

No approved first-party COO, product/shipment origin or batch/order traceability record was identified. Official general guidance does not close those gaps.
