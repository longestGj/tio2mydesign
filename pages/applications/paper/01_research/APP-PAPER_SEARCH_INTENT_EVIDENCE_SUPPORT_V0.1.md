## 6. Supported intent interpretation

A bounded interpretation is:

- **Informational / technical evaluation:** understand how actual paper system, optical method, dispersion/particle grouping, sample identity and finished-paper results affect candidate evaluation.
- **Commercial investigation:** find paper-use TiO2 Grades, inspect documents and move toward a controlled sample or quotation discussion.
- **Method/support intent:** confirm measurement scope, definitions and comparable records.

The appropriate landing-page problem is not “which Grade is best?” It is “what must be defined and compared before a candidate can advance, and where can the reader review the project’s currently approved Paper relationships?”

## 7. Other reasonable explanations and counter-intent

- Some searchers want a standard method for TiO2 content rather than a supplier or Application page.
- Some want preservation, nanoparticles, photocatalysis or paper-art research, outside APP-PAPER's pigment-procurement scope.
- Some want a finished paper product, paper coating formulation or mill troubleshooting rather than raw TiO2 pigment.
- Paper laminate can present materially different requirements from fine, coated, decorative or lightweight paper; a generic page must not flatten these contexts.
- Supplier pages commonly recommend Grades and publish thresholds, but their presence does not make those claims valid for this project.
