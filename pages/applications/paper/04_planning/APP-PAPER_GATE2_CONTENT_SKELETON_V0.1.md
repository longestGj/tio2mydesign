## 4. Stable content boundaries

- Decorative and lightweight paper are the approved technical contexts. The evaluation logic may help frame another declared paper system, but no result or condition transfers automatically between paper types.
- The page contains no formula, dosage, retention target, chemistry prescription, process setting, universal acceptance threshold or mill-performance promise.
- M-350 and M-2377 appear only as current neutral Paper application links. No Chloride, Sulfate, process, suitability, performance, equivalence, substitution or availability statement is inferred.
- Brightness, whiteness, colour and opacity retain their distinct ISO method families; no value conversion or tolerance is supplied.
- A laboratory comparison is a bounded screen. It does not establish mill performance or replacement equivalence.
- One, several and unknown Grade paths remain open. Unknown system details and estimated quantity do not block a request.

## 6. Source plan

The visible source set contains one publisher-hosted papermaking-filler review and six official ISO landing pages. Source 1 is limited to category-level and TiO2 mechanism evidence. Sources 2–7 are limited to their public method scope, status and stated exclusions; no public abstract becomes a full procedure or universal requirement.
