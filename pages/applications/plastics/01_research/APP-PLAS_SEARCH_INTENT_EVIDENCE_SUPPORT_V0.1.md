## 4. Representative page reading

### 4.1 Chemours application and product pages

The accessible text at `https://www.tipure.com/en/applications/plastics` organizes content by resin/application context, including polyolefins, PVC, ABS, PET/PBT and other systems. It links to a Grade selection guide, Contact and SDS. The separate product page states that a plastics Grade depends on resin type, processing conditions and finished performance requirements.

**Intent interpretation:** a substantial part of the commercial result set tries to help a buyer move from generic application understanding to resin/process questions, candidate products and a supplier action. Manufacturer claims apply only to that manufacturer and cannot prove TiO2 Malaysia Grade performance.

### 4.2 SpecialChem selection portal

The accessible result combines overview text, many product records, supplier pages, related documents and sample/support signals.

**Intent interpretation:** some searchers may be comparing suppliers, product data or document availability rather than looking only for a chemistry definition. Portal inventory and supplier claims are not evidence for this project.

### 4.3 TINOX application page

The page combines `Plastics & Masterbatch`, names PVC, polyolefin film, injection moulding and outdoor plastics, lists supplier Grades and offers sample/selection actions.

**Intent interpretation:** external pages often collapse Plastics and Masterbatch. This creates an ownership risk for APP-PLAS and APP-MB; it does not justify copying that combined architecture.

### 4.4 Grade-selection guide

The accessible Chemours guide states that selection depends on desired performance in a specific application, then organizes discussion around resin/end-use systems and product properties.

**Intent interpretation:** application-level evaluation is expected to connect resin, process and finished requirements. Its named Grade recommendations are proprietary to Chemours and cannot be transferred to TiO2 Malaysia.

### 4.5 Direct-powder versus Masterbatch result

The expanded query returned `https://allzonetrading.ca/knowledge/masterbatch/articles/w13-a-001-white-mb-vs-direct-tio2`, which frames the choice between direct TiO2 powder and pre-dispersed white masterbatch and asks for process, polymer, loading, equipment and quality context.

**Intent interpretation:** the introduction route is a real buyer question. The page is a commercial third-party article and its performance/economic claims are not admitted as technical evidence. It is used only to identify the boundary question.

## 5. Supported intent explanations and alternatives

### Supported explanations

1. The cluster includes buyers or technical evaluators seeking application-level guidance, not merely a definition of TiO2.
2. Resin, introduction route, processing conditions, finished-part or film requirements, optical outcome, dispersion and weathering recur as organizing questions in representative pages.
3. Product/Grade navigation, technical-document access, contact and sample paths occur in commercial results, so a useful page should not end as an isolated article.
4. Plastics and Masterbatch overlap in external pages. The project needs an explicit responsibility split rather than silently merging the two registered owners.

### Other explanations and counterevidence

1. Scientific and government results show that the original query also serves environmental, safety and general technical research. The page should not claim that every visitor is ready to buy.
2. Supplier pages often publish direct recommendations and performance superlatives. Their presence shows a commercial pattern, not permission to rank TiO2 Malaysia Grades.
3. Search results contain low-authority and newly published marketing articles with unsupported numbers. These are excluded from technical fact support.
4. No current Semrush metrics or controlled Google-location session were collected. `Search Volume`, `KD`, `CPC`, trend and local rank remain `Unavailable`.
