## 3. New Finding

### `RES-TRADE-UK-G2-BR-F03` — Minor — Required

**Location:** final RFQ paragraph in B and the supporting RFQ receiver row in C.

> Submit your application, required quantity or a positive estimate in metric tonnes, destination and any other context you already know.

**Buyer impact:** `positive estimate` reads like an internal numeric-validation rule rather than natural procurement language. `Submit your application` can also momentarily read as submitting an application form or document rather than describing the product's end use. The buyer can recover the intended meaning from context, so the action path is intact, but the sentence is the weakest copy on the revised page.

**Required revision:** Use direct Buyer Clean wording such as `Tell us your application, required or estimated quantity in metric tonnes, destination and any other context you already know.` Apply the same natural wording in C without changing the underlying positive-numeric validation rule.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- `application` clearly means use/application context;
- `required or estimated quantity in metric tonnes` remains visible;
- internal `positive` validation terminology is not shown in Buyer Copy;
- unknown Grade, team review/contact behavior and the no-customs-result boundary do not regress.
