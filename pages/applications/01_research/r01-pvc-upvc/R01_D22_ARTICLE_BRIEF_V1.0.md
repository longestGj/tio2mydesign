## 2. Working title direction

Titanium Dioxide for PVC and uPVC: How to Compare Candidates for Profiles, Pipes, and Other Defined Vinyl Systems

The exact final title may be refined by the governed workflow if the reader decision and keyword ownership remain unchanged.

## 3. Primary reader

PVC compounders, rigid-PVC profile or pipe producers, extrusion technical teams, application laboratories, quality teams and technical procurement teams evaluating pigmentary TiO2 for a defined PVC system.

## 4. Article decision

Help the reader decide what evidence is needed before a TiO2 candidate can enter a matched PVC qualification.

The article must require the reader to define:

- rigid/unplasticized or flexible/plasticized PVC;
- stabilizer package and relevant formulation context;
- white or coloured finished article;
- profile, pipe, sheet, siding, conductor, film or another defined product;
- process and specimen;
- interior, storage, exterior or other exposure;
- initial and weathered acceptance endpoints;
- incumbent, rejection conditions and any owner-controlled document requirements.

## 5. Required content

1. Explain why a generic plastics-grade label is insufficient for PVC qualification.
2. Distinguish PVC, rigid/uPVC and flexible PVC without treating them as interchangeable.
3. Explain the application-level relationship among UV shielding, pigment photoactivity, surface treatment, colour/gloss retention and chalking.
4. Explain why stabilizer context, pigment treatment and exposure must be assessed together.
5. Treat controlled-chalking and non-chalking behaviour as context-dependent questions to verify, not universal quality rankings.
6. Cover profiles, doors/windows, siding, sheets, pipes and other outdoor rigid-PVC contexts without creating separate mini-articles.
7. Give pipe qualification and substitution its own boundary section.
8. Define a matched evaluation plan using initial optics, processing/dispersion observations and method-bounded weathering/property-retention endpoints.
9. Explain the limits of TDS comparison, supplier claims, accelerated weathering and competitor-equivalent language.
10. End with a concise buyer-input checklist for requesting current documents or discussing a controlled sample trial.

## 6. Scope boundaries

- PVC and uPVC remain one article.
- uPVC is the rigid/unplasticized branch.
- Flexible PVC is a comparison branch, not a second article.
- Outdoor profiles, sheets, siding and pipes are contexts inside the article.
- PVC film and calendered-film depth are excluded and remain for R02.
- PVC masterbatch production and concentrate qualification belong to APP-MB and future R20.
- Generic photochemistry that is not needed for the buyer decision should not displace the Application article.

## 7. Product boundary

Do not name, recommend, rank or imply PVC suitability for any TiO2 Malaysia Grade.

The current project allows eight Grades only as neutral APP-PLAS Grades to Review. That relationship does not establish PVC suitability, equivalence, availability, performance or recommendation. Historic M-200 and M-108 descriptions are research leads only and are not sufficient public proof for this article.

## 8. Prohibited claims

- best, ideal or recommended TiO2 Malaysia Grade for PVC;
- universal dosage, phr, replacement percentage, processing temperature or stabilizer recipe;
- direct equivalence to Chemours, Kronos, Tronox, Venator or another supplier;
- guaranteed lifespan, warranty, colour/gloss/impact retention, warpage prevention, UV outcome or cost saving;
- certification, potable-water acceptance, pipe-standard acceptance or regulatory compliance without exact current Grade evidence;
- conversion of accelerated-weathering hours into outdoor years;
- a claim that chloride or sulfate process alone proves PVC suitability;
- competitor or supplier claims presented as independent proof;
- nanoparticle or photocatalytic research generalized to the site's pigmentary Grades.

## 9. Evidence requirements

D22 must execute its own governed research and evidence process. D23 research is a frozen topic-and-boundary input, not a substitute for D22 A02 evidence production.

Priority source lanes:

- official standards or trade-association technical guidance for pipe qualification;
- peer-reviewed journal research for rigid-PVC/uPVC weathering and property retention;
- first-party producer application material for commercial topic structure and decision-variable discovery;
- the approved APP-PLAS parent article only for the inherited system-first method.

Every quantitative statement, threshold, formulation value and study result must retain source-specific conditions. Unverified supplier claims may inform query discovery but cannot become accepted facts.
