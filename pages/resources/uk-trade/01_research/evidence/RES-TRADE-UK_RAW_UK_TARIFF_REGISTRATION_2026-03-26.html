Registration of imports of Rutile Titanium Dioxide from China - 26 March 2026

Chapters: 28, 32

From:
HM Revenue & Customs
Published
26 March 2026

The Trade Remedies Authority have instructed HMRC to register the importation of Rutile Titanium Dioxide originating from China from the 27 March 2026 for the following commodity codes:

  • 2823000010
  • 2823000030
  • 2823000080
  • 2823000083
  • 3206110010
  • 3206110030
  • 3206110080
  • 3206110085

Further information can be found in Trade Remedies Notice 2026/14.
