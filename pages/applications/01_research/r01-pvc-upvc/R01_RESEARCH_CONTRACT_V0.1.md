## 2. Research question

Determine whether `TiO2 for PVC and uPVC` supports a substantial, commercially relevant and non-duplicative second-layer Application article under `APP-PLAS`, and whether outdoor profiles, sheets, pipes or other rigid-PVC contexts should remain inside one article or require later separation.

The research must not presume that a keyword deserves a page or that an identified buyer problem authorizes a named TiO2 Malaysia grade recommendation.

## 4. Required tests

1. Exact-keyword metrics where actually accessible; otherwise use `Unavailable`.
2. Live SERP evidence for core PVC, uPVC and commercial-supplier queries.
3. Search-intent classification and result-type distribution.
4. Difference from the approved generic Plastics article.
5. Rigid versus flexible PVC and product-context boundaries.
6. APP-PLAS versus APP-MB, grade page, market page and resource ownership.
7. Availability of authoritative evidence for optics, dispersion, processing context, weathering and matched qualification.
8. Whether a useful article can be written without dosage, lifetime, suitability, equivalence, recommendation or guaranteed-performance claims.

## 5. Existing authority and constraints

- The current page-keyword master places `titanium dioxide for pvc` inside the APP-PLAS cluster and records only qualitative keyword evidence.
- The current cluster rule says polymer variants remain on one Plastics page unless later SERP evidence proves separation.
- Current product/application relationship authority permits only approved neutral mappings. It does not authorize a named PVC recommendation.
- Portfolio descriptions and PRD application contexts are research leads, not sufficient public performance evidence.
- The approved APP-PLAS article already covers system definition, finished-system optics, dispersion versus defects, durability boundaries, resin/carrier/specimen context, TDS shortlisting, matched qualification and cost at accepted output. R01 must establish a distinct PVC-specific buyer problem rather than restating that framework.
