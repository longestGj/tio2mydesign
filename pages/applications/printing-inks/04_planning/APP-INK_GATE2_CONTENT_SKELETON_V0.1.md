## 4. Stable content boundaries

- APP-INK owns the Printing Inks main-category evaluation. It does not create flexographic, gravure, offset, screen or inkjet child pages.
- M-350, M-510, M-52 and M-2377 may appear only as current, equal Printing Inks Grade links. M-2377 receives no public process classification on this page.
- The page does not recommend, rank, equate or predict a Grade, finished-ink result, print result, availability, sample, price or timing.
- Research examples retain their exact system, specimen, method and endpoint limits. Coatings evidence is not converted into a Printing Inks conclusion.
- No formula, dispersant choice, dose, loading, milling setting, shelf-life target, print setting or universal acceptance threshold is supplied.
- One, several and unknown Grade paths remain open. Unknown system details and estimated quantity do not block a request.

## 6. Source plan

The visible source set contains one bounded flexographic print study, one bounded aqueous TiO2-dispersion study and four official ISO landing pages. Each source note names the entity or publisher, page type, publication date or edition date and the exact proposition supported. Source notes do not turn a study condition into a Grade claim or universal procedure.
