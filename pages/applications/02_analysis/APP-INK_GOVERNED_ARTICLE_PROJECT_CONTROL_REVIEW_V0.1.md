## 3. D23 content assessment

### Buyer decision and article role — PASS

The article answers one bounded question: whether and how a candidate TiO2 should enter a controlled incumbent-versus-candidate evaluation for a defined white-ink system, printing process, substrate, deposited film and end use. It remains a category-level application guide and does not take over a named-grade page, formulation resource, diagnostic page or country-supplier page.

### Technical decision path — PASS

The reader path is coherent:

1. define the intended print and incumbent reference;
2. interpret opacity in the actual printed white layer;
3. connect achieved dispersion with rheology and storage response;
4. interpret supplier values by specimen, method, units and reference;
5. compare incumbent and candidate without losing the comparison basis;
6. compare economics only on accepted printed output;
7. prepare a bounded document and sample discussion.

The article keeps pigment contribution separate from prepared-ink and printed-specimen performance. It also separates opacity, coverage/pinholes, colour, grind, rheology and storage observations rather than treating them as one universal score.

### Evidence and claim discipline — PASS WITH NARROW SCOPE

The visible article uses two accountable studies and four ISO scope pages. It does not upgrade the aqueous untreated-rutile experiment into finished commercial-ink proof or the reverse-flexo/PET experiment into a pigment-replacement result. It does not invent a universal recipe, threshold, loading, print setting, shelf life, grade suitability, equivalence, saving, compliance conclusion or guarantee.

The final scope is deliberately narrower than the original direction where evidence was insufficient:

- no substantive offset, screen, inkjet or equipment-wear performance branch;
- no finished-laminate conclusion;
- no named TiO2 Malaysia grade or current candidate-document fact;
- no proprietary performance, current commercial term, quantified saving or regulatory conclusion.

These omissions are acceptable. They must not be silently filled during page transformation.

### Buyer Clean and reading quality — PASS WITH PRESENTATION CONTROL

RQR's primary review correctly identified an article-wide procedural voice and dense citation repetition. The allowed A05 correction improved the prose and the restricted re-check returned `READER_READY`.

The Markdown still contains 204 private `APP-INK-A04-ST...`, `APP-INK-A05-ST...` and source trace comments. These comments are not reader-visible when rendered, but they must be stripped from every public page or HTML derivative without changing the approved visible wording. The separate trace package remains the audit source.

### Length and completeness — PASS WITH RECORDED LIMIT

The approximately 2,009 visible words are below the original 2,400–3,400 planning range. The direction explicitly made evidence-supported value more important than meeting a quota. The article supplies a complete bounded decision model and avoids padding unsupported process branches, so D23 does not recommend another D22 writing cycle merely to add words.

### Buyer next action — PASS

The close asks for the incumbent, ink chemistry, process, substrate, deposited-film basis, drying/cure route, end use, optical target, rejection conditions, methods, document questions and sample requirements. It invites a controlled sample/print discussion without promising TiO2 Malaysia testing, formulation, regulatory advice, product fit or response timing.
