## D23 review conclusion approved by the user

The existing article is a sound technical-research draft but is not suitable for direct use as APP-PAPER buyer-visible copy. The revision must preserve its evidence discipline while resolving the following page-readiness defects.

### 1. Remove internal governance language

Remove or rewrite reader-visible statements such as:

- `A public article need not name the grade.`
- `Its current edition and status must be confirmed before publication; this article does not perform that confirmation.`
- `Preparing the package does not contact a supplier, authorize a trial, approve a grade, release a product, or start another production stage.`

The buyer-visible article must not discuss publication control, content-authoring decisions, stage authorization, product release or internal workflow.

### 2. Make the article specifically about TiO2 in paper

The revised article must explain why the evaluation questions matter specifically when replacing a titanium dioxide grade in decorative or lightweight paper. It must not rely on a general filler framework as though that alone proves TiO2-specific performance.

Any new TiO2-specific explanation requires traceable, appropriately scoped technical evidence. Targeted evidence acquisition is permitted only where needed to support the revised explanation; it must not become a new broad research programme.

### 3. Improve the buyer narrative

- Add an early, concise answer to the approved core buyer question.
- Keep brightness, whiteness, colour, opacity, retention, dispersion, identity, laboratory screening and cost-in-use distinctions that help the decision.
- Reduce repetitive caution, procedural phrasing and checklist density.
- Prefer positive decision language: what the buyer should establish, compare and prepare, followed by concise limits where necessary.

### 4. Add a buyer-facing next step

End with a natural, non-promissory action for a paper producer evaluating a replacement: prepare the current grade context, paper type/furnish, addition route, target properties, methods and current difficulties; then request the relevant technical documents and discuss a controlled sample evaluation.

This is article-level buyer guidance. It does not approve a final CTA label, route, form, sample outcome or supplier capability claim for APP-PAPER.

### 5. Preserve existing fact and product boundaries

- Do not invent dose, retention percentage, brightness gain, saving, operating setting, compatibility, batch consistency, mill result or guaranteed outcome.
- Do not turn laboratory screening into equivalence or mill qualification.
- Do not use competitor product material as TiO2 Malaysia evidence.
- Named TiO2 Malaysia grades are not required in this article revision. If D22 proposes them, only the current PRODUCT V0.3 neutral Paper relationships for `M-350` and `M-2377` may be considered, and D23 must decide their page presentation separately.
- `NO_PUBLIC_MAPPING` does not mean unsuitable or not recommended.
