### Evidence fit — PASS

The reader source notes support the key technical boundaries:

1. peer-reviewed pigmentary TiO2 particle-size work supports separating bluish tint direction from maximum tinting strength/scattering;
2. ISO 23900-5 supports method-, equipment-, condition-, polymer- and concentrate-preparation boundaries for FPV comparison and is confirmed current by ISO in 2025;
3. peer-reviewed exterior rigid-PVC work supports the simultaneous UV-screening, photocatalytic, chalking, gloss, colour, loading, stabilizer and climate considerations;
4. peer-reviewed PBT hydrolysis research plus product-family-specific BASF processing guidance support chain-scission/moisture concerns without creating a universal drying rule;
5. NIST supports the need for validated indoor/outdoor correlation before service-life prediction.

Manufacturer material appears only as explicitly bounded PBT product-family handling guidance, not as evidence of TiO2 Malaysia performance or as the article's principal technical authority.
