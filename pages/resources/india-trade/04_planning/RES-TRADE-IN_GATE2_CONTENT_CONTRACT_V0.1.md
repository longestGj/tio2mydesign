## 2. Page responsibility

The page owns a dated, source-linked explanation of the India titanium dioxide anti-dumping proceeding for a buyer. It must answer:

1. the status of the quashed 2025 final findings and levy;
2. the status of DGTR’s 3 August 2026 remand final findings and recommendation;
3. whether a later Ministry of Finance/CBIC implementation notification was located in the current official recheck;
4. the written product/origin scope, exclusions and indicative tariff items;
5. the exact six recommended origin/export/producer paths, including two distinct USD 681 paths;
6. the future-notification and valid-commercial-invoice conditions;
7. the separation of anti-dumping, BCD and IGST; and
8. the shipment facts an import team should prepare.

The page must not determine classification, customs origin, producer identity, invoice compliance, customs value, duty, tax, exemption, clearance or legal outcome for a transaction.
