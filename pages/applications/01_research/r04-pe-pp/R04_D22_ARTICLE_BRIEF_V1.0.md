## 2. Working title direction

**Titanium Dioxide in PE and PP Plastics: Which Test Results Transfer—and Which Do Not?**

The exact title may be refined if the combined scope, reader, owner, cross-resin decision and explanatory centre remain unchanged.

## 3. Primary reader and decision

Plastics compounders, converters, application laboratories, quality teams and technical procurement teams evaluating one pigmentary TiO2 candidate across a portfolio that includes PE and PP finished products.

Help the reader decide which observations can be compared across PE and PP, and which formulation, conversion, specimen, conditioning, exposure, method and endpoint evidence must be rebuilt for each declared system before attributing a difference to TiO2.

The intended next action is a bounded request for current material documents and separately interpretable finished-system evidence—not a generic `PE/PP suitable` conclusion or Grade selection.

## 4. Core question

When a TiO2 candidate is considered across PE and PP, what does existing evidence actually prove, and what must be re-established in each declared resin, conversion route and finished article?

## 5. Required explanatory argument

1. Explain that `PE` and `PP` identify material families rather than one matched test system.
2. Use this explanatory spine: exact resin/material state → additives and TiO2 introduction route → conversion history → finished specimen/geometry → conditioning and exposure → method and endpoint → transfer limit.
3. Explain why resin grade, virgin/recycled/blended state, filler/reinforcement, stabiliser/additive package and TiO2 introduction route must travel with a result.
4. Explain why a PE-specific dispersion result, a PP processing study or a supplier `polyolefin` label cannot automatically prove performance in the other resin or another conversion route.
5. Distinguish pigment dispersion inside the declared material from masterbatch pellet identity, carrier choice and concentrate-manufacturing quality.
6. Separate colour/undertone and opacity, dispersion screens, process response, visible defects, mechanical/functional properties and weathering/durability endpoints.
7. Explain how film, moulded part, fibre and other geometries can change the specimen, optical path, surface, process history and relevant endpoint without attempting to cover every use.
8. Define two valid comparison types: matched candidate/incumbent comparison within one declared system; and documented cross-system comparison whose differences are retained rather than attributed to TiO2 alone.
9. Explain that changing both resin and pigment compares two systems and cannot isolate a pigment effect.
10. End with a concise buyer input list for current documents and separately controlled PE and PP evidence.

## 7. Product and claim boundary

Do not name, recommend, rank or imply PE- or PP-specific suitability for any TiO2 Malaysia Grade. The current generic APP-PLAS mapping does not prove resin-specific fit.

Prohibited:

- treating PE and PP as interchangeable or represented by one universal test system;
- universal TiO2 dosage, let-down ratio, carrier compatibility, resin choice, stabiliser/additive package, screw design, temperature, residence time, throughput, filtration limit or acceptance threshold;
- direct equivalence, drop-in replacement or guaranteed opacity, colour, dispersion, processing, mechanical performance, UV protection, outdoor life, recyclability, food contact, compliance or savings;
- a nanoparticle, photocatalytic, biomedical, environmental-litter or specialist composite study generalized to pigmentary TiO2;
- film evidence generalized to moulded parts, fibres or another geometry;
- a masterbatch TDS, carrier label or supplier `polyolefin` description used as finished-product proof;
- a historical accelerated exposure converted into outdoor years;
- a chloride/sulfate, rutile/anatase, treatment or particle-size label treated as sufficient evidence of final PE or PP performance.

Do not expose internal governance vocabulary, evidence-state machines, Gate/owner/route/handoff mechanics or a package-control architecture. The reader should remember how to interpret PE and PP evidence—not an approval process.
