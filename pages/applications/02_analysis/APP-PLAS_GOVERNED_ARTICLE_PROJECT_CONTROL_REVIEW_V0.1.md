## 3. D23 content assessment

### Buyer decision and article role — PASS

The article answers one coherent application-level decision: whether a candidate deserves a controlled incumbent-versus-candidate trial for a defined resin, material state, introduction route, process, specimen, exposure and rejection condition. It does not take over APP-MB, a named-grade page, a formulation/process page, a diagnostic resource or a country supplier page.

### Technical structure — PASS

The final reader path is coherent:

1. define the application and acceptance basis;
2. interpret opacity in the finished system;
3. separate method-bounded dispersion evidence from film defects and wider causes;
4. keep accelerated weathering separate from universal service-life conversion;
5. qualify resin, carrier, specimen and conditioning context;
6. use TDS data for shortlisting rather than equivalence;
7. compare incumbent and candidate under matched conditions;
8. calculate cost only at the same accepted output;
9. prepare a bounded supplier discussion and controlled sample comparison.

This substantially preserves the approved selection model while replacing unsupported certainty with testable buyer decisions.

The visible prose contains no page URL, CTA destination, module name, SEO field, Schema instruction, publication claim or named TiO2 Malaysia grade. RQR corrected the earlier SOP-heavy cadence and returned `READER_READY_WITH_NOTES`.

Removing hidden comments must not alter the approved reader-visible wording.

### Evidence and claim discipline — PASS WITH RECORDED LIMITS

The article visibly cites standards, studies and bounded technical explanations. These are acceptable evidence-bound omissions, not hidden facts. They must not be silently strengthened during page transformation.
