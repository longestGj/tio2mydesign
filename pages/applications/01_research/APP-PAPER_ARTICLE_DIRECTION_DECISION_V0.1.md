## Approved article direction

The article is for decorative-paper and lightweight-paper producers that already use titanium dioxide and are evaluating a replacement supplier or grade because the current option has performance, consistency, support or supply limitations.

Working title:

> TiO2 for Paper: How to Evaluate a Replacement Grade for Decorative and Lightweight Paper

Core buyer question:

> When replacing an existing titanium dioxide grade for decorative or lightweight paper, how should a paper producer compare candidates without relying on TiO2 content or whiteness figures alone?

Approved narrative:

1. Use replacement evaluation as the main narrative.
2. Use paper-type differences only to clarify that the evaluation context changes.
3. Explain technical metrics only where they help a buyer plan a defensible comparison.
4. Lead the reader toward a laboratory evaluation based on their actual furnish, process and target paper properties.

## Required subject coverage

- Why nominal TiO2 content or a single whiteness value cannot decide replacement suitability.
- Brightness, whiteness, color shade and the distinction between relevant measurement systems.
- Opacity and light-scattering performance in the intended paper construction.
- Retention and the difference between pigment performance and retained pigment performance.
- Water dispersion and compatibility with the buyer's actual wet-end or coating system.
- Batch consistency, technical-document comparison and test-method comparability.
- Cost-in-use and trial outcome rather than purchase price alone.
- A practical laboratory comparison path and the information a supplier needs from the paper producer.

## Evidence and product boundaries

- Industry mechanisms require authoritative technical or primary research sources and explicit operating conditions.
- Do not invent dosage, retention percentage, brightness gain, cost saving, process setting or guaranteed outcome.
- Competitor grades and the separate CR-300/CR-320/CR-330/CR-501/CR-501+ customer discussion are background only. They are not TiO2 Malaysia products and must not be copied or used to prove TiO2 Malaysia capability.
- The current TiO2 Malaysia PRODUCT V0.3 relationship authority permits only neutral Paper mappings for `M-350` and `M-2377`.
- If grades are mentioned, use a neutral `Grades to Review` treatment only. Do not claim recommendation, ranking, equivalence, replacement suitability, paper-performance advantage or selection rationale.
- `NO_PUBLIC_MAPPING` must not be rewritten as not suitable or not recommended.
