## 1. Executive conclusion

MARKET-EU-001 can proceed as a P0 European Union procurement landing page without changing the 57-page registry or keyword master.

The recommended content intent is:

> Help EU buyers evaluate a Malaysia-based titanium dioxide supply option by connecting application and Grade review with importer responsibility, document requests, origin checks, current customs/trade verification and an editable EU-context RFQ.

The page must not imply that TiO2 Malaysia is an EU-established supplier, holds EU stock or warehouses, or can guarantee a tariff, REACH, delivery or document outcome. The commercial keyword remains valid as search intent; page wording carries the entity-location boundary.

The 2026-08-25 EU absorption reinvestigation means trade facts are actively changing. No fixed duty rate or “non-China automatically exempt” message is eligible for evergreen Hero or sales copy. The Market page should explain the check, show source/update context and route detail to the Trade Update owner.

## 8. Time-sensitive trade control

### 8.1 Safe public direction

Buyer-clean direction for Gate 2:

> EU customs treatment depends on the product's classification, origin and the measures in force at the time of import. Buyers and importers should verify the current position in TARIC and the applicable official regulation for their product and transaction.

This is a direction, not approved final copy.

### 8.2 Prohibited directions

- “Avoid EU anti-dumping duties.”
- “Malaysian TiO₂ is duty-free.”
- “Non-China origin is automatically exempt.”
- “The duty is always €X/kg.”
- “Guaranteed compliant with EU rules.”
- Any current outcome of the 2026 reinvestigation before an official decision.

### 8.3 Freshness contract

Trade content must store source URL, official document/date, scope, checked date and next-review status. At Gate 2 approval and again before Gate 10, verify Regulation 2025/4, Notice C/2026/4533 and TARIC. If current status cannot be verified, omit the detailed statement and retain only a neutral verification route.

## 9. Product and application boundary

The V0.3 matrix supports neutral technical relationships across Coatings, Plastics, Masterbatch, Printing Inks, Paper and Specialty Materials. It does not establish EU demand priority, regulatory acceptance, stock or service availability.

Gate 1 decision:

1. Use application categories as buyer entry paths.
2. Do not name a “recommended EU Grade” in the first content version.
3. If Gate 2 needs Grade examples, each example must be traceable to a V0.3 eligible row and worded as “Grades to review”, not “best/recommended for EU”.
4. M-996/M-2196 differentiation and any equivalence/ranking remain frozen.
5. Detailed technical content belongs to Product/Application owners.

## 10. Document and compliance boundary

The page can explain the review purpose of product, safety, quality, origin and supplier information. It cannot claim a universal public file set.

Gate 1 eligible process language:

- identify the Grade and intended use;
- identify the EU importing entity / OR arrangement where relevant;
- request the current product and safety information needed for review;
- confirm origin/classification information for customs assessment;
- verify applicability, revision and language before relying on a document.

Frozen: actual file list, revision date, languages, universal TDS/SDS/COA/COO/REACH availability, approval time and immediate delivery.
