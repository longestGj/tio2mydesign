## 2. Current observations

### Regulation (EU) 2025/4

- EUR-Lex displayed the legal status as **In force** on 2026-09-07.
- The operative product scope remains titanium dioxide in the stated forms and preparations containing at least 80% TiO2 by weight on a dry-matter basis, all particle sizes, originating in the People's Republic of China.
- The listed classification references remain CN `ex 2823 00 00` and `ex 3206 11 00`, with TARIC `2823 00 00 10`, `2823 00 00 30`, `3206 11 00 10` and `3206 11 00 30` in the regulation. The 2026 notice expressly says its CN/TARIC codes are provided for information only.
- The operative fixed-duty table remains EUR 0.74/kg for LB Group (`89CB`), EUR 0.25/kg for Anhui Gold Star Group (`89CC`), EUR 0.64/kg for other cooperating companies listed in the Annex (applicable Annex code) and EUR 0.74/kg for all other PRC-origin imports (`8999`).
- The individual rates remain conditional on the specified valid commercial invoice. Until it is presented, the all-other PRC-origin rate applies.
- Article 2 continues to state a conditional exemption for goods imported for use in production of white graphic inks for printing, subject to the Union Customs Code end-use procedure, particularly Article 254.

### Notice C/2026/4533

- EUR-Lex shows publication on 25 August 2026.
- The notice identifies the measures imposed by Regulation (EU) 2025/4 as the existing measures currently in force.
- It reopens an Article 12 absorption reinvestigation, with an investigation period of 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
- It states that an affirmative result may lead to recalculation and that a resulting duty cannot exceed twice the amount imposed under Regulation (EU) 2025/4. This is a possible future boundary, not a current rate.
- The stated schedule is normally six months and no later than nine months from publication.
- The EUR-Lex relationship search returned no document that mentions C/2026/4533 on 2026-09-07. This is a discovery observation only; it is not proof that no unpublished, unindexed or transaction-specific event exists.

### TARIC and category separation

- The Commission describes TARIC as the integrated EU database for Common Customs Tariff and commercial/agricultural measures, including anti-dumping duties, additional codes and supporting-document codes.
- The Commission says TARIC data are transmitted daily to national administrations.
- The TARIC information page states that national VAT rates are not contained in TARIC.
- The Commission anti-dumping page confirms that anti-dumping duties are paid by EU importers and collected by national customs authorities. It is a general explanation, not the TiO2 rate authority.
