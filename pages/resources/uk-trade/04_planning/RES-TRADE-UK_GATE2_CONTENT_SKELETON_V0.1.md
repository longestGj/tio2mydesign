## 2. Reader job

A UK buyer needs a fast, dated answer to four questions: what AD0086 is doing now; which written goods and origin are in scope; what registration may and may not mean; and which shipment facts must be checked before using the procurement or official-information paths.

## 4. Scan hierarchy

- The first screen carries the dated answer, one-sentence boundary, primary procurement CTA and official-case secondary link.
- Module 2 uses a four-row status table: investigation, registration, current measure check and last reviewed.
- Module 3 separates written product/origin scope, commodity codes and additional codes.
- Module 4 uses a `Registration is not a duty rate` callout and a separate indicative-timeline table.
- Module 5 presents a buyer input checklist without deciding classification, origin or customs treatment.
- Module 6 presents four side-by-side concepts: AD0086, ordinary duty, VAT, and GB/Northern Ireland route.
- Module 7 states receiver expectations beside each link.

## 5. Boundary rules

- The page reports the official investigation and registration position; it does not determine legal, customs, classification, origin or clearance outcomes.
- It does not present Malaysia-origin product supply as automatic exclusion, exemption or zero duty.
- It does not duplicate the UK Market owner’s commercial procurement argument, the Product owner’s grade exploration, or the RFQ owner’s full form.
- It does not give avoidance, relabelling, routing or circumvention advice.
