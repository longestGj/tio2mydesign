Representative visible results, in returned order:

1. Chemours press release: `Chemours Launches Ti-Pure™ TS-6706 ... Ti-Pure™ R-706` — Chemours describes its own TS-6706 as a version/drop-in for its own R-706.
2. Chemours `New and Improved Ti-Pure™ R-104` — manufacturer product-update content, not a third-party substitute.
3. Chemours `Titanium Technologies` — manufacturer portfolio/background page.
4. Chemours `Our Businesses` — company/portfolio page.
5. Chemours German version of TS-6706 press release — duplicate-language manufacturer result.
6. East Materials titanium dioxide page — third-party supplier using broad functional-equivalence language.
7. Chemours coatings guide PDF — manufacturer selection/application material.
8. Wiley paper about non-TiO₂ photonic whitener pigments — material substitution research, not pigment-grade sourcing.
9. Chemours inks brochure titled `A Brighter Alternative` — Chemours product marketing where “alternative” is not a competitor substitution query.
10. Ti-Pure coatings page — manufacturer application/grade-selection material.
11. SEMITECH R-902 page — third-party direct-equivalence claim.
12. LinkedIn Chemours TS-6706 post — social duplicate of manufacturer update.

Observed pattern: mixed intent. The result set combines a Chemours-to-Chemours formulation update, manufacturer portfolio/selection content, third-party grade-substitution claims and non-TiO₂ material substitution. It does not expose one stable, evidenced Chemours-to-IKHLAS mapping.

## Exclusions and limitations

- Third-party claims such as `1:1`, `drop-in`, matching performance or a percentage cost saving are not accepted as technical evidence.
- Chemours' own statement that TS-6706 can be used as a drop-in for R-706 is limited to those two Chemours grades. It cannot be transferred to any IKHLAS grade.
- Semrush and browser-Google were not required for this Gate 1 conclusion. No volume, KD, CPC or trend was observed; all remain `Unavailable`.
