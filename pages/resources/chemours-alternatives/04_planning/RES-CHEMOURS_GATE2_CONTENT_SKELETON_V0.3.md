## 1. Page task and buyer path

**Page task:** Help a technical or procurement buyer turn a Chemours/Ti-Pure reference into a grade-specific evaluation brief without presenting a Chemours-to-IKHLAS product match.

**Buyer path:** Identify the exact current grade and use → prepare the evaluation inputs → review documented IKHLAS product facts → qualify a candidate in the buyer's own system → request documents for a buyer-selected IKHLAS grade.

### 3. Why the Exact Ti-Pure Grade Matters

**Candidate copy**

> Ti-Pure includes different grades for different applications and performance priorities. Before reviewing another supply source, identify the exact grade you use and the job it performs in your formulation or process.

Purpose: prevent the buyer from treating the Chemours brand as one product specification.

### 4. Prepare Your Evaluation Brief

**Candidate introduction**

> Use these four inputs to organize your evaluation. Start with the information you have, then add details as you review relevant product pages.

**Four-part checklist**

1. **Current reference and use** — the exact Ti-Pure grade, application, formulation or system type, and end use.
2. **Process and handling constraints** — the conditions that affect how the pigment is dispersed, processed or incorporated in your system.
3. **Performance and acceptance criteria** — the properties your application depends on, together with the test methods, target values and pass/fail criteria your team uses.
4. **Documents required for review** — the technical, safety, quality or origin documentation your team needs before qualification.

Purpose: give the page unique working value by converting a vague brand-alternative search into a usable evaluation brief.

### 5. Compare Documented Facts, Then Qualify the Grade

**Candidate three-step method**

1. **Build a shortlist.** Use the Product Hub to find IKHLAS grades listed for your application or production process. Treat those relationships as a shortlist for review, not as replacements for your current grade.
2. **Compare product-specific information.** Open the relevant product pages and compare documented properties with the criteria in your evaluation brief.
3. **Validate in your own system.** Run your normal formulation trials and acceptance tests before changing supply.

Purpose: separate documented comparison from the buyer's own performance qualification.
