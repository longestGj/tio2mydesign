## Module order and core candidate copy

### DE-01 — Hero: Germany destination, Malaysia origin

**H1:** `Titanium Dioxide Supplier for Germany`

> Evaluate Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide for an industrial requirement with delivery to Germany. Start with your coatings, plastics or masterbatch application, then review product information or send the quantity and destination context you already know.

- Primary: `Request a Quote` → `/request-a-quote/`
- Secondary: `Explore Products` → `/products/`

### DE-02 — Build application context around German industrial uses

**H2:** `Start with the German Industrial Use You Need to Serve`

Use two dated, bounded German industry references as context rather than as Grade evidence:

- VdL's 2025 German coatings review lists automotive OEM, vehicle-refinish and metal-products coatings among industrial demand fields.
- GKV's 18 February 2026 industry statement says German plastics processors produce packaging, construction products and technical parts, among other products.

Turn those contexts into buyer prompts for coating system/end use, or polymer/processing/end-product information. Do not claim that every Grade fits those sectors.

### DE-03 — Product and application evaluation path

**H2:** `Choose the Right Evaluation Path`

A country or industry label does not select a Grade. Route buyers to the Product Hub and the Coatings, Plastics and Masterbatch application owners. Unknown Grade remains a supported RFQ path through `Not sure / Need help`.

### DE-04 — Documents and sample path

**H2:** `Use Documents and Samples for the Review You Are Planning`

Explain the receiver accurately:

- Request Documents is a controlled request; one Grade must be selected before submission, and availability/applicable scope remains subject to review.
- Request a Sample collects an evaluation request; submission does not confirm sample approval or supply.
- If the Grade is unknown, use Products or RFQ first rather than inventing a document relationship.

### DE-05 — Germany destination brief

**H2:** `Describe the German Destination Beyond a Country Label`

Ask for Germany as destination country, the final receiving city/site, and any known port or handover point. If Hamburg is part of the proposed plan, distinguish port handover from an onward German destination. The Hamburg Port Railway source supports only the existence of onward rail-network infrastructure, not a TiO2 Malaysia route, mode, cost or lead time.

### DE-06 — EU parent and trade-owner handoff

**H2:** `Keep EU-Wide Checks with the EU Owners`

Send importing-role, EU document/compliance and general customs context to `/markets/european-union/`. Send dated anti-dumping detail to `RES-TRADE-EU`. Germany retains application and destination procurement responsibility and does not publish a tariff or customs result.

### DE-07 — Final Germany RFQ

**H2:** `Send a Germany Destination Request`

> On the quotation form, select a Product / Grade or choose `Not sure / Need help`. Tell us your application, required or estimated quantity in metric tonnes, Germany destination and any other context you already know. After submission, our team reviews your requirements and details, then contacts you using the information provided.

Primary: `Request a Quote` → `/request-a-quote/`.
