## 4. GEO Answer Blocks

All answer blocks must be visible, context-complete and consistent with the full copy.

### 4.1 Definition answer

`Non-China titanium dioxide is a procurement term used to describe TiO2 presented as originating outside China. For procurement teams, it is a sourcing category and due-diligence starting point—not proof of verified origin, technical equivalence, application suitability, availability or destination-market treatment.`

### 4.2 Qualification checklist answer

`Before qualifying an alternative-origin source, verify the supplier and its transaction role, product and grade identity, origin evidence and scope, current technical documents and compatible test methods, application conditions, document or lot controls and destination requirements.`

### 4.3 Equivalence answer

`A different origin does not make two titanium dioxide grades equivalent. Equivalence requires defined criteria, compatible evidence and validation under the buyer's intended formulation or processing conditions.`

### 4.4 Evidence-scope answer

`Supplier identity, product identity, origin evidence and order, lot or shipment records answer different questions. Each record should be used only within the product, transaction and time scope it supports.`

### 4.5 Destination answer

`Check titanium dioxide trade or regulatory context using current official sources for the intended destination and verify the exact product, origin, procedure and dates. A general origin description does not establish a current legal outcome.`

### 4.6 Decision answer

`Continue to product review when identity, evidence scope and application criteria are clear. Request specific missing evidence when a gap is identifiable, or hold the decision when the available evidence cannot support qualification.`
