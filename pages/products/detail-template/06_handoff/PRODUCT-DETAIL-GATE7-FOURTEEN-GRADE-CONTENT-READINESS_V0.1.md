## 4. Prohibited completion shortcuts

- Do not copy M-350 paragraphs, decisions, Evaluation items, technical rows, document language, ALT or Schema description to another grade.
- Do not convert PRODUCT V0.3 neutral relationships into performance, ranking, recommendation or formulation-fit copy.
- Do not use PRD role summaries as a substitute for current product technical evidence.
- Do not expose placeholder values, lorem ipsum, empty modules or guessed TDS rows.
- Do not mark a grade `approved_for_publication` because the template or route exists.
- Do not generate mirrored M-996/M-2196 content or implied selection differences.
- Preserve the exact governance state `M996_VS_M2196_DIFFERENTIATION_FROZEN` until a later approved evidence decision replaces it.
- Do not turn M-2377 Rubber evidence into a public taxonomy, badge, link, prefill or Schema value.

## 5. Per-grade completion inputs

Before one of the 13 remaining grades can advance to content/implementation readiness, its task must provide:

1. Approved current TDS and any applicable controlled documents.
2. Evidence-aligned identity, positioning, Applications, technical rows and qualification copy.
3. Unique Title, meta description, H1, visible direct answer and Product JSON-LD description.
4. Approved media/ALT or a safe omission decision.
5. Module-level statuses and administrative evidence ledger entries.
6. Route-key resolution for every visible destination.
7. Independent content, technical, SEO/GEO, compliance and responsive review.

## 6. Hard stop

M-350 is the only approved Gate 5 example. Gate 7 does not create final text, technical data or page lifecycle advancement for the other 13 grades and does not authorize implementation.
