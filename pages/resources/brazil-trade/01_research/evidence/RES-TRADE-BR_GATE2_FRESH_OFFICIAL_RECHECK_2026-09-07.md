## 5. Source-precision decisions for Gate 2

Allowed dated statements:

- “As checked on 7 September 2026, MDIC lists a definitive anti-dumping measure for the specified China-origin rutile TiO2.”
- “The official index lists the measure through 24 October 2030, subject to later official decisions.”
- “Resolution 850 removes Siegwerk from Resolution 802’s named producer/exporter list.”
- “No later final TiO2 public-interest decision was located in the official entry points checked on 7 September.”

Prohibited transformations:

- an exact amount → applicable rate for a shipment without the full origin/entity/scope facts;
- MDIC’s stale long list → current list without Resolution 850;
- Resolution 850 removal → a replacement band or exemption;
- NCM `3206.11.10` → automatic scope coverage;
- Ex-001 → a general paper-use or rutile exclusion;
- public-interest initiation/deadline → suspension, reduction, maintenance or termination;
- Malaysia origin → automatic non-application, preference, exemption or zero duty.
