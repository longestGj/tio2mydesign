## Buyer task

Help an EU procurement, import or compliance reader identify the current official measure, understand what the 2026 proceeding did and did not change, and assemble the facts needed for a shipment-specific assessment without receiving a customs or duty outcome from this page.

## Module order

### 1. Hero — EU Titanium Dioxide Anti-Dumping Duty: Current Measure and 2026 Review

- Give a dated direct answer: Regulation (EU) 2025/4 remains in force as checked on 7 September 2026; Notice C/2026/4533 reopened an absorption reinvestigation and did not itself replace the fixed-duty table.
- Primary CTA: `Review EU Procurement Options` → `/markets/european-union/`.
- Secondary CTA: `Check the Official EU Measure` → official EUR-Lex regulation.

### 2. What Is in Force and What Is Still Under Review

- State publication and effective dates for Regulation 2025/4.
- State the 2026 notice date, investigation period and procedural timetable.
- Keep a possible future recalculation separate from current rates.

### 3. Read the Written Scope and Fixed-Duty Table

- State the regulation's product definition and PRC-origin scope.
- Show CN/TARIC references as information, not a classification decision.
- Show the fixed EUR/kg table with producer category/additional code.
- Keep the valid-invoice condition beside the table.
- Explain the white-graphic-ink provision only in its narrow end-use-procedure context.

### 4. Prepare a Shipment-Specific Assessment

- Checklist: exact product/composition, proposed classification, customs origin evidence, exporting producer, additional code, commercial invoice condition, net mass, import date, end use and EU destination/import procedure.
- Explain that a Malaysia-origin product statement is an input, not a customs or anti-dumping result.
- Direct the buyer to current official sources and their customs/compliance resources for the actual transaction.

### 5. Keep Anti-Dumping, Ordinary Tariff and VAT Separate

- Explain the three categories without supplying ordinary-tariff or VAT percentages.
- Link TARIC as the current EU tariff/trade-measure system.
- State that TARIC does not contain national VAT rates.

### 6. Continue with the Correct Owner

- EU Market page owns supplier/product procurement context.
- Product Hub owns Grade/application exploration.
- RFQ accepts product or `Not sure / Need help`, application, estimated/required MT quantity and destination; it does not determine customs treatment.
- Repeat the official-measure link without creating an avoidance or result promise.
