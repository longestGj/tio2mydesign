## 6. Evidence-backed Fact Set

The detailed inventory is in `RES-TRADE-BR_GATE1_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_V0.1.csv`.

- Brazil's official in-force page lists a definitive anti-dumping measure on the specified China-origin rutile TiO2 product, commonly classified under NCM 3206.11.10.
- The four published specific-duty amounts are USD 1,148.72/t; USD 1,159.18/t; USD 1,223.92/t; and USD 1,267.74/t for other companies.
- The measure is listed through 24 October 2030.
- Resolution 850 is a later authority that removes Siegwerk from the Resolution 802 producer/exporter list.
- The written product scope, not the NCM alone, is required to assess coverage. Anatase and the specified Ex-001 are excluded in the official evidence.
- A public-interest evaluation started 27 March 2026. Circular 82 extended the procedure and the final-submissions phase ended 4 September 2026. No final decision was found in the checked official sources on 6 September 2026.

No shipment-specific duty calculation, automatic Malaysia-origin customs outcome or final public-interest result is in the proposed Gate 1 fact set.
