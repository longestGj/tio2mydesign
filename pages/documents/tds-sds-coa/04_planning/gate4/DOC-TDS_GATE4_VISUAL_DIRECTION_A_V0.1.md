## 2. Direction statement

`Technical Editorial Decision Desk` presents TDS, SDS and COA as three distinct procurement-review tools. It extends the approved Documents Hub `Documentation Editorial Request` language through disciplined typography, navy editorial structure, sparse functional teal and precise document-selection components. It does not repeat the Hub's navigation-card composition and does not resemble a public download library.

## 4. Module treatment

The approved ten-module order and all Buyer Clean copy remain unchanged.

1. **Hero** — white-led Soft Background, left editorial thesis, single primary request CTA, secondary Hub route and a compact three-document decision key.
2. **Direct Answer** — compact Deep Navy band with a two-part question/answer composition.
3. **Document choice** — TDS, SDS and COA use restrained linear document-token cards; the fourth card remains a non-selectable multi-document explainer.
4. **Product Grade context** — split editorial explanation and precise Grade input card; selection does not imply availability.
5. **Comparison** — professional wide-screen table; stacked document cards on mobile.
6. **Request checklist** — quiet Soft Background with functional teal check tokens and no availability badges.
7. **Request process** — four-step editorial timeline with a restrained review notice.
8. **FAQ** — bordered accordion; functional teal disclosure marks; one-open-state proof.
9. **Related paths** — three compact editorial cards for REACH, COO and the Documents Hub.
10. **Final CTA** — Primary Navy band with decorative Malaysia Teal top rule and accessible Functional Teal action.

## 6. Interaction and evidence boundaries

- TDS, SDS and COA remain three independent checkboxes; multi-document guidance is not a fourth checkbox.
- One primary Grade may be selected; additional Grades remain supporting text context.
- All three primary CTAs carry the same editable request context after selection.
- Document requests remain human-reviewed; no availability or delivery confirmation is implied.
- No fake PDF thumbnails, certificate art, public-download badges, factory imagery, stock photography, tourism cues, inventory claims or invented certifications are introduced.
- `ECHA_TIO2_DIRECT_ANSWER_PUBLICATION_BLOCKER` and `SITE_WIDE_CROSS_PAGE_MALAYSIA_ORIGIN_CLAIM_HOLD` remain unchanged and outside this page's visual expansion.
