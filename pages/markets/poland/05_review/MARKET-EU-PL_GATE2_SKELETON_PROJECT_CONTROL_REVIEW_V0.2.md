## 2. Independent conclusion

Project Control read the revised candidate copy directly. The five modules each serve a distinct task: identify the Malaysia-origin industrial raw material, distinguish it from finished white masterbatch, support product comparison, route Grade-specific document requests, and prepare an RFQ for Poland.

The first Buyer Review V0.1 completed the three V0.3 challenge tables but missed two action-expectation problems. Project Control returned them as PC-PL-01/02. A V0.2 and C V0.2 now resolve both:

- PL-03 uses coating system/end use and resin/process/finished-product information as self-guided comparison criteria on Product pages; it no longer asks buyers to `Include` or `State` information where no receiver exists.
- The Meta distinguishes comparing Grades, requesting product documents and submitting a quotation request; it no longer implies that product documents can be reviewed from a public inventory.

Buyer re-review V0.2 verified both Findings closed. Project Control independently confirms the acceptance conditions are met.

## 3. Scope to preserve

- Module order: Hero → Confirm the Material You Need → Prepare Your Product Review → Documents for Product and Supplier Review → Request a Quote for Delivery to Poland.
- Primary `Request a Quote`; secondary `Explore Products`; supporting Documents and EU-owner links.
- Raw TiO2 input versus finished white masterbatch answer.
- Coatings and plastics/masterbatch evaluation context without a Poland-specific Grade recommendation.
- Exact approved sentence: `A Certificate of Origin is available upon request.`
- Unknown Grade, estimated quantity and multiple-Grade context remain usable under current receiver contracts.
- Post-submit expectation is review and contact only.
- No Poland market overview, FAQ, local office, warehouse, stock, logistics, trade, process-advantage or current regulatory module.
