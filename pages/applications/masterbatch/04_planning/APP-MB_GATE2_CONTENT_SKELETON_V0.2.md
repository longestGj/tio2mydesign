## 3. Responsibility and action logic

- APP-MB owns high-concentration masterbatch carrier/formulation/production evaluation and the link between concentrate evidence and receiving-resin/final-specimen trials.
- APP-PLAS owns the broader final-plastic application after the TiO2 introduction route is chosen.
- Product Grade review follows the technical evaluation modules and precedes Documents, Sample and RFQ actions.
- One, several and unknown-Grade paths remain usable. Unknown technical inputs are stated rather than guessed; estimated quantity is accepted by RFQ.
- Grade pages own model facts. Documents, Sample and RFQ own their request forms, receivers and outcomes.

## 4. Content controls

- Preserve the approved article's two-stage system, evidence-role distinctions, document roles, bounded evaluation sequence, accepted-endpoint economics and four public sources.
- Do not reproduce the article's private statement comments.
- Do not prescribe a carrier, loading, formulation, process setting, pass threshold or universal protocol.
- Do not render internal Gate, evidence-status, readiness or SOP language; do not recommend, rank, equate or promise the performance or availability of a Grade.
- Schema candidates: `WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList`; conditional `ItemList` only when the same seven Grade links are visibly eligible.
