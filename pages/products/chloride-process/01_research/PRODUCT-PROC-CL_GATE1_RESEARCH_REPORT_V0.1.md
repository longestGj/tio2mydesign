## 4. Evidence-backed technical scope

The process may be explained at a high level using institutional sources: titanium-bearing feedstock is chlorinated to form TiCl4; the intermediate is purified and oxidized to form TiO2; downstream treatment produces differentiated products.

This supports only a neutral category definition. It does not establish:

- IKHLAS plant ownership, manufacturing steps, feedstock, production capacity or environmental performance;
- universal purity, whiteness, opacity, durability, dispersion, efficiency or quality superiority;
- a performance conclusion for any of the eight Grades;
- a comparison with Sulfate.

The BREF and manufacturer technical guide both show that downstream treatment and application-specific assessment matter. The appropriate buyer conclusion is therefore that process classification is a useful starting point, not a complete purchasing decision.
