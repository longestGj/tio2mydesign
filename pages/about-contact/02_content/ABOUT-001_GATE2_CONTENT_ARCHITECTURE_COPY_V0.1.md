## 1. 页面定位与设计判断

### 1.1 核心任务

用可见、可归因且有更新时间的事实，说明 TiO2 Malaysia 与 `IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD.` 的关系、Malaysia manufacturing/origin 定位、国际市场服务范围、产品与文件支持以及出口协调能力，降低国际采购商首次评估供应来源时的不确定性。

### 1.2 明确不采用

- 不做企业历史、办公室文化、创始人故事或 Mission / Vision / Values 长篇介绍。
- 不使用工厂、生产线、仓库、装柜、证书、团队、港口或泛工业图库照片。
- 不使用生成图、工厂图标、认证徽章、国旗墙或旅游式 Malaysia 视觉。
- 不发布注册号、公司状态、成立日期、法定业务性质或 registered address。
- 不发布 ISO、认证 QMS 或 batch traceability claim。
- 不在 About 管理 TDS/SDS/COA/COO/REACH 文件申请；只说明支持关系并导向 Documents owner。
- 不用 broad supplier、price、lead time 或 quotation 文案抢 Home、Market 或 RFQ 的页面职责。

### 1.3 对用户原始方向的三项校正

1. H1 使用 `malaysia titanium dioxide manufacturer` 的 owner 语义，避免 About 把 primary keyword 让给 Home，也避免仅写泛化 supply proposition。
2. Hero 不使用工业、港口或包装照片；采用排版、Malaysia 节点和四个目标市场的轻量信息图。该图只表示项目服务市场关系，不作为物流路线或出货证明。
3. Documentation 模块可以写 COA availability where applicable，但不使用 “batch documentation” 或流程图暗示完整批次追溯系统。

## 6. Entity and Schema Contract

### 6.1 Visible entity relationship

`TiO2 Malaysia` is the customer-facing brand/platform. `IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD.` is the operating company named visibly on the page. The page states this relationship in the Hero and Company Facts; Schema may not introduce a stronger legal, ownership or certification relationship than the visible copy.

### 6.2 Candidate Schema graph for later approval

- `AboutPage`
  - `name`: About TiO2 Malaysia
  - `url`: `https://tio2malaysia.com/about/`
  - `dateModified`: actual approved content update date at publication
  - `about`: Brand and Organization nodes that are also visible on the page
- `Brand`
  - `name`: TiO2 Malaysia
  - `url`: approved production URL only
  - `logo`: approved shared Logo only
- `Organization`
  - `name`: IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD.
  - `brand`: TiO2 Malaysia Brand node
  - `location`: a `Place` visibly labelled `Taiping Manufacturing Site`
  - `areaServed`: European Union, United Kingdom, India and Brazil only because these are visible priority markets
  - `knowsAbout`: rutile titanium dioxide and the visible application categories

### 6.3 Schema fields that remain prohibited

`legalName` as an independently verified registry field, `taxID`, registration number, `foundingDate`, registered address, employee count, ISO/certification, award, batch-traceability claims, production capacity, guaranteed export destinations, guaranteed document availability and any `sameAs` not separately approved.

The Taiping address may appear only as the visible manufacturing-site `location`, not as registered office or visitor address. `More than 35,000 metric tons supplied annually` remains a visible company-reported metric and is not converted into Schema production capacity.
