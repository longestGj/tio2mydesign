## 2. Required findings

### PC-PL-01 — PL-03 instructions have no receiving action

- **Location / current copy:** `Include the properties your team needs to evaluate for that formulation and process.` and `State whether you are evaluating TiO2 directly in a plastic product or as an input for masterbatch production.`
- **Likely buyer interpretation:** the buyer is being asked to enter or communicate this information now.
- **Impact:** PL-03 only links to `Explore Products`; the Products catalogue does not receive these statements. The instructions therefore create an unbound task instead of showing how the information helps the buyer review grades.
- **Severity:** `IMPORTANT`.
- **Required revision:** rewrite the two application prompts as criteria the buyer can use while comparing product information. Do not add a form, new module or Grade recommendation.
- **Acceptance condition:** every imperative in PL-03 either describes an action the current target supports or clearly functions as self-guided evaluation rather than information submission.

### PC-PL-02 — Meta implies document review rather than document request

- **Location / current copy:** Meta Description: `Explore Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide for coatings, plastics and masterbatch production in Poland. Review grades and documents, then request a quote.`
- **Likely search-user interpretation:** product documents can be reviewed from this page or a linked public inventory.
- **Impact:** the approved experience provides a Document Hub and a Grade-specific document request; it does not establish public document availability or direct review access.
- **Severity:** `IMPORTANT`.
- **Required revision:** replace `Review grades and documents` with wording that distinguishes comparing grades from requesting product documents.
- **Acceptance condition:** the Meta describes the Products, document-request and RFQ paths without implying a public document inventory or availability.
