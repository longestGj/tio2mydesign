## 2. Page responsibility

The page owns a dated, source-linked explanation of Brazil’s titanium dioxide anti-dumping measure for a buyer. It must answer:

1. the current definitive-measure status and listed duration;
2. the historical investigation/provisional stages and current Resolution 802 measure;
3. the written rutile-product, China-origin, NCM and exclusion boundaries;
4. the four specific USD/t bands and exact producer/exporter conditions;
5. the effect and limit of Resolution 850’s Siegwerk removal;
6. the status of the separate public-interest evaluation;
7. the separation of anti-dumping, ordinary import duty and import taxes; and
8. the shipment facts an import team should prepare.

The page must not determine classification, customs origin, producer/exporter identity, exclusion eligibility, customs value, duty, tax, landed cost, clearance or legal outcome for a transaction.
