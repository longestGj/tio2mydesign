## 3. Visual character

The direction is `Professional / Reliable / Technical / Clean / Malaysia-origin B2B` with restrained legal-document presentation:

- white-led long-form reading surfaces;
- Deep Navy identity and policy Hero;
- Primary Navy headings and table headers;
- Malaysia Teal for decorative/large graphical emphasis and verified use on Deep Navy;
- Accessible Functional Teal for filled CTAs, links, focus indicators and interactive boundaries on white/light surfaces;
- Soft Background for quiet separation rather than large gray blocks;
- no decorative industrial or factory photography on legal pages;
- subtle orbit/particle geometry may appear only as a restrained non-factual Hero motif.

## 5. Typography and reading density

- Inter is the required family; system sans-serif is the technical fallback.
- Desktop policy H1: 52–58px / 700; Mobile: 34–38px / 700.
- Desktop H2: 34–40px / 700; Mobile: 26–30px / 700.
- Body: 16–18px with 1.55–1.7 line height; supporting text: 14–16px.
- Main reading measure should remain approximately 760–880px on Desktop.
- Long paragraphs use natural spacing; important entity/contact/retention information may use restrained Soft Background callouts.
