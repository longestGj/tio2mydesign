Positioning

Where M-510 Fits in Product Evaluation

M-510 is a specially surface-treated rutile titanium dioxide pigment produced by the chloride process. Its multi-application profile supports a coatings-first evaluation path, with additional directions in engineering plastics, masterbatch, profiles and printing inks.

Chloride-process rutile pigment
Zirconia, alumina and organic treatment
Bluish tone and high brightness
High durability
Multifunctional profile
Applications

Application Directions to Evaluate

Coatings remain the primary page narrative. Plastics, masterbatch and printing inks are secondary evaluation paths.

Architectural Coatings

Interior and exterior architectural coating formulations where color direction, brightness and rutile pigment performance are relevant.

Automotive Coatings

Automotive OEM and refinishing paint systems, subject to complete formulation and application testing.

Other Coating Systems

Coil coatings, anticorrosion paints, decorating coatings and powder coatings.

Engineering Plastics and Masterbatch

Engineering plastics, masterbatch and profiles under the buyer's actual processing conditions.

Printing Inks

Letterpress, offset and intaglio printing inks, with final suitability determined in the buyer's formulation and printing process.

Formulation Evaluation

What to Evaluate in Your Formulation

Optical and Appearance Evaluation

Review color direction, brightness, dispersion and finished appearance in the buyer's coating, plastic or ink system.

Durability and Versatility Evaluation

Review durability, compatibility, processing behavior and final performance under relevant formulation and service conditions.

These evaluation directions describe what to check during buyer testing. They do not guarantee a fixed result in every formulation or application.
