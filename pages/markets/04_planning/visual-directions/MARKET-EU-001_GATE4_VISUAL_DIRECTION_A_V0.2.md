## 1. Direction statement

The European Union page uses a procurement-oriented industrial editorial system. Its visual signature is the supplier-to-procurement rail, followed by clear transitions among application evaluation, representative Grades, document requests, importing responsibilities, origin, customs/trade context, destinations and conversion.

The page must not resemble a tourism page, promotional country directory, compliance dashboard, warehouse locator or logistics-coverage map.

## 3. Page-specific component direction

### 3.1 Hero and supplier-to-procurement rail

- Preserve the approved 7/5 Desktop split.
- Use a vertical six-node rail with numbered circles, a fine connecting rule and Soft Background surface.
- The rail communicates a review sequence only. It does not imply logistics coverage, certification, guaranteed delivery or a completed compliance process.
- Tablet and Mobile stack the rail after Hero content without reordering its steps.

### 3.2 Direct answer and procurement steps

- Direct answer uses a Teal left rule on a quiet surface.
- The six procurement steps use consistent Navy headings, Teal numbering and thin borders.
- Cards remain flat and technical; no success icons, illustrations or decorative status graphics are introduced.

### 3.3 Applications

- Five Application cards remain text-led, with equal neutral treatment and Teal inline links.
- No Application receives its own colour theme, marketing illustration or implied priority.

### 3.4 Representative Grades

- Desktop uses two equal category panels: Coatings and Plastics & Masterbatch.
- Each panel contains three white Grade cards on a Soft Background surface.
- Grade name is the primary label; approved short positioning is secondary; `View Grade` is the only card action.
- The two groups remain equal weight. No Grade is ranked, recommended or decorated as best/premium/top.
- Mobile stacks Coatings before Plastics & Masterbatch and preserves full card readability.
- The single `View All Titanium Dioxide Grades` CTA follows both groups.

### 3.5 Documents and EU importing responsibilities

- Documents remains a separate Soft Background section with a quiet 2×2 information grid on Desktop.
- `Request Documents` is the Teal primary action; the process link is Navy outline.
- EU importing responsibilities remain in the following White section, using a 7/5 content/checklist relationship on Desktop.
- The checklist surface supports procurement review but must not resemble a certificate, approval badge or compliance guarantee.
- Official ECHA source text remains secondary and linked.

### 3.6 Manufacturing, origin, customs and trade

- Manufacturing and origin use a Deep Navy trust band with one primary and one supporting action.
- No factory photograph, Malaysia flag, certification seal or provenance icon is added.
- Customs guidance and dated trade context remain visually separated by independent editorial rules and surfaces.
- Official references occupy a dedicated compact source block; no tariff rate or duty conclusion is visualised.

### 3.7 Destinations, FAQ and terminal conversion

- Six destinations use flat bordered navigation rows, not a map, flag grid or availability matrix.
- FAQ remains an eight-row disclosure system. Expanded examples prove copy rhythm without turning answers into cards.
- Final conversion and Footer use Deep Navy. Quote remains the Teal primary action; Documents is outline; Sample is supporting text.

## 4. Media rule

Gate 4 remains image-free. No generated or stock image is required. Prohibited visual content includes maps, flags, globes, ports, shipping routes, office pins, warehouse markers, stock indicators, coverage regions, factory photographs and certification badges.

- Mobile Header remains `Logo | RFQ | Menu`.
- Links and controls retain visible focus rings and minimum 44px action height.
- Mobile Menu-open continuity passes, but the complete hover/focus/keyboard/disclosure state contract remains Gate 5 work.
