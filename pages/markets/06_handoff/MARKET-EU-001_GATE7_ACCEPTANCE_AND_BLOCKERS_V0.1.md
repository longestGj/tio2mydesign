## 5. Release blockers

Release/indexing is blocked by any of the following:

- `EU-G6-R01` remains open while origin relationships appear in visible or machine-readable output;
- time-sensitive trade text lacks a current official source/scope/date review;
- a required visible internal route is broken, cross-scope or non-canonical;
- contextual RFQ, Documents or Sample behavior is unavailable, misleading or not privacy-consistent;
- Canonical, robots, sitemap, social metadata or Schema diverges from the approved/evidence-filtered projection;
- a hidden Grade, FAQ Schema, EU establishment, warehouse, stock, registration, customs result or unsupported document promise appears;
- Cookie Settings/CMP behavior or consent defaults diverge from the shared approved contract;
- keyboard, focus, Mobile Menu, disclosure, 200% zoom or 390px usability fails;
- any content, menu, media, metadata, form or cache fallback crosses `site_scope`.
