## How to Use This Application Hub

1. **Choose your application.** Start with the category that matches what you make. Review more than one path if your work spans several applications.
2. **Compare technical information.** Use current technical data to compare the grades under review against your requirements.
3. **Validate in your own system.** Check the grade in your formulation and process before making a specification decision.
