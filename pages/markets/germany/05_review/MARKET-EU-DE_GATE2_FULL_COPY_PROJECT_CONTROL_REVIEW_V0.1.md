## 2. Buyer-page assessment

1. establish Malaysia origin and Germany destination;
2. turn bounded German coatings and plastics contexts into usable application inputs;
3. route neutral Grade evaluation;
4. separate Documents and Sample request behavior;
5. collect Germany destination detail without inventing a route;
6. hand EU-wide compliance and dated trade detail to their owners;
7. complete the Germany RFQ path.

The visible page answers the Gate 1 buyer questions without becoming an EU compliance article or a country-name substitution page. VdL, GKV and Hamburg context is used to help buyers describe the industrial use and destination. It does not establish a customer, qualification, Grade fit, warehouse, route or delivery capability.

The incomplete-information path is usable. A buyer may continue with an unknown Grade through `Not sure / Need help`, provide an estimated quantity, omit a city/port not yet known, and still reach RFQ. The Documents path accurately requires a known Grade and permits the receiving owner to control document types and availability. Sample and RFQ submission text promises only review and contact.

The wording revision from `finished use` to `end use` and `intended end use` resolves the only Buyer Review issue while preserving the distinct `finished product` meaning for Plastics.
