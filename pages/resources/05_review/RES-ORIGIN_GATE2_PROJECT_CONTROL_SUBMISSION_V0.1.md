### Direct answer

`Non-China titanium dioxide describes TiO2 sourced with an origin outside China. For procurement teams, the term identifies a supply-source category; it does not establish how a grade will perform, whether it is available or how authorities will treat a particular import. Those questions require separate evidence for the supplier, product, transaction, application and destination.`

### Technical safeguard

`A side-by-side table organizes evidence. It does not prove identical performance, equivalence or interchangeability.`

### Qualification safeguard

`A hold is an evidence decision, not a statement that a material is technically unsuitable.`
