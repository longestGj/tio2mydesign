### 3.2 Decision the page should enable

After reading, a procurement team should be able to:

1. treat origin as one evaluation dimension rather than a technical or legal conclusion;
2. identify the evidence needed to verify supplier, product, origin and transaction context;
3. compare technical documents and test methods without assuming equivalence;
4. define application and processing validation before qualification;
5. route destination-specific, product, application and document questions to their authoritative owner pages; and
6. decide whether to continue to product review, request missing evidence, or begin a scoped RFQ.

The page does not select a grade, approve a supplier, validate a formulation, or determine customs/trade treatment.

## 4. Proposed H1 and Core Definition

### H1

`Non-China Titanium Dioxide: A Procurement Evaluation Guide`

### Direct definition direction

> Non-China titanium dioxide describes titanium dioxide sourced with an origin outside China. In procurement, that label is a starting point for due diligence—not proof of technical equivalence, application suitability, supply availability or tariff and regulatory treatment.

### Why this direction

- It contains the approved primary keyword naturally without turning the page into a supplier landing page.
- It frames the topic as evaluation, not avoidance or automatic advantage.
- It gives an answer suitable for visible text and GEO extraction while preserving the owner-page boundaries.
- It does not claim that TiO2 Malaysia, IKHLAS, any product, grade or shipment has Malaysia origin.
