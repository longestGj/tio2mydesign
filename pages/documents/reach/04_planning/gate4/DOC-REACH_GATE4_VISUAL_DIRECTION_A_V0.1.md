## 2. Direction statement

`Regulatory Evidence Ledger` presents REACH review as a disciplined evidence-checking task. The page uses visible source rails, jurisdiction markers, legal-role distinctions, dual-date source treatment and restrained editorial depth to help a buyer separate public substance information from supplier-specific coverage. It is intentionally distinct from DOC-TDS `Technical Editorial Decision Desk` and does not resemble an official regulator site, certificate, compliance badge or public download library.

## 4. Module treatment

The approved eleven-module order and all Buyer Clean copy remain unchanged.

1. **Hero** — subtle ledger grid, clear EU/EEA–GB–NI scope orientation, one primary request action and one Documents Hub route.
2. **General Direct Answer** — Deep Navy evidence band placed before detailed legal guidance.
3. **Substance information vs supplier coverage** — paired evidence panels plus a full-width Grade context panel; no registration inference.
4. **Legal actors** — three cards with restrained role rails for manufacturer, importer and Only Representative.
5. **Regulatory scope** — three jurisdiction cards with distinct top bars so EU/EEA, Great Britain and Northern Ireland are not conflated.
6. **Buyer verification checklist** — seven numbered checks using circular evidence markers rather than compliance badges.
7. **Official sources** — four ledger rows with source, scope and date columns; `Source updated` remains optional while `Reviewed` remains visible.
8. **Request process** — four-step review sequence and one explicit `REACH Documentation` request selection.
9. **Buyer questions** — bordered FAQ with a visible focus/expanded-state pattern.
10. **Related paths** — compact routes to the EU Market page and Documents Hub.
11. **Final CTA** — Deep Navy/teal close with request and Hub choices; no availability implication.

## 6. Interaction and evidence boundaries

- The TiO2-specific registration question and answer remain unrendered while `ECHA_TIO2_DIRECT_ANSWER_PUBLICATION_BLOCKER=OPEN`.
- No company registration, named importer/Only Representative, registration number, tonnage band, Grade coverage or unqualified compliance claim is added.
- Source styling indicates evidence organisation, not endorsement, certification or regulator ownership.
- EU/EEA, Great Britain and Northern Ireland remain separate decisions at every viewport.
- If the request route is unavailable, every visible request action and its structured-data relationship must be absent atomically; no disabled action or automatic Contact fallback is shown.
- No public-download iconography, fake document thumbnails, certificate seals, flags, factory imagery, stock photography or invented certification is introduced.
