## 4. Stable technical fact and source contract

| ID | Permitted visible predicate | Source boundary |

The reported LLDPE system illustrates the two-stage distinction; no carrier, loading or formulation prescription transfers. |
| `MB-G2-C02` | ISO 23900-5 FPV depends on equipment, conditions, test polymer and consistent concentrate preparation. Raw line-pressure rise is not automatically a reported ISO FPV. | ISO 23900-5:2015, edition 1, May 2015, confirmed 2025. No procedure, lab selection, universal protocol or pass threshold. |
| `MB-G2-C03` | Flow, FPV, microscopy/distribution and final-specimen tests answer distinct process, spatial and functional questions. No mechanism or single verdict. |
| `MB-G2-C04` | Favourable concentrate evidence may support a final-specimen trial but cannot close an unmeasured final-use requirement; the cited ageing observation is not outdoor service-life evidence. | Cabello-Alvarado et al. 2020 and approved article. No cross-polymer, service-life or Grade inference. |
| `MB-G2-C05` | TDS, SDS, lot CoA and application reports answer distinct screening, safety, lot and tested-system questions. | Approved article; current Chemours Ti-Pure R-350 and KRONOS 2220 manufacturer pages are examples of product positioning, document links and per-field value status only.
