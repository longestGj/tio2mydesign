## 4. Buyer task synthesis

The useful reader task is not “show me one replacement.” It is:

> Help me turn a Chemours/Ti-Pure reference into a complete evaluation brief, so I can identify plausible IKHLAS candidates from approved product facts and then qualify them in my own system.

The page should help the reader prepare:

1. exact current Ti-Pure grade and application;
2. formulation/system context and processing constraints;
3. required appearance, optical, durability, dispersion/handling or other acceptance criteria actually used by the buyer;
4. current test methods, target values and pass/fail criteria;
5. required technical, safety, quality and origin documentation;
6. a plan for formulation trials and qualification before a supplier change.

These are evaluation inputs, not proof that a candidate will pass.

## 5. Page viability and recommendation

### Recommendation: `PROCEED_AS_BOUNDED_INDEPENDENT_GUIDE / PENDING_USER_DECISION`

The page can add unique value if it explains why a brand-level query must be converted into grade-specific evaluation inputs and directs the buyer into the site's Product and Document workflows. This differs from:

- Product Hub: owns our portfolio and approved application/process relationships;
- individual Product pages: own our grade facts;
- RES-R706: owns the exact R-706 alternative/equivalent query;
- Request Documents: collects the buyer's selected IKHLAS grade and document need;
- general process guide: explains chloride/sulfate rather than competitor-reference evaluation.

The page becomes thin or unsafe if it is implemented as a list of competitor brands, a generic paragraph about alternatives, or an unverified comparison table. Therefore independent existence is supported only under the bounded direction in the Brief.

## 7. Important findings, counterevidence and limits

1. Chemours itself provides multiple Ti-Pure grades for different applications. This contradicts a single brand-wide replacement answer.
2. Chemours' own TS-6706 “drop-in” statement is narrowly tied to R-706 and a named internal test programme. It is not transferable evidence for us.
3. Third-party “1:1” or price-saving claims are common enough to reveal risky buyer language, but are not accepted as facts.
4. A non-TiO₂ material replacement interpretation exists and must be excluded to avoid irrelevant traffic and unsafe scope expansion.
5. Without quantitative metrics, priority remains P3 and must not be inflated.
6. Without approved comparison packages, the page must not name an IKHLAS model as the alternative to any Chemours grade.
