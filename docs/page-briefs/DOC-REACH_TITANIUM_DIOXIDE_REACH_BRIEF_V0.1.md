## 4. Buyer and page strategy

The page helps a buyer frame the correct REACH question before relying on a document or supplier statement. It serves technical evaluation and supplier qualification, not legal determination. Success means the buyer knows which entity/role, product or substance, market/use and evidence date to identify in a qualified review.

The page does not own EU supplier intent, country purchasing content, trade-remedy analysis, grade performance, company registration claims, legal advice or the request form.
