## 1. Executive conclusion

The UK page is admissible for Gate 1 and has a distinct procurement purpose. Search evidence supports commercial supplier intent, but many current results satisfy that intent with UK-presence, stock, pack-size, price and delivery claims that TiO2 Malaysia has not established. The recommended differentiation is therefore decision support rather than imitation: separate Great Britain from Northern Ireland, collect application/specification and document-review inputs, keep origin/classification/trade checks source-bound, and route an editable UK-context RFQ.

Gate 1 recommendation: approve the Page Intent Card with zero named UK-specific grade recommendations and retain all capability, route and time-sensitive trade holds. Gate 2 is not authorized by this draft.

## 8. Time-sensitive trade control

### 8.1 Safe Gate 1 direction

The page may eventually tell buyers to verify the current UK tariff classification and trade-remedy position using official sources. A short dated status sentence is optional, not mandatory.

### 8.2 Gate 2 decision required

Choose exactly one approach after a fresh official-source check:

1. One short, date-stamped factual summary plus a route to `RES-TRADE-UK`; or
2. A neutral “verify the current UK trade position” block with no case-status summary.

Recommendation: use option 2 unless `RES-TRADE-UK` is approved, freshly maintained and route-ready at the same release checkpoint.

### 8.3 Prohibited directions

- No “avoid anti-dumping duty”, “non-China exemption”, “tariff-free”, “lower duty”, or comparable sales proposition.
- No inference that Malaysia branding proves origin or removes the goods from any measure.
- No publication of a rate, classification, scope outcome or investigation result without current official evidence and approval.
- No competitor or media source as the authority for current trade status.

### 8.4 Freshness contract

- Check TRA Public File and current GOV.UK notice at Gate 2 approval.
- Check again before Gate 5 approval if trade text is visible.
- Check again at Gate 9 production-equivalent QA and Gate 10 release authorization.
- If the source changes or cannot be verified, omit the dated trade statement and retain only the official verification path.
