# R01 Technical Evidence Feasibility V0.1

## Decision

The topic has enough authoritative and first-party technical evidence to support a substantial article, provided D22 writes a qualification guide rather than a recipe or product-ranking page.

## Evidence lanes

| Lane | Available source type | What it can support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| PVC profile/application structure | Chemours profile and plastics application pages | Natural commercial scope; profile, colour, exposure, chalking/non-chalking and stabilizer questions | Competitor product recommendations cannot become TiO2 Malaysia recommendations |
| PVC pipe qualification | Plastic Pipe Institute TR-02 | Substitution is conditional and tied to defined compound, amount and qualification conditions | Do not generalize its conditions to every pipe standard, country or Grade |
| Pipe weathering | PIPA TN006 | A technical-association example of PVC weathering, TiO2 protection and exposure-study reasoning | Its formulation values are source-specific; they are not a universal recipe |
| uPVC durability | Polymer Degradation and Stability article | Finished uPVC pipe colour, gloss and mechanical-property retention as legitimate endpoints | Historic study conditions and results stay bounded to the study |
| Rigid-PVC weathering | Journal article indexed by EPA HERO | UV shielding, visible-light scattering, photocatalysis, chalking, climate, stabilizer and loading as interacting variables | The abstract does not authorize service-life or product claims |
| PVC siding | Polymer Degradation and Stability article | Finished-product accelerated-weathering chemistry as an evidence lane | Accelerated hours cannot be converted into outdoor years |
| Profile formulation context | American Institute of Chemists technical article | Heat build-up, pigment surface treatment, colour and chalking as practical questions | Practitioner guidance is not a universal standard |

## Feasible article claims

- PVC/uPVC qualification depends on the defined finished system, not a generic “plastics grade” label.
- Rigid and flexible PVC, white and coloured products, and interior and exterior exposure should not be treated as one undifferentiated condition.
- Weathering evidence should identify specimen, formulation context, exposure method, endpoints and limits.
- Surface treatment, stabilizer context, dispersion, colour/gloss and mechanical-property retention are legitimate comparison variables.
- A candidate replacement must be evaluated under the actual PVC qualification and any applicable owner-controlled pipe/document requirements.

## Claims that remain unavailable or prohibited

- TiO2 Malaysia dosage recommendations or PVC recipes.
- A universal best Grade, PVC compatibility statement or direct replacement.
- Named competitor equivalence.
- Guaranteed lifetime, warranty, savings, heat reduction or weathering outcome.
- Certification, potable-water, pipe-standard or regulatory compliance unless the exact Grade and current document are verified by the proper owner.
- Conversion of accelerated-weathering hours to outdoor years.
- A claim that any current neutral Plastics Grade is specifically suitable for PVC.

## Product-evidence boundary

Current project authority permits eight Grades to appear only as neutral APP-PLAS Grades to Review. It does not establish resin-specific suitability. R01 therefore does not choose or rank any TiO2 Malaysia Grade. Historic PRD descriptions of M-200 or M-108 are research leads, not sufficient public performance proof.

## Feasibility result

PASS_WITH_CLAIM_CONTROLS.
