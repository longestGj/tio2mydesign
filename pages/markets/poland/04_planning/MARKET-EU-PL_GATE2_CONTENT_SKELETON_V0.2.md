## 1. Proposed buyer path

The page follows five distinct tasks:

**understand the product and origin → distinguish TiO2 raw material from finished white masterbatch → describe the production context → request documents when a grade is known → request a quote with the information currently available**

A buyer who is ready to enquire may use the Hero quotation action immediately. The path does not require every module to be read or a grade to be selected before an RFQ can begin.

## 2. Proposed page content in order

### PL-01 · Hero

**H1**

> Titanium Dioxide Supplier for Poland

**Hero copy**

> Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide raw material for industrial procurement in Poland. Review grades for coatings, plastics and masterbatch production, or send us your application, estimated quantity and delivery requirements for a quotation.

**Actions**

- `Request a Quote` → `/request-a-quote/`

This first screen answers what is supplied, where it originates, which production contexts the page supports, and the two approved next steps. It makes Poland the procurement/delivery context without implying Polish manufacture, a local warehouse or local stock.

### PL-02 · Confirm the Material You Need

**Candidate copy**

> The products presented by TiO2 Malaysia are industrial titanium dioxide raw materials. A finished white masterbatch combines TiO2 with a polymer carrier. If you produce masterbatch, evaluate the TiO2 grade as an input to your own formulation and process.

This short answer resolves the Poland Gate 1 product-form question. It does not become a masterbatch comparison article, add a finished masterbatch SKU, or claim that all buyers have this misunderstanding.

### PL-03 · Prepare Your Product Review

**Introductory copy**

> You do not need to know the exact grade before you start. Begin with the application information your team already knows, then compare the relevant grades on the product pages.

**Coatings**

**Plastics and masterbatch**

> Compare grade information against the resin, processing route and finished product, such as film, bags, pipes or fittings. Evaluate direct use in a plastic product separately from TiO2 used as an input for masterbatch production.

This module turns the Poland research into information a buyer can use to describe a project. It provides evaluation context without recommending a country-specific grade or asking for a confidential formulation.

## 3. Module decisions

| Decision | Reason and effect |
|---|---|
| Keep the material-identity answer separate from the application review | Raw TiO2 versus finished white masterbatch is a Poland Gate 1 problem. Combining it into a long Hero would hide the distinction; expanding it into a comparison article would distract from procurement. |
| Use one application-review module with two practical paths | Coatings and plastics/masterbatch require different project information, but both lead to product evaluation. Separate full modules would repeat the same action. |
| Keep document choice and RFQ as separate tasks | A document request requires a known grade under the current receiver contract; the RFQ supports an unknown grade. Combining them would create the wrong action expectation. |
| Do not add a Poland market overview, logistics claim, trade summary, FAQ or local-presence module | Gate 1 did not establish market-share, warehouse, inventory, delivery-time or current trade claims. The five buyer questions are answered directly in the proposed modules. |
| Link to the EU owner without expanding EU policy | Buyers can reach the regional context while Poland retains the country supplier/procurement intent. |
