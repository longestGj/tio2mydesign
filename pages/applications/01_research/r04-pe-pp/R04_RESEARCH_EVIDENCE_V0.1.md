## 2. Keyword and SERP finding

The current keyword master assigns `titanium dioxide for pe` and `titanium dioxide for pp` as secondary terms under APP-PLAS. Neither term has current quantitative metrics.

Separate original-query samples are fragmented. PE mixes weathering, rotomoulding, generic suppliers, film photocatalysis and masterbatch. PP is dominated by ageing, photocatalytic/nanocomposite research and masterbatch. The polyolefin query is more commercially recognisable but mostly returns generic grade/portfolio pages rather than a distinct finished-plastic decision.

Therefore:

- two standalone resin articles are not supported;
- a generic polyolefin guide would duplicate APP-PLAS and attract masterbatch/nano noise;
- one combined article can be justified only if its centre is cross-resin evidence transfer, not an encyclopaedia of PE and PP uses.

## 3. Technical evidence feasibility

ISO 23900 supplies a useful asymmetry. Part 4 addresses colouristic properties and ease of dispersion of white pigments in polyethylene by two-roll milling; Parts 5 and 6 address filter-pressure and film-test routes under their stated preparation and method contexts. A result obtained under the PE-specific route does not automatically establish performance in PP or in a converted final article.

The 1981 *European Polymer Journal* comparison of PE and PP with added TiO2 reports, within its historical systems, that analytical methods did not behave identically across the two polymers: infrared measures were described as suitable for PE but not PP, while tensile-property change was the more sensitive degradation measure in that study. This supports method-and-polymer specificity, not a modern formulation rule or outdoor-life claim.

Current PubMed-indexed PP extrusion research shows that thermal and mechanical stress during twin-screw processing can affect polymer melt and product properties, including work with PP compounds containing TiO2. It supports declaring processing history, machine scale and compound context; it does not isolate or rank TiO2 candidates.

The broader literature includes rotomoulded PE, PP injection mouldings, PE/PP films, recycled blends, fibres, wood-plastic composites, nanoparticles and photocatalytic degradation. These provide bounded question leads but demonstrate why the final article and conversion route must be named before evidence is transferred.

## 4. Content difference from APP-PLAS and R02

The approved Plastics parent already tells buyers to declare the resin, TiO2 introduction route, conversion process, specimen, exposure and endpoints. R04 adds one narrower decision: when a team uses PE and PP, what does a result actually prove across those two material families, and which evidence must be regenerated in each declared system?

R02 owns plastic film, including the thickness–opacity–dispersion relationship in finished film. R04 may use film only as a routing example and must not rebuild R02.

R07 and the Masterbatch pillar own concentrate formulation, carrier choice, high-loading processing and masterbatch qualification. R04 may record masterbatch identity and let-down history as inputs to a finished-plastic comparison but cannot teach carrier compatibility or concentrate manufacture.

## 5. Evidence and claim limitations

- PE and PP each contain multiple grades, morphologies, additive packages, recycled/blended conditions and conversion routes; the family name is not a controlled formulation.
- No source proves that one TiO2 candidate, masterbatch carrier or test method transfers across PE and PP.
- No current TiO2 Malaysia evidence proves PE- or PP-specific Grade suitability.
- Nanoparticle, photocatalytic and environmental-litter studies cannot be used as pigmentary Grade claims.
- No universal dosage, processing window, outdoor-life conversion, recycling outcome, food-contact status or cost saving is supported.

## 6. Research conclusion

Technical evidence is sufficient for one bounded APP-PLAS child article explaining cross-resin and cross-process evidence transfer. It is insufficient and search intent is too fragmented for two separate PE and PP articles.
