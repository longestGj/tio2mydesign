## Exclusions and counterexamples

- Search results repeatedly introduced `chloride vs sulfate` comparisons, superiority, cost, environmental and performance claims. These are excluded from PRODUCT-PROC-CL and remain owned by RES-PROC.
- Several supplier and directory pages present individual grades. Their technical and application claims belong to those suppliers and cannot be transferred to IKHLAS.
- Academic, patent, nanoparticle and titanium-metal results are not the intended industrial-pigment selection task.
