## 3. Responsibility and action logic

- APP-PLAS owns evaluation in the final plastic system. It records whether TiO2 enters as powder, compound or masterbatch only as application context.
- APP-MB owns high-concentration masterbatch production and qualification. APP-PLAS links to that owner without reproducing its detailed formulation work.
- Neutral Grade review precedes Documents, Sample and RFQ actions. Grade pages own exact model facts.
- Unknown Grade, several candidates, estimated quantity and incomplete technical conditions all have explicit continuation paths.
- Documents requires one known Grade per request; Sample supports known or unknown Grade; RFQ supports a known lead Grade or `Not sure / Need help`.

## 4. Content controls

- Preserve the approved article's system-specific technical meaning and all 14 source roles.
- Do not render private statement markers, internal workflow labels, pass/fail SOP, Grade recommendation, ranking, equivalence, fixed performance, availability, price, supply, sample, quote, lead-time or delivery claims.
- Use resin examples only to identify questions. Do not infer compatibility, drying, process settings or Grade suitability.
- Schema candidates: `WebPage` and `BreadcrumbList` only.
