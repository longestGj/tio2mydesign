## 2. Traceable search and page observations

Both exact queries surface the current MDIC definitive-measure page, but they also surface the superseded provisional page and non-official commentary. Expanded official discovery was necessary to reconstruct the controlling sequence:

1. original investigation opened in 2024;
2. provisional measure applied in 2024 and later ended;
3. Resolution GECEX No. 802/2025 imposed a definitive specific duty;
4. Resolution GECEX No. 850/2026 later removed Siegwerk from the named producer/exporter list;
5. a public-interest evaluation opened on 27 March 2026 and remained without a located final decision in the official sources checked through 6 September 2026.

The official-source pattern supports a reader who needs the current measure, written product/origin scope, applicable rate structure, later amendments and a practical verification path. It does not support treating this page as a generic Brazil supplier landing page.

## 3. Intent interpretations, alternatives and counterexamples

### Selected interpretation

Current-status informational intent with an import/compliance task: explain Brazil's definitive anti-dumping measure for specified China-origin rutile TiO2, distinguish it from the ordinary import tariff and from the active public-interest evaluation, identify the rate structure and exclusions, and tell the reader which official facts to verify before using a landed-cost assumption.

### Alternative intents retained

- `titanium dioxide import duty brazil`: answer by separating ordinary import duty from the additional anti-dumping measure; do not combine them into a transaction total.
- `direito antidumping dióxido de titânio brasil`: preserve Portuguese legal names and official links while the registered Resource page remains English.
- commercial Brazil procurement: route to `MARKET-BR-EN` or `MARKET-BR-PT`, not duplicate their supplier role.

### Counterexamples rejected

- Presenting the old provisional measure as current.
- Copying the stale MDIC named-company list without applying Resolution No. 850/2026.
- Concluding that the public-interest evaluation suspended or changed the duty because final submissions closed.
- Treating NCM 3206.11.10 as sufficient by itself to decide whether a product is covered.
- Turning `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide` into an unverified shipment-specific customs or anti-dumping conclusion.
- Publishing an ordinary tariff percentage as if it were the anti-dumping amount, or vice versa.
