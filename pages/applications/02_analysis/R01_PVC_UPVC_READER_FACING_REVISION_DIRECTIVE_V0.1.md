## 2. Revision objective

Convert the current technically valid internal-release candidate into an external expert article by performing **reader-facing governance stripping**. Preserve the technical argument and buyer decision value while removing or translating internal workflow language.

The article should primarily explain **what technical questions a PVC buyer must resolve and why**. It should not expose a detailed internal control system for who owns, routes, gates or hands off each question.

## 3. Must preserve

- PVC-U/PVC-P distinction and the warning against uncontrolled evidence transfer.
- Product-branch distinction across profiles, windows/doors, siding, sheet and pipe.
- TiO2 weathering relationship: UV screening/protection versus residual surface photoactivity, in formulation, stabilizer, temperature, moisture and exposure context.
- Separate appearance, surface and retained-property endpoints.
- Pipe ingredient/programme route versus certification, regulatory/water-contact and finished-product approval distinction.
- Matched candidate-comparison logic.
- Exposure and retained-property reasoning.
- Conservative claim boundaries: no named TiO2 Malaysia grade recommendation, recipe, dosage, equivalence, guarantee, certification or universal threshold.

## 4. Must reduce, hide or translate

Unless indispensable to reader meaning, remove or rewrite repeated expressions such as:

- `hold for authority`;
- `route to the qualified/responsible specialist`;
- `decision owner` and `missing-evidence owner`;
- `classified evidence package` and `permitted evidence`;
- `complete, owned basis`;
- repeated `advance / hold / reject` state-machine language;
- internal gate, routing, ownership, handoff and package-completeness mechanics.

Section 5 should explain document roles in direct reader language: standards define scope or test methods, supplier documents state candidate identity/claims, and application decisions still require matched system evidence. It should not reproduce an evidence-classification system.

Sections 6–8 should be consolidated or simplified where useful. A concise, practical buyer checklist may remain, but the article must not read as a qualification request-package specification.
