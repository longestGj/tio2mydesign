## 6. Evidence-backed Fact Set

The detailed claim inventory is in `RES-TRADE-EU_GATE1_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_V0.1.csv`.

- Regulation (EU) 2025/4 was published 9 January 2025 and entered into force 10 January 2025.
- It imposes a definitive fixed anti-dumping duty on the defined TiO₂ product originating in the People's Republic of China.
- The stated fixed amounts are EUR 0.74/kg for LB Group, EUR 0.25/kg for Anhui Gold Star Group, EUR 0.64/kg for other cooperating Annex companies and EUR 0.74/kg for all other companies.
- Individual company rates require the specified valid commercial invoice; the all-other-company rate applies when that condition is not met.
- The white-graphic-ink production exemption is conditional on the Union Customs Code end-use procedure.
- Notice C/2026/4533, published 25 August 2026, reopened an absorption reinvestigation and states the 2025/4 measures are in force.
- The notice concerns a possible future recalculation; it is not a current replacement rate.

No ordinary customs tariff or destination-specific VAT rate is in the proposed Gate 1 fact set.
