## 2. Reader job

A Brazil buyer, importer, customs, compliance or finance team needs a dated answer to four questions: what definitive titanium dioxide measure Brazil currently lists; which written product, China-origin and producer/exporter conditions control the four USD/t bands; what Resolution GECEX 850/2026 changed; and what the still-open public-interest record means before a shipment-specific assessment.

## 4. Scan hierarchy

- The first screen carries the dated definitive-measure answer, written China-origin boundary, listed end date, primary procurement CTA and official-measure secondary link.
- Module 2 separates historical investigation/provisional events from the current definitive measure and later public-interest procedure.
- Module 3 presents the general rutile scope, indicative NCM reference, anatase boundary and narrow Ex-001 definition as distinct checks.
- Module 4 uses four amount cards or rows. Every row repeats China origin and identifies the exact producer/exporter condition; Resolution 850 is placed beside the USD 1,223.92 list condition.
- Module 5 states that a public-interest evaluation and a submissions deadline do not themselves suspend or alter the measure.
- Module 6 separates anti-dumping, ordinary import duty and other import taxes without numeric transaction output.
- Module 7 supplies the shipment-input checklist and refuses to determine a band, exclusion or customs result.
- Module 8 states receiver expectations and finishes with official links and the visible review date.

## 5. Boundary rules

- The page reports the current official record and bounded 7 September 2026 recheck; it does not determine legal, customs, classification, origin, producer/exporter, exclusion, duty, tax or clearance outcomes.
- Resolution 802 controls the definitive measure and Resolution 850 removes Siegwerk from the named list. The stale MDIC detail-page list must not be treated as complete without the later amendment.
- Exact legal-entity identity and the current official list matter. A group name, brand, supplier or related-company claim is not enough to assign a band.
- NCM `3206.11.10` is indicative for the measure. The written product description and exclusions remain necessary.
- Malaysia-origin supply does not create an automatic non-application, preference, exemption or zero-duty result.
- The page gives no avoidance, relabelling, routing, transshipment or circumvention advice.
