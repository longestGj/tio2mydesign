## 4. Buyer and page strategy

The page helps buyers frame an origin-document review without inferring product or shipment origin. It serves supplier qualification and pre-shipment/import documentation planning. Success means the buyer can identify the product, order/shipment, destination and requested certificate/evidence context and understands that document review does not determine customs treatment.

The page does not own company manufacturing claims, country supplier terms, trade-remedy analysis, origin determination, customs advice, grade specifications or request-form states.
