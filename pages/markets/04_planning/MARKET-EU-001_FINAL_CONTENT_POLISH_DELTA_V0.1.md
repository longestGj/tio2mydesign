## 4. FAQ direct-answer-first delta

The following answers are the exact revised values:

### Can TiO2 Malaysia supply titanium dioxide to EU buyers?

> Yes. TiO2 Malaysia supplies Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide for evaluation by EU industrial buyers. Buyers can review application paths and representative grades, request documents, and submit sample or quotation enquiries. TiO2 Malaysia is operated by IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. in Malaysia and is not presented as an EU-established supplier.

### Is TiO2 Malaysia an EU-based supplier?

> No. TiO2 Malaysia is operated from Malaysia and is not presented as an EU-established supplier. The platform supports industrial buyers, including procurement teams in the European Union.

### Who handles REACH and CLP responsibilities for an EU import?

> The applicable responsibilities generally depend on the EU importing role and the structure of the transaction. They generally sit with the EU importer or, for applicable REACH obligations, an EU-established only representative. Confirm the substance, annual volume and transaction circumstances.

### What documents can EU buyers request?

> EU buyers can request product, safety, quality, COA-related, origin and supplier-qualification documentation. Availability and applicable scope are confirmed after the selected grade and request purpose are submitted.

### How should we select a grade?

> Start with the intended application, formulation, process and evaluation criteria. Then compare the relevant product information and identify the grade in your request when known.

The remaining three answers already start directly and remain unchanged. The FAQ remains eight questions and does not become longer as a module.
